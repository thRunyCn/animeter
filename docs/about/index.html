About Animeter

Animeter is a fan-made guide to anime. It ranks about a thousand series and answers the questions people ask before and after watching: is it worth pushing through, which episodes are filler, what order to watch in, and which chapter of the manga or light novel to read once the anime ends.

Where the data comes from

Scores, popularity, genres, tags, covers and season lists come from AniList. Filler and arc data and the manga hand-off points are researched per series, and each series page links its sources. Drop points and Animeter ratings come from visitors who sign in and report.

Corrections and contact

Spotted a wrong chapter or a missing series? Open an issue on GitHub.
