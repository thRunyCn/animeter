Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Introduction and House of Evolution Ep 1–3

  1. Saitama one-shots Vaccine Man and other monsters with no effort.
  2. Genos asks to become Saitama's disciple after Saitama saves him.
  3. Saitama dismantles the House of Evolution and its experiments.

Hero Association and the Meteor Ep 4–7

  1. Speed-o'-Sound Sonic declares himself Saitama's rival.
  2. Saitama aces the physical test but barely passes the written exam, starting at Class C.
  3. Saitama destroys a meteor heading for City Z, but gets blamed for the debris.

Deep Sea King Ep 8–10

  1. Mumen Rider faces the Deep Sea King despite having no chance.
  2. Saitama finishes the Deep Sea King with one punch and then lets the crowd blame him.

Dark Matter Thieves Ep 11–12

  1. S-Class heroes fight off the invaders.
  2. Saitama finally has a real fight against Boros and wins with a Serious Punch.

Hero Hunt Ep 13–16

  1. Garou leaves Silver Fang's dojo and beats several heroes.
  2. Monsters claim a new Monster Association is forming.
  3. Metal Bat stands up to Garou while protecting his sister.

Super Fight Ep 17–18

  1. Saitama fights as 'Charanko' and keeps winning without trying.
  2. Monsters crash the tournament.

Monster Association Uprising Ep 19–24

  1. The S-Class heroes hold an emergency meeting.
  2. Garou keeps beating stronger heroes and barely survives Bang and Bomb.
  3. The Monster Association kidnaps Waganma, the son of a Hero Association executive, setting up a rescue mission.

Monster Association Raid Ep 25–36

  1. S-Class heroes split up in the monsters' labyrinthine base.
  2. Saitama wanders the base and keeps beating dragon-level monsters by accident.
  3. Tatsumaki and other S-Class heroes battle the Association's Dragon-level executives.
