Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Agent of the Shinigami Ep 1–20

  1. Rukia Kuchiki transfers her powers to Ichigo to save his family from a Hollow.
  2. Ichigo meets Quincy rival Uryū Ishida and the shopkeeper Kisuke Urahara.
  3. Byakuya Kuchiki and Renji Abarai arrive, beat Ichigo, and take Rukia back to Soul Society for execution.

Soul Society: The Sneak Entry Ep 21–41

  1. Ichigo regains his Soul Reaper powers through Urahara's harsh training.
  2. The group is fired into the Seireitei by Kūkaku Shiba's cannon.
  3. Ichigo defeats Squad 11 captain Kenpachi Zaraki in a brutal duel.

Soul Society: The Rescue Ep 42–63

  1. Ichigo achieves Bankai and defeats Byakuya at the execution site.
  2. Captain Sōsuke Aizen is revealed to have faked his death and orchestrated everything.
  3. Aizen, Gin Ichimaru and Kaname Tōsen defect to Hueco Mundo with the Hōgyoku.

The Bount Ep 64–109

  1. Ichigo meets the Bount leader Jin Kariya and his followers.
  2. The Bount break into Soul Society to take revenge on the Soul Reapers.
  3. Ichigo defeats Kariya in their final battle.

Arrancar: The Arrival Ep 110–131

  1. Espada Grimmjow and Ulquiorra lead early raids on Karakura Town.
  2. Ichigo trains with the Visored to control his inner Hollow.
  3. Orihime Inoue is taken to Hueco Mundo by Ulquiorra.

Arrancar: Hueco Mundo Sneak Entry Ep 132–151

  1. The group befriends former Espada Nel Tu.
  2. Ichigo defeats Privaron Espada Dordoni.
  3. Rukia and Chad face Espada Aaroniero and Nnoitra's subordinates.

Arrancar: The Fierce Fight Ep 152–167

  1. Ichigo defeats Grimmjow after a drawn-out duel.
  2. Renji and Uryū beat Szayelaporro with Mayuri Kurotsuchi's help.
  3. Captains arrive in Hueco Mundo as Aizen moves on Karakura Town.

The New Captain Shūsuke Amagai Ep 168–189

  1. Shūsuke Amagai becomes captain of Squad 3.
  2. Ichigo protects the young Kasumiōji clan heiress.
  3. Amagai's revenge plot against Central 46 is exposed.

Arrancar vs. Shinigami Ep 190–205

  1. Ichigo transforms into a full Hollow after being killed by Ulquiorra.
  2. Kenpachi and Byakuya defeat Nnoitra and Zommari.
  3. Ulquiorra disintegrates after his loss.

Turn Back the Pendulum Ep 206–212

  1. Urahara becomes captain of Squad 12 and founds the research institute.
  2. Aizen's experiments Hollowfy Shinji Hirako and other officers.
  3. Urahara and Tessai are exiled to the human world.

Decisive Battle of Karakura Ep 213–229

  1. Captain Commander Yamamoto traps Aizen's leaders in a ring of fire.
  2. Captains battle Barragan, Starrk and Halibel.
  3. The Visored join the fight against Aizen.

Zanpakutō Unknown Tales Ep 230–265

  1. Muramasa frees the Zanpakutō spirits from their wielders' control.
  2. Captains have to defeat their own swords.
  3. Muramasa's master Kōga Kuchiki is revealed and beaten.

Arrancar: Downfall Ep 266–316

  1. Ichigo learns the Final Getsuga Tenshō from Zangetsu.
  2. Gin betrays Aizen and is killed.
  3. Urahara's kidō seals Aizen as Ichigo loses his powers.

Gotei 13 Invading Army Ep 317–342

  1. Reigai copies of the captains appear and attack.
  2. Scientist Kagerōza Inaba is behind the copies.
  3. Ichigo and Rukia stop Inaba's plan.

The Lost Agent Ep 343–366

  1. Ichigo develops his Fullbring under Kūgo Ginjō.
  2. Ginjō is revealed to be the first Substitute Shinigami and steals Ichigo's power.
  3. The captains restore Ichigo's Soul Reaper powers and Ichigo defeats Ginjō.

Thousand-Year Blood War: The Blood Warfare Ep 367–379

  1. The Sternritter steal Bankai and devastate the Seireitei.
  2. Captain Commander Yamamoto is killed by Yhwach.
  3. Ichigo has his Bankai broken by Yhwach.

Thousand-Year Blood War: The Separation Ep 380–392

  1. Ichigo learns about his Quincy mother and reforges his Zanpakutō.
  2. The Wandenreich replaces the Seireitei with its own city.
  3. Uryū joins Yhwach's side as his successor.

Thousand-Year Blood War: The Conflict Ep 393–406

  1. Kenpachi unlocks his Bankai and the Sternritter fall one by one.
  2. Yhwach kills the Soul King and absorbs his power.
  3. Aizen is freed to help fight Yhwach.

Thousand-Year Blood War: The Calamity Ep 407–414

  1. Ichigo, Renji and the others confront Yhwach in the palace.
  2. Uryū moves against Yhwach from within.
  3. The anime departs further from the manga from episode 411.
