Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

The Man Who Became a Kaiju Ep 1–5

  1. A small kaiju enters Kafka's body and he becomes Kaiju No. 8.
  2. Kafka hides his transformation and saves his coworker Reno Ichikawa.
  3. With Reno, Kafka passes the Defense Force entrance exam as a candidate on probation.

Sagamihara Subjugation Operation Ep 6–8

  1. The rookies fight swarms of yojū in Sagamihara.
  2. Kafka secretly transforms to defeat a powerful honjū and save Kikoru Shinomiya.
  3. The intelligent Kaiju No. 9 appears and gathers data on the battle.

Raid on Tachikawa Base Ep 9–10

  1. Vice-Captain Soshiro Hoshina fights a humanoid kaiju.
  2. Kafka transforms in front of everyone to protect Mina, revealing his secret.

Kaiju No. 8 Captured Ep 11–12

  1. Director General Isao Shinomiya tests Kafka in a fight to the death.
  2. Kafka regains control of his kaiju form and is spared to serve as a weapon for the Force.

Kaiju Weapon Ep 13–17

  1. Kafka is assigned to Narumi's First Division.
  2. Isao Shinomiya battles Kaiju No. 9 and is killed and absorbed.
  3. Kikoru and the others push to grow stronger after the loss.

The Compatible User Ep 18–21

  1. Reno shows high compatibility with the Kaiju No. 6 weapon.
  2. Kafka struggles against the kaiju instincts in his body.
  3. Signs of a large-scale kaiju attack begin to appear.

Cataclysms Ep 22

  1. Numbered kaiju strike several cities at once.
  2. Divisions are spread thin across the country to respond.

The Second Wave Ep 23

  1. The Defense Force braces for the second wave of attacks.
  2. Season 2 ends with Kafka and his allies heading into the next battles.
