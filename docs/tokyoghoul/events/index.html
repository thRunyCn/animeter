Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Introduction and Nishiki Arc Ep 1–3

  1. Rize Kamishiro attacks Kaneki, and a falling steel beam ends the encounter.
  2. Kaneki finds shelter with the ghouls of the café Anteiku and Tōka Kirishima.
  3. Kaneki clashes with the ghoul Nishiki Nishio while protecting his friend Hide.

Gourmet Arc Ep 4–5

  1. Tsukiyama lures Kaneki to the ghoul restaurant to be eaten.
  2. Tsukiyama kidnaps Nishiki's girlfriend, Kimi Nishino, as bait.
  3. Kaneki, Tōka and Nishiki defeat Tsukiyama in a church.

Doves' Emergence Arc Ep 6–8

  1. Mado kills Hinami's mother, Ryōko.
  2. Tōka and Hinami take revenge and kill Mado.
  3. Kaneki fights Amon and spares him.

Aogiri Tree Arc Ep 9–12

  1. Aogiri Tree kidnaps Kaneki.
  2. The ghoul Yamori (Jason) tortures Kaneki for days.
  3. Kaneki embraces his ghoul side, his hair turns white, and he kills Yamori.

Root A: Joining Aogiri Ep 13–20

  1. Kaneki leaves Anteiku and joins Aogiri Tree.
  2. Amon and Akira Mado lead the CCG's pursuit of Aogiri.
  3. Kaneki uses his place in Aogiri to protect the people close to him.

Owl Suppression Operation Ep 21–24

  1. The CCG surrounds Anteiku and Yoshimura reveals his past.
  2. Kaneki returns to protect Anteiku.
  3. Kaneki carries a wounded Hide through the battle and faces Kishō Arima.

Torso and Nutcracker Investigations Ep 25–27

  1. Haise mentors the Quinx: Urie, Mutsuki, Shirazu and Saiko.
  2. The squad investigates the serial killer Torso and the ghoul Nutcracker.
  3. Haise visits the café :re, run by Tōka.

Auction Mopping-Up Operation Ep 28–31

  1. The CCG and Aogiri clash at the auction venue.
  2. Haise is pushed to the edge by Aogiri's twins Kurona and Nashiro.
  3. Haise faces flashbacks to his life as Kaneki.

Rose Investigation Ep 32–34

  1. The Quinx track the ghoul family behind the Rosewald group.
  2. Haise learns the Tsukiyama family is the target.

Tsukiyama Family Extermination Ep 35–36

  1. Shirazu dies fighting Nashiro.
  2. Haise regains his memories as Kaneki and saves Tsukiyama.

Rushima Landing and Cochlea Raid Ep 37–40

  1. Kaneki rescues Hinami from Cochlea.
  2. The CCG wins on Rushima but suffers major losses.
  3. Kaneki is defeated by Arima, who reveals his own secret before dying.
  4. Kaneki becomes the new One-Eyed King and forms Goat.

Clown Siege and CCG Lab Infiltration Ep 41–42

  1. The Clowns attack Kaneki's group.
  2. Goat infiltrates the CCG's laboratory and uncovers its secrets.

24th Ward Raid Ep 43–44

  1. Kaneki and Tōka grow close and marry.
  2. Kaneki is forced to confront his past self and fall into an uncontrollable kakuja.

Dragon War Ep 45–48

  1. The Dragon spreads deadly spores across Tokyo.
  2. Humans and ghouls form the Tokyo Security Committee.
  3. Kaneki is freed and defeats Furuta, ending the war.
