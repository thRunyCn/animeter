Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Meeting Nagisa Ep 1–3

  1. Tomoya meets Nagisa at the foot of the hill and starts helping her revive the drama club.
  2. Tomoya first visits the Furukawa bakery and meets Nagisa's parents, Akio and Sanae.
  3. Nagisa's poor health and Tomoya's injured shoulder are revealed.

Fuko Arc Ep 4–9

  1. Fuko turns out to be in a coma, and the girl at school is something like her spirit.
  2. The group helps convince Kouko to hold her wedding with Yusuke.
  3. Fuko gradually fades from people's memories, but her friends remember her in time for the wedding.

Kotomi Arc Ep 10–14

  1. Kotomi's disastrous violin playing and her shyness are brought out as she joins the drama club.
  2. Kotomi's parents turn out to have died in a plane crash years ago, and Tomoya recalls knowing her as a child.
  3. Her friends restore her overgrown garden, and a briefcase from her late parents helps her move on.

School Life: Sunohara, Kyou and Tomoyo Ep 15–18

  1. Nagisa can't find a faculty advisor and briefly gives up on the drama club.
  2. Tomoya, Youhei and Kyou win a three-on-three basketball game against the basketball club.
  3. Tomoya is suspended after a fight, and Tomoyo becomes student council president.

Nagisa Arc: The School Play Ep 19–22

  1. Tomoya moves in with the Furukawas to escape his father.
  2. Nagisa learns her parents gave up their dreams to care for her when she nearly died as a child.
  3. Nagisa freezes on stage, but her parents' encouragement lets her finish the play, and Tomoya confesses to her.

Summer Break (extra) Ep 23

  1. Mei invents embarrassing plans to push the shy couple together.
  2. Tomoya and Nagisa finally hold hands.

Sunohara and Mei Ep 24–27

  1. Akio's baseball team wins a local game on Tomoya's final hit.
  2. Sanae poses as Youhei's girlfriend to reassure Mei.
  3. Youhei's lost soccer career is revealed, and he fights the soccer club to protect Mei.

Misae Arc Ep 28–29

  1. A boy Misae once knew, Katsuki Shima, is revealed to have been granting her wishes.
  2. Misae's wish is fulfilled and an orb of light appears.

Yukine Arc Ep 30–31

  1. Yukine's brother Kazuto is revealed to have died, ending the gang fighting.
  2. Yukine shares the legend of the orbs of light that grant wishes.

Graduation and Working Life Ep 32–36

  1. Nagisa's illness forces her to repeat her last year of school.
  2. Tomoya becomes an electrician under Yusuke.
  3. Tomoya proposes, earns Akio's blessing, and marries Nagisa.

Nagisa's Pregnancy Ep 37–39

  1. Nagisa becomes pregnant and decides to keep the baby despite the risk.
  2. Nagisa dies shortly after giving birth to their daughter, Ushio.

Ushio Arc Ep 40–44

  1. Tomoya bonds with Ushio on a trip and learns from his grandmother how his own father sacrificed for him.
  2. Tomoya reconciles with his father, who moves back to the countryside.
  3. Ushio falls ill with the same sickness as Nagisa and dies in Tomoya's arms.

The Illusionary World Ep 45

  1. The girl in the illusionary world is revealed to be connected to Ushio.
  2. The orbs Tomoya collected let him rewrite events so that Nagisa survives childbirth.

One Year Before (extra) Ep 46

  1. Nagisa is knocked out by a prank Tomoya and Youhei set up, and a classmate befriends her.
  2. Nagisa keeps an encouraging message Tomoya wrote, which helped her make the friend.

Under the Green Tree (recap) Ep 47

  1. Tomoya tells Ushio the story of how he met Nagisa.
  2. The family has a picnic under the tree.
