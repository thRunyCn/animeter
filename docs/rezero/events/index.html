Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Arc 1: The Loot House Ep 1–3

  1. Subaru is killed at the loot house and wakes up earlier that day, discovering his Return by Death.
  2. He learns the hard way that he can't tell anyone about his ability.
  3. Subaru saves the girl, who reveals her real name is Emilia, with help from the Sword Saint Reinhard.

Arc 2: Roswaal Mansion Ep 4–11

  1. Subaru dies repeatedly in his sleep and suspects the mansion's maids, Rem and Ram.
  2. Rem kills Subaru in one loop, suspecting him of being tied to the Witch Cult.
  3. A curse from the village children turns out to be behind his deaths, and Subaru wins Rem's trust by saving the village from mabeasts.

Arc 3: The Royal Selection and Despair Ep 12–18

  1. Subaru humiliates himself at the royal selection and is beaten in a duel by Julius.
  2. Emilia leaves him behind, and he returns to find the Witch Cult massacring the village.
  3. Rem dies protecting him, Subaru breaks down, and Rem's faith in him in episode 18 pulls him back from despair.

Arc 3: The White Whale and Sloth Ep 19–25

  1. Subaru forms an alliance with Crusch Karsten and Anastasia Hoshin.
  2. Wilhelm van Astrea finally avenges his wife by slaying the White Whale.
  3. Subaru and Julius defeat Petelgeuse Romanee-Conti, the Archbishop of Sloth, and Subaru confesses his love to Emilia.

Arc 4: The Sanctuary (Part 1) Ep 26–38

  1. Rem falls into a coma and is erased from everyone's memories by Gluttony, except Subaru's.
  2. Emilia undertakes the Sanctuary's trials while the villagers are held there.
  3. Subaru meets the Witch of Greed, Echidna, and other witches in a dream-like tea party.
  4. Subaru learns that Elsa has attacked the mansion, while a giant rabbit swarm threatens the Sanctuary.

Arc 4: The Sanctuary (Part 2) Ep 39–50

  1. Otto and Garfiel become Subaru's allies after he learns to lean on others.
  2. Emilia confronts her past in the Elior Forest and completes the trials.
  3. Subaru rejects Echidna's contract and the Sanctuary's barrier is broken.
  4. Beatrice leaves the Forbidden Library and makes a contract with Subaru.

Arc 5: The Water Gate City of Priestella Ep 51–66

  1. The Witch Cult takes over the city's control towers and broadcasts its demands.
  2. Subaru and Emilia defeat Regulus Corneas, the Archbishop of Greed.
  3. Wilhelm and Reinhard are forced to face a revived Theresia van Astrea.
  4. The city is saved, but many people lose their names, memories or even their bodies to the Archbishops.

Arc 6: The Pleiades Watchtower – Loss Arc Ep 67–77

  1. The group reaches the tower and meets its guardian, Shaula, who claims Subaru is her master.
  2. A trial of strength pits Julius against the ghost of the first Sword Saint, Reid Astrea.
  3. Subaru wakes up with his memories of the other world gone and falls into suspicion and paranoia.
  4. Subaru, still amnesiac, vows to save everyone after his companions die.

Arc 6: The Pleiades Watchtower – Recapture Arc Ep 78–85

  1. Subaru confronts the Gluttony Archbishop who stole his memories and takes back his identity.
  2. Ram faces the Gluttony Archbishop Roy Alphard.
  3. Shaula's fate as the tower's guardian comes to a head in the finale.
  4. Rem finally wakes up from her long coma.
