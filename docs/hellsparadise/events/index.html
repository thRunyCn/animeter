Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Island Arc Ep 1–6

  1. Gabimaru agrees to the mission so he can return to his wife.
  2. The convicts land on Shinsenkyō and find bizarre monsters and flower-covered corpses.
  3. Convicts and executioners start dying fast, and uneasy alliances form.

Lord Tensen Arc Ep 7–13

  1. Gabimaru and Sagiri learn to sense and use Tao.
  2. The group battles the Tensen and learns how their bodies regenerate.
  3. Several groups of survivors join forces as the Tensen fight back.

Lord Tensen Arc: Reinforcements Ep 14–17

  1. A second party of Yamada Asaemon executioners and Iwagakure ninja arrives on the island.
  2. The survivors and newcomers form an alliance against the Tensen.

Hōrai Arc Ep 18–25

  1. The alliance splits up to take on the Tensen inside Hōrai.
  2. Truths about the elixir and the island's master come to light.
  3. Gabimaru fights to keep his promise to return home.
