Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Joining Night Raid Ep 1–6

  1. Tatsumi's childhood friends are tortured to death by a seemingly kind noble family.
  2. Tatsumi kills the corrupt police captain Ogre and wins Akame's respect.
  3. Akame defeats the executioner Zanku the Beheader.
  4. Seryu and her creature Koro ambush Night Raid, killing Sheele.

The Three Beasts Ep 7–8

  1. Bulat defeats Liver but succumbs to poison.
  2. Tatsumi inherits Bulat's armor Incursio and kills Nyau.

Esdeath and the Jaegers Ep 9–14

  1. Esdeath falls for Tatsumi after he wins a tournament and drags him to the palace.
  2. Tatsumi escapes and warns Night Raid about the Jaegers.
  3. Night Raid kills Dr. Stylish after he turns himself into a Danger Beast.
  4. Tatsumi and Esdeath are stranded together on an island, where she reveals her past.

Path of Peace and Kyoroch Ep 15–19

  1. Leone loses an arm fighting Kurome's puppet army, and Chelsea assassinates the Jaeger Bols.
  2. Chelsea takes Bols's form to poison Kurome but is caught and killed.
  3. Mine kills Seryu, and Bolic is killed in the attack on the palace at Kyoroch.

The Revolution Ep 20–24

  1. Lubbock dies after killing Syura, and Tatsumi is captured and sentenced to death.
  2. Mine defeats General Budo but dies after confessing her love to Tatsumi.
  3. Tatsumi stops the Emperor's giant Imperial Arm and dies in Akame's arms.
  4. Akame kills Esdeath and Leone kills the Prime Minister before dying of her wounds.
