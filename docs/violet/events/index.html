Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Becoming an Auto Memory Doll Ep 1–4

  1. Violet decides to become an Auto Memory Doll, writing letters on others' behalf.
  2. At doll training school, Violet helps her classmate Luculia send a message to her wounded brother.
  3. On a job in Iris's hometown, Violet helps her coworker make peace with her family.

Letters and Clients Ep 5–7

  1. Violet helps a princess and prince exchange honest love letters before their royal wedding.
  2. At an observatory, Violet works alongside the lonely scholar Leon.
  3. While helping a playwright write for his dead daughter, Violet learns Gilbert is presumed dead.

Gilbert's Fate Ep 8–9

  1. Violet confronts Gilbert's brother Dietfried and visits Gilbert's grave.
  2. Flashbacks show how Gilbert took Violet in and what happened in his final battle.
  3. Violet struggles with guilt over the lives she took but chooses to keep writing letters.

Letters That Last Ep 10–11

  1. Violet writes fifty years' worth of birthday letters from a dying mother to her young daughter, Anne.
  2. Violet travels to a war zone and writes a young soldier's final letters home.

The Peace Treaty and the Air Show Ep 12–13

  1. Violet fights to protect Dietfried when rebels attack the train.
  2. Violet and her friends stop a bombing of the bridge.
  3. Violet writes a letter to Gilbert for the Air Show.

Surely, Someday, You Will Understand Love Ep 14

  1. Violet writes lyrics for the opera singer Irma.
  2. The song helps Irma move past her grief.

Violet Evergarden: The Movie Film

  1. Decades later, Anne's granddaughter Daisy traces the story of the Doll who wrote her grandmother's letters.
  2. Violet learns that Gilbert has been living on a remote island under a new life.
  3. Violet and Gilbert finally reunite, and Violet stays with him.
