Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Toman Arc Ep 1–5

  1. Takemichi discovers he can time-leap by shaking hands with Hinata's brother Naoto.
  2. He meets Tokyo Manji Gang leader Mikey and vice-captain Draken.
  3. Naoto becomes a police detective in the new future and teams up with Takemichi.

Moebius Arc Ep 6–12

  1. Toman's Pah-chin stabs Moebius leader Osanai after Moebius attacks his friends.
  2. Kiyomasa stabs Draken during the Toman split on August 3rd.
  3. Takemichi saves Draken's life, but the future is still not fixed.

Valhalla Arc Ep 13–24

  1. Kazutora Hanemiya and Baji's ties to Mikey's past are revealed.
  2. Toman clashes with Valhalla at the Bloody Halloween battle.
  3. Baji dies, and Takemichi becomes captain of Toman's First Division.

Christmas Showdown Arc Ep 25–37

  1. Takemichi meets Hakkai Shiba and his older siblings Taiju and Yuzuha.
  2. Taiju Shiba's Black Dragons are confronted at a church on Christmas Eve.
  3. Mikey defeats Taiju, and Tetta Kisaki is expelled from Toman.

Tenjiku Arc Ep 38–50

  1. Emma Sano is killed by Kisaki's plot.
  2. Toman and Tenjiku fight the Kanto Incident battle in Yokohama.
  3. Izana Kurokawa and Kisaki both die as the arc ends.

War of the Three Titans Arc Ep 51–53

  1. Takemichi returns to a future where Mikey leads the criminal group Bonten.
  2. Three gangs vie for control of Tokyo's underworld.
