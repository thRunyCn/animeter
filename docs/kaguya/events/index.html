These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Season 1: The Battle of Confessions Begins Ep 1–12

  1. Kaguya and Shirogane wage daily mind games over who will confess first.
  2. Secretary Chika Fujiwara keeps wrecking their plans by accident.
  3. Treasurer Yu Ishigami joins the council's antics.
  4. The pair spend the summer fireworks festival together.

Season 2: Love Is War? Ep 13–24

  1. Miko Iino runs against Shirogane in the student council election.
  2. Ishigami's past and the reason he is shunned at school are revealed.
  3. Shirogane wins re-election and Miko joins the council.

Season 3: Ultra Romantic Ep 25–37

  1. Shirogane reveals he will be studying at Stanford next year.
  2. Ishigami grows closer to Tsubame Koyasu.
  3. At the culture festival Shirogane and Kaguya share their first kiss.

The First Kiss Never Ends Ep 38–41

  1. Kaguya struggles to act normally around Shirogane after the kiss.
  2. The two spend Christmas together and take their relationship a step further.

The Stairway to Adulthood Ep 42–43

  1. Kaguya and Shirogane navigate life as a new couple.
