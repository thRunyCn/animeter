Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Life in Kowloon Ep 1–3

  1. A photo at a teahouse suggests Reiko is missing memories.
  2. Reiko learns she resembles a woman known as Kujirai B, Kudou's former fiancee.
  3. Gwen tells Reiko that Kujirai B is dead.

The Second Kowloon Ep 4–8

  1. Kudou admits to killing Kujirai B, then claims the confession was hypothetical.
  2. Reiko finds a hidden ring, and an explosion rocks the city.
  3. Reiko learns she is a Generic, a copy of the original Reiko, who took her own life.
  4. Gwen explains that the city is a loop of time populated by digital copies.

The City Fades Ep 9–13

  1. Reiko begins recovering lost memories through a pair of glasses.
  2. Xiaohei's copy vanishes and Xiaohei and Yaomay leave the city.
  3. The city disappears as Kudou faces his regrets.
  4. Reiko leaves in physical form and reunites with Kudou in Hong Kong two years later.
