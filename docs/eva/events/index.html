Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Arrival in Tokyo-3 Ep 1–6

  1. Shinji reluctantly pilots Unit-01 against Sachiel, and the Eva berserks.
  2. Shinji runs away from NERV and comes back.
  3. Shinji and Rei defeat Ramiel together in Operation Yashima.

Asuka and the Angel Battles Ep 7–13

  1. Asuka and Unit-02 arrive and defeat Gaghiel at sea.
  2. Shinji and Asuka must sync their moves to beat the split Angel Israfel.
  3. The Angel Ireul invades NERV's computers from inside.

Cracks Beneath the Surface Ep 14–19

  1. Shinji is swallowed by Leliel and Unit-01 breaks out on its own.
  2. Toji is chosen as the Fourth Child and Unit-03 is taken over by Bardiel.
  3. Unit-01 devours Zeruel's S2 engine after going berserk.

Collapse Ep 20–24

  1. Shinji is dissolved inside Unit-01 and later recovered.
  2. Kaji is killed after uncovering the truth.
  3. Rei self-destructs and the clone tanks are revealed, and Shinji kills Kaworu, the final Angel.

Instrumentality (TV ending) Ep 25–26

  1. The episodes take place largely inside Shinji's psyche.
  2. Shinji accepts himself and is congratulated by the cast.

The End of Evangelion Film

  1. SEELE sends JSSDF troops to invade NERV headquarters.
  2. Asuka battles the Mass Production Evas and is torn apart.
  3. Third Impact merges humanity, but Shinji rejects it and wakes beside Asuka.
