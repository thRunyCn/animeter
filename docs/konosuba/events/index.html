Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Arrival in Axel and Beldia Ep 1–6

  1. Kazuma and a useless Aqua start as broke adventurers in the beginner town of Axel.
  2. They recruit Megumin, who can only cast one Explosion a day, and Darkness, a masochistic crusader.
  3. Megumin's daily explosions provoke the Devil King's general Beldia, whom the party defeats by flooding him with Aqua's water.

Winter and the Destroyer Ep 7–10

  1. Kazuma is killed by the Winter Shogun and revived by Aqua.
  2. The party visits the magic shop run by Wiz, a gentle lich living in Axel.
  3. The town stops the giant walking fortress Mobile Fortress Destroyer, but its collapse leaves Kazuma in trouble.

Kazuma's Trial and Vanir Ep 11–16

  1. Kazuma is put on trial and saddled with a huge debt.
  2. Kazuma duels Darkness to help her escape an arranged marriage.
  3. The demon Vanir possesses Darkness before Megumin blasts him, clearing Kazuma's debt.
  4. Vanir becomes Wiz's business partner, offering to sell Kazuma's modern-world inventions.

Alcanretia Ep 17–20

  1. Axis Church followers aggressively try to convert Kazuma and Darkness.
  2. Someone is poisoning the town's hot springs, and the townspeople blame Aqua.
  3. The culprit turns out to be Hans, a Devil King general, whom the party defeats before being chased out of town.

Legend of Crimson Film

  1. Kazuma meets Megumin's family in the Crimson Demon village.
  2. The Devil King's general Sylvia attacks the village.
  3. Megumin and Kazuma grow closer, and the village defeats Sylvia with a final Explosion.

The Royal Capital and Princess Iris Ep 21–26

  1. Kazuma stays at the palace as Iris's playmate and learns how lonely she is.
  2. Kazuma joins forces with the gentleman thief Chris to steal dangerous divine treasures.
  3. Iris reveals she knew all along that Kazuma was the thief.

Darkness's Wedding Ep 27–31

  1. The adventurers of Axel team up to kill a Kowloon Hydra.
  2. Darkness agrees to marry the corrupt Lord Alderp to pay off her family's debt.
  3. Kazuma and Aqua crash the wedding, Kazuma pays off the debt, and Alderp's crimes are exposed.
  4. Darkness rejoins the party.
