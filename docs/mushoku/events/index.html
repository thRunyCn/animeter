Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Childhood Ep 1–4

  1. Roxy Migurdia tutors the young Rudeus in magic, then leaves after he passes her exam.
  2. Rudeus befriends Sylphiette, a village girl bullied for her green hair.
  3. His parents send him away to the city of Roa to work as a tutor.

Roa: Tutoring Eris Ep 5–8

  1. A staged kidnapping helps Rudeus win over the unruly Eris.
  2. He trades lessons with the swordswoman Ghislaine.
  3. A huge magical disaster during his birthday celebrations teleports everyone across the world.

Demon Continent Ep 9–12

  1. The feared Superd warrior Ruijerd saves them and offers to escort them home.
  2. The three form the adventuring party Dead End.
  3. The Demon Empress Kishirika gives Rudeus a magic eye that lets him see a moment into the future.

Great Forest Ep 13–15

  1. Roxy just misses Rudeus as she searches for him.
  2. Rudeus is jailed by the beastfolk after freeing kidnapped beast children.
  3. The real smuggler is exposed, and the party waits out the rainy season in Doldia village.

Millis Ep 16–18

  1. Rudeus fights his father Paul, who has fallen into despair, until his little sister Norn stops them.
  2. Geese helps father and son reconcile, and they agree to split up to search for the rest of the family.
  3. Roxy returns to her home village and learns Rudeus passed through.

Central Continent and Shirone Ep 19–20

  1. Rudeus rescues his half-sister Aisha but is trapped by Prince Pax.
  2. Zanoba and Eris help free him, and Lilia and Aisha can finally head home.

Return to Fittoa Ep 21–23

  1. Orsted, the Dragon God, nearly kills Rudeus before Nanahoshi persuades him to stop.
  2. Ruijerd leaves, and Rudeus finds the Fittoa region in ruins.
  3. Eris leaves to train while Rudeus sleeps, leaving him heartbroken.

Guardian Fitz Ep 24

  1. Sylphie lands in the Asura Kingdom and saves Princess Ariel from an assassination attempt.
  2. She becomes Ariel's masked bodyguard under the name Fitz.

The Brokenhearted Mage Ep 25–28

  1. Rudeus joins the adventurers of Counter Arrow and rescues Sara from a blizzard.
  2. Rudeus hits his lowest point and is pulled back by Soldat.
  3. Prompted by the Man-God, he accepts an invitation to Ranoa University of Magic.

Ranoa University Ep 29–36

  1. Rudeus researches teleportation with Fitz and the masked Nanahoshi.
  2. Demon King Badigadi loses a duel to Rudeus and joins the school.
  3. Fitz reveals she is Sylphie, and she and Rudeus become a couple and get engaged.

Married Life Ep 37–42

  1. Rudeus and Sylphie buy a house and hold their wedding reception.
  2. Paul sends Norn and Aisha to live with them, and Rudeus works to win Norn's trust.
  3. Sylphie becomes pregnant just as a letter asks Rudeus to help rescue his mother.

Teleport Labyrinth Ep 43–48

  1. Rudeus reunites with Paul and rescues Roxy deep in the labyrinth.
  2. Paul dies fighting a Manatite Hydra, and Rudeus loses his left hand.
  3. Zenith is rescued with lasting memory damage.
  4. Rudeus marries Roxy as his second wife, and his daughter Lucy is born.

Eris's Training (Mad Dog) Ep 49–50

  1. Eris trains at the Sword Sanctuary, determined to become strong enough to stand against Orsted.
  2. Ghislaine accompanies Eris as she pushes herself through brutal training under the Sword God.

Home Life and the Chaos Breaker Ep 51–58

  1. Rudeus juggles home life with Sylphie and Roxy.
  2. Nanahoshi's teleportation research reaches a new stage.
  3. The group visits Perugius's flying fortress, the Chaos Breaker.

Turning Point 4 Ep 59–62

  1. An older Rudeus from a ruined future appears with his diary.
  2. The diary shows Rudeus a tragic future for his family and the Man-God's role in it.
  3. Rudeus resolves to protect his family, setting up a clash involving Orsted in Part 2 (due 2027).
