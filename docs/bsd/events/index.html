Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Joining the Agency Ep 1–2

  1. Atsushi discovers he is the white tiger, thanks to his own ability.
  2. A staged bombing serves as Atsushi's entrance test for the Agency.

Bounty on the Tiger Ep 3–5

  1. The Port Mafia's Ryunosuke Akutagawa ambushes Atsushi.
  2. Atsushi learns a mysterious buyer has put a 7-billion-yen bounty on him.
  3. Ranpo solves a murder case with Atsushi tagging along.

The Azure King Ep 6–7

  1. Kunikida investigates the Azure King's attacks.
  2. The case forces Kunikida to confront the cost of his ideals.

Kyouka and Rashomon Ep 8–10

  1. Atsushi tries to rescue Kyouka from the Mafia's control.
  2. Atsushi and Akutagawa fight aboard a Mafia ship.
  3. Atsushi defeats Akutagawa.

The Guild Arrives Ep 11–12

  1. The Guild's leader Francis Fitzgerald arrives seeking the Agency's operating license.
  2. The Guild makes clear it is also after Atsushi.

The Dark Era Ep 13–16

  1. A rogue group called Mimic begins provoking the Port Mafia.
  2. Ango is revealed to be a government spy.
  3. Oda dies fighting Mimic's leader, setting Dazai on the path to leave the Mafia.

The Guild War Ep 17–24

  1. The Guild unleashes Kyusaku Yumeno's curse ability across the city.
  2. Dazai and Chuuya reunite as the duo 'Double Black' to defeat Lovecraft.
  3. Ranpo is trapped inside Poe's mystery novel.
  4. Atsushi and Akutagawa team up to beat Fitzgerald and bring down the flying whale Moby Dick.

Walking Alone Ep 25

  1. A short side story set after the Guild war.
  2. It was bundled with the manga's 13th volume instead of being broadcast.

Fifteen Ep 26–28

  1. Dazai and Chuuya are forced into an uneasy partnership.
  2. The mystery of the god-like Arahabaki ties into Chuuya's origins.
  3. Chuuya joins the Port Mafia.

Rats in the House of the Dead Ep 29–33

  1. Kyouka's future is threatened by her past crimes.
  2. A disgraced Fitzgerald starts rebuilding his fortune.
  3. A flashback reveals Fukuzawa's past as a skilled assassin.

Cannibalism Ep 34–37

  1. An ability infects Fukuzawa and Mori, and both will die unless one kills the other.
  2. Fukuzawa and Mori come to blows before the scheme is broken.
  3. Dostoevsky is taken into custody.

Founding of the Detective Agency Ep 38–40

  1. Fukuzawa, a lone bodyguard, is hired to watch over Ranpo.
  2. Ranpo's deductive genius is first put to the test.
  3. The Agency is born.

The Decay of Angel Ep 41–50

  1. Mushitaro Oguri's 'perfect murders' make the Agency public enemies.
  2. The Hunting Dogs hunt the scattered Agency members.
  3. Dazai and Dostoevsky face off from inside the Meursault prison.
  4. Atsushi infiltrates the floating Sky Casino in search of a way to clear the Agency's name.

Fukuchi's Endgame Ep 51–61

  1. Hunting Dogs captain Gen'ichiro Fukuchi, the Decay of Angel's true leader, moves openly toward his goal of uniting the world's militaries under one command.
  2. A vampire outbreak spreads, turning people worldwide into Fukuchi's pawns.
  3. Akutagawa falls in battle with Fukuchi.
  4. Dazai and Dostoevsky's war of wits in Meursault reaches its end.
