Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Journey's End Ep 1–6

  1. Himmel dies of old age decades after the victory, and Frieren cries at his funeral.
  2. Frieren takes on Heiter's ward Fern as her apprentice.
  3. The party heads north toward Aureole, where souls are said to rest, so Frieren can speak with Himmel again.
  4. The fearful warrior Stark joins after facing down a dragon.

Aura the Guillotine Ep 7–10

  1. Frieren insists demons only imitate human speech to deceive people.
  2. Fern and Stark face Aura's subordinates Lügner and Linie.
  3. Frieren turns Aura's Scales of Obedience against her, and Aura is forced to kill herself.

Northern Lands Travels Ep 11–17

  1. The priest Sein joins the party for a while.
  2. Stark and Fern's awkward closeness grows.
  3. Sein leaves to look for his missing friend.

First-Class Mage Exam Ep 18–28

  1. Frieren and Fern work with temporary teams in the first test.
  2. In the dungeon test, examinees face perfect magical copies of themselves.
  3. The founder Serie interviews the finalists and fails Frieren on sight.
  4. Fern passes and earns the right to travel north.

Northern Plateau Travels Ep 29–33

  1. The story of the Hero of the South, who fought before Himmel, is told.
  2. The party prepares for the harsh Northern Plateau.

Divine Revolte Ep 34–36

  1. Frieren and her allies team up with knights to subdue a dangerous demon.
  2. The fight concludes in the episode titled 'A Magnificent End'.

Himmel's Memoirs Ep 37–38

  1. Memories of Himmel resurface as the party moves on.
  2. The season ends with the party on the road to the Golden Land, setting up the next arc.
