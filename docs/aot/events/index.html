Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Fall of Shiganshina Ep 1–2

  1. The Colossal Titan breaches Wall Maria at Shiganshina.
  2. Eren watches a Titan eat his mother and vows to wipe them all out.
  3. Eren, Mikasa and Armin flee as refugees.

Humanity's Comeback (Training Corps) Ep 3–4

  1. Eren struggles with the maneuver gear until he finds his equipment was broken.
  2. The cadets graduate and choose their branches of the military.

Battle of Trost District Ep 5–13

  1. Eren is swallowed by a Titan and seemingly killed.
  2. A rogue Titan fighting other Titans turns out to be Eren himself.
  3. Eren in Titan form plugs the breach with a boulder.

Eve of the Counterattack Ep 14–16

  1. Levi beats Eren in court to win custody for the Survey Corps.
  2. The cadets pick their branches, and many join the Survey Corps.

57th Exterior Scouting Mission Ep 17–22

  1. The Female Titan wipes out Levi's special squad.
  2. Levi and Mikasa rescue Eren from her mouth.

Assault on Stohess Ep 23–25

  1. Annie Leonhart is exposed as the Female Titan.
  2. Annie seals herself in crystal after losing to Eren.
  3. A Titan is revealed hidden inside the wall itself.

Clash of the Titans Ep 26–37

  1. Ymir reveals she is a Titan to save Christa.
  2. Reiner and Bertholdt confess to being the Armored and Colossal Titans and kidnap Eren.
  3. Eren uses the Coordinate to command Titans and escape.

Uprising Ep 38–49

  1. Historia is revealed to be the true royal heir.
  2. Rod Reiss explains how the Founding Titan is passed down.
  3. Historia kills her father and is crowned queen.

Return to Shiganshina Ep 50–59

  1. Erwin leads a suicide charge against the Beast Titan.
  2. Armin is burned saving the plan and is turned into the new Colossal Titan.
  3. Grisha's journals reveal the world beyond the sea.

Marley and the War for Paradis Ep 60–75

  1. Eren infiltrates Liberio and attacks during Willy Tybur's declaration of war.
  2. Sasha is killed by Gabi during the Survey Corps' retreat.
  3. Zeke and Eren's plan to meet splits Paradis's military.

War for Paradis (continued) Ep 76–87

  1. Eren and Zeke enter the Paths, where Ymir Fritz holds the key.
  2. Eren takes the Founding Titan's power and starts the Rumbling.

The Rumbling (The Final Chapters) Ep 88–94

  1. Hange sacrifices herself so the alliance can escape.
  2. Mikasa kills Eren, ending the Rumbling and the power of the Titans.
  3. Armin is left to make peace with the outside world.
