Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Hunter Exam Ep 1–26

  1. Gon, Killua, Kurapika and Leorio survive a string of lethal exam stages.
  2. Gon steals Hisoka's badge in the Zevil Island hunt and realizes Hisoka let him.
  3. Killua is manipulated by his brother Illumi into killing a candidate and is disqualified.
  4. Gon and friends travel to the Zoldyck estate to bring Killua back.

Heavens Arena Ep 27–38

  1. Wing teaches the boys Nen, the aura technique behind every Hunter's power.
  2. Gon recklessly fights a Nen user before he is ready and Wing bans him from fighting for two months.
  3. Gon faces Hisoka in the arena and lands the punch he owed him.

Yorknew City (Phantom Troupe) Ep 39–58

  1. Kurapika uses his Nen chains to kill Uvogin.
  2. Gon and Killua are captured by the Troupe.
  3. Kurapika captures Chrollo and seals his Nen, forcing the Troupe to leave.

Greed Island Ep 59–75

  1. Biscuit Krueger trains the boys hard inside the game.
  2. Gon's team beats the Bomber Genthru in a dodgeball game and a final fight.
  3. Gon clears the game and is sent to Kite, Ging's apprentice.

Chimera Ant Ep 76–136

  1. Neferpitou kills Kite, leaving Gon bent on revenge.
  2. The Hunter Association raids King Meruem's palace.
  3. Netero detonates the Poor Man's Rose on Meruem, and Meruem dies of poison beside Komugi.
  4. Gon forces himself into an adult form to kill Pitou and is left near death.

Hunter Association Election Ep 137–148

  1. Ging and the Zodiacs run a messy, scheming election.
  2. Killua brings his sister Alluka, whose power heals Gon.
  3. Gon finally meets Ging at the World Tree.
