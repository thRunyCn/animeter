Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Stone World Ep 1–5

  1. Senku and his friend Taiju make a revival fluid from nitric acid and alcohol.
  2. They revive Tsukasa Shishio, who wants a world without the old adults and turns on them.
  3. Senku is left for dead by Tsukasa but survives thanks to the petrification.

Kingdom of Science Ep 6–12

  1. Senku meets Chrome, the village's self-taught 'sorcerer' of science.
  2. The Kingdom of Science makes glass, iron and electric light.
  3. Gen Asagiri switches sides to help Senku.

Grand Bout and the Village's Origin Ep 13–17

  1. Kinro and Ginro, Chrome and a masked Kohaku compete in the Grand Bout.
  2. Senku cures Ruri's illness with sulfa drugs and becomes village chief.
  3. Senku learns that the village was founded by his father Byakuya, an astronaut.

Communications Ep 18–24

  1. Hyoga attacks the village and is pushed back.
  2. The kingdom builds a hydroelectric generator and vacuum tubes.
  3. A working telephone is completed.

Stone Wars Ep 25–35

  1. Gen imitates singer Lillian Weinberg's voice over the phone to sway Tsukasa's army.
  2. The kingdom builds a steam-powered car to storm Tsukasa's base.
  3. Hyoga betrays Tsukasa, who is put into cryosleep after being saved.

Ryusui Film

  1. Senku revives Ryusui to captain the kingdom's ship.
  2. The crew gets oil to run their ship.

Age of Exploration Ep 36–40

  1. The kingdom builds the sailing ship Perseus.
  2. Senku maps the land from the air before setting sail.

Treasure Island Ep 41–57

  1. The crew is petrified by the Petrification Weapon after landing.
  2. Kohaku and others infiltrate Ibara's palace.
  3. Senku seizes the device known as Medusa and the people of the island are freed.

Science Future: New World Ep 58–69

  1. The Perseus reaches America and Senku's team finds a corn city.
  2. Senku battles his old mentor Dr. Xeno Houston Wingfield and soldier Stanley Snyder.
  3. Senku is shot and later revived by the petrification-revival process.

South America Ep 70–80

  1. Senku's team and Stanley's forces race across the Americas.
  2. A new kind of weapon is used at the end of the chase.
  3. The two sides decide to cooperate.

Rocket Construction Ep 81–87

  1. Teams spread around the globe to gather resources.
  2. Senku builds a computer and works out how to launch a rocket.

Moon Mission Ep 88–94

  1. The crew launches into space.
  2. Senku makes contact with the beings behind the petrification.
  3. The series ends with humanity looking toward the future.
