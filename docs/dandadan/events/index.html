Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Turbo Granny Ep 1–5

  1. Momo is abducted by the Serpoians and awakens psychic powers.
  2. Okarun is possessed by the yokai Turbo Granny, who steals his 'golden balls'.
  3. Momo and Okarun beat Turbo Granny in a high-speed chase and set out to recover the stolen balls.

Acrobatic Silky Ep 6–7

  1. Aira is possessed by the spirit Acrobatic Silky.
  2. Silky's tragic past as a mother who lost her daughter is revealed.
  3. Silky's spirit ends up bonded to Aira, giving her powers.

Dover Demon and Nessie Ep 8–9

  1. The Serpoians return with the Dover Demon.
  2. Momo and Okarun face the merged Serpo Dover Demon Nessie.

Jiji and the Cursed House Ep 10–12

  1. Jiji transfers in and stirs up Okarun's jealousy.
  2. A cattle-mutilation case leads to another fight with aliens.
  3. The group heads to Jiji's cursed house.

Evil Eye Ep 13–18

  1. The village worships a 'giant snake' legend tied to human sacrifice.
  2. Jiji is possessed by the Evil Eye, a vengeful spirit of a boy who was sacrificed.
  3. Jiji comes to terms with the Evil Eye and joins the group.

Space Kaiju Ep 19–24

  1. Okarun works to get stronger.
  2. The group works to fix Jiji's house.
  3. A space kaiju attacks, and a giant robot is built to face it.
