Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Exorcist Cram School Ep 1–17

  1. Rin's blue flames awaken and his guardian Father Fujimoto dies protecting him.
  2. Rin discovers that Yukio is already an exorcist and will be his teacher.
  3. During a training camp the class learns that Rin carries Satan's flames, and the Vatican puts him on trial.

Anime-original ending Ep 18–25

  1. The Order treats Rin as a threat and debates whether he should be executed.
  2. Satan possesses a body and opens a Gehenna Gate over the academy.
  3. Rin and Yukio confront Satan together to close the gate.

Kyoto Impure King Ep 26–37

  1. The Impure King's left eye is stolen from the academy and a traitor is suspected among the Myoo Dharani sect.
  2. Rin trains to master his flames with Shura's help and makes peace with Suguro.
  3. The Impure King is revived and Rin destroys it with his controlled flames.

Academy Seven Wonders and Shimane Illuminati Ep 38–49

  1. Renzo Shima is revealed to be working for the Illuminati as a double agent.
  2. Izumo is abducted and learns the truth about her mother and her sister Tsukumo.
  3. Rin and the class storm the Illuminati facility in Shimane to rescue Izumo.

Beyond the Snow Ep 50–61

  1. Yukio's left eye shows signs of Satan's flames.
  2. Rin's and Yukio's birthday turns into a confrontation over the secrets Yukio keeps.
  3. Yukio leaves the Order and joins the Illuminati.

The Blue Night Ep 62–73

  1. Rin witnesses the history of Shiro Fujimoto and his mother Yuri Egin.
  2. The origin of Satan's possession and the events of the Blue Night are revealed.
  3. Rin returns to the present resolved to bring Yukio back.
