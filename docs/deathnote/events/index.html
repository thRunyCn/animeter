Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Kira vs. L Ep 1–10

  1. Light meets the shinigami Ryuk, the notebook's original owner.
  2. L baits Light into revealing that Kira is in the Kanto region.
  3. Light kills FBI agent Raye Penber and his colleagues.
  4. L enrolls at Light's university and reveals himself to Light.

The Second Kira Ep 11–15

  1. Misa Amane trades half her life for Shinigami Eyes and tracks Light down.
  2. L detains Light and Misa as suspects.
  3. Light gives up ownership of the notebook and loses his memories to clear his name.

Yotsuba Ep 16–25

  1. The task force traps Kyosuke Higuchi of the Yotsuba Group as the third Kira.
  2. Light regains his memories when he touches the notebook again.
  3. Rem kills Watari and L to protect Misa, and dies herself.

Mello and Near Ep 26–37

  1. Mello kidnaps the police director's daughter to get the notebook, and Soichiro Yagami dies after the raid.
  2. Light enlists Teru Mikami and Kiyomi Takada as Kira's agents.
  3. Near exposes Light at the Yellow Box warehouse, and Ryuk writes Light's name.
