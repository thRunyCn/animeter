Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Under One Roof Ep 1–4

  1. Chinatsu moves in with Taiki's family and they agree to keep it secret.
  2. Taiki vows to reach nationals and Chinatsu gives him a good-luck bracelet.
  3. Inspired by Chinatsu, Taiki beats Kishi in a preliminary match.

Inter-High Qualifiers Ep 5–8

  1. Taiki's childhood friend Hina discovers the secret and hurts her ankle.
  2. Taiki loses his qualifying matches while Chinatsu advances to nationals.

Summer Festival Ep 9–12

  1. Taiki falls ill and Chinatsu nurses him, nearly leading to a kiss.
  2. Chinatsu sets boundaries before summer vacation.
  3. Chinatsu sees Taiki with Hina at the festival and is hurt by his earlier lie.

Nationals and Hina's Confession Ep 13–16

  1. Hina confesses to Taiki.
  2. On Chinatsu's birthday a storm strands the two at an inn where they clear up misunderstandings.
  3. Chinatsu's mother returns and Hina tells Chinatsu about her confession.

Yusa Match and Culture Festival Ep 17–20

  1. Taiki beats Yusa.
  2. Taiki replaces the prince in the class play and a prop accident sparks rumors about him and Hina.

Training Camp and Hina's Answer Ep 21–25

  1. Chinatsu admits to Karen that she is interested in Taiki.
  2. At the campfire Taiki turns down Hina, who runs off in tears.
  3. Taiki commits to pursuing Chinatsu, accepting that his friendship with Hina will change.

Season 2 Ep 26–27

  1. Taiki and Chinatsu's outing is interrupted by Matsuoka and Kido.
  2. Kido mocks Chinatsu for having quit basketball.
