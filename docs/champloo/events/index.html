Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Tempestuous Temperaments Ep 1

  1. Fuu saves Mugen and Jin from execution.
  2. She makes them promise to help her find 'the samurai who smells of sunflowers'.

The Road West (standalone) Ep 2–11

  1. The trio are hired as bodyguards in a gang war.
  2. Fuu picks up a lead that points the trio toward Nagasaki.

The Disorder Diaries Ep 12

  1. Fuu recounts the journey so far.

Misguided Miscreants Ep 13–14

  1. Mugen meets his old partner Mukuro.
  2. The two clash in an explosive ending.

The Road Continues (standalone) Ep 15–23

  1. Fuu explains she wants revenge on her father.
  2. She learns the sunflower samurai's name is Seizo Kasumi.
  3. The blind musician Sara tries to kill Jin.

Evanescent Encounter Ep 24–26

  1. Fuu meets her dying father, a hidden Christian.
  2. Jin and Mugen fight the shogunate's assassins.
  3. The trio parts ways.
