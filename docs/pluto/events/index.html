Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

The Robot Murders Ep 1–3

  1. The beloved Swiss robot Mont Blanc is found destroyed with horn-like objects at the head.
  2. Gesicht asks Atom, another of the seven great robots, to help analyze the murders.
  3. Gesicht begins to suspect his own memory data has been altered.

Atom and Uran Ep 4

  1. Ochanomizu takes in a battered robot dog and tries to repair it.
  2. A mysterious man claiming to be the dog's owner visits the professor's house.

Gesicht's Past Ep 5–6

  1. Gesicht escorts Adolf Haas to a safe house and investigates his past.
  2. Hercules senses the approaching threat.
  3. In Persia Gesicht meets Abullah and catches him in a lie.
  4. Gesicht recovers his memory of killing a human after his robot child was destroyed.

Atom's Revival Ep 7

  1. Tenma tries to restore Atom using Gesicht's memories.
  2. Epsilon refuses to fight and is urged to evacuate.

Bora Ep 8

  1. Ochanomizu watches Atom's erratic behavior after his revival.
  2. Dr. Tenma confronts his past and the mastermind behind the murders is revealed.
  3. Atom faces Pluto as a world-destroying weapon is triggered.
