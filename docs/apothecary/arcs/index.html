The Rear Palace

Maomao, an apothecary's daughter from the pleasure district, is kidnapped and sold as a servant into the emperor's rear palace, where she tries to keep her head down. Her knowledge of medicine and poisons soon catches the eye of the beautiful eunuch Jinshi.
