Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

The Rear Palace Ep 1–12

  1. Maomao discovers that lead-based face powder is what's been making the emperor's infant children sick.
  2. Jinshi makes Maomao a food taster for Consort Gyokuyou at the Jade Pavilion.
  3. Maomao untangles a poisoning at the garden party and the old secret behind Consort Ah-Duo's lost child.
  4. Maomao is dismissed in a palace shake-up, but Jinshi buys her contract to bring her back.

The Outer Court and Lakan Ep 13–24

  1. Maomao links a string of accidents and thefts to a plot against Jinshi and saves him from a falling ornament.
  2. She exposes the court lady Suirei's faked death, done with a resurrection drug.
  3. Maomao learns that the strategist Lakan is her biological father, and she beats him in a high-stakes game of shogi.
  4. Lakan buys out the contract of Maomao's mother, the sick courtesan Fengxian.

Return to the Rear Palace Ep 25–36

  1. Maomao finds that trendy perfumes are endangering pregnancies in the palace.
  2. Maomao reveals how the 'moon fairy' illusion was done during the foreign envoys' visit.
  3. The Empress Dowager asks Maomao to look into whether a curse killed the late emperor.
  4. Assassins attack Jinshi on a northern hunt, and he and Maomao hide in a cave.

The Shi Clan Rebellion Ep 37–48

  1. Maomao helps Consort Gyokuyou safely through a breech birth.
  2. Maomao is kidnapped to the Shi clan's stronghold, where she finds they are preparing for war.
  3. Jinshi leads a force to rescue her, and the Shi clan's leaders die.
  4. Gyokuyou becomes empress, while Loulan, thought dead, secretly survives and prepares to leave the country.

The Locust Crisis Ep 49–51

  1. Jinshi faces warnings of a coming locust swarm, and Maomao finds a Shi clan book about preventing one.
  2. Maomao links a fireproof 'fire-rat' robe to a possible spy.
