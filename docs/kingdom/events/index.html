Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Sei Kyou's Rebellion Ep 1–15

  1. Shin's friend Hyou dies after serving as the king's body double.
  2. Shin meets Ei Sei and the mountain girl Ten.
  3. Ei Sei allies with the mountain tribes under Yo Tan Wa and retakes the palace.

Battle of Dakan Plains Ep 16–22

  1. Shin fights in a five-man squad led by Hakuhei.
  2. Shin kills the Wei general Kyuukou.
  3. The great general Ou Ki appears on the battlefield.

Training Ep 23–24

  1. Ou Ki sends Shin to a lawless frontier region to fight bandits as training.
  2. Shin learns to lead men in battle rather than only fight alone.

Battle of Bayou Ep 25–38

  1. Ou Ki names Shin's unit the Hi Shin Unit.
  2. Shin kills Zhao general Fuuki.
  3. Ou Ki falls to Hou Ken and Ri Boku's scheme.

Alliance and the Third Faction Ep 39–48

  1. Ryo Fui's faction threatens to dominate the court.
  2. Ei Sei's past as a hostage in Zhao and his escape with Shi Ka are told.
  3. Ei Sei wins the support of the Third Faction.

Sanyou Campaign Ep 49–71

  1. Kyou Kai joins the Hi Shin Unit.
  2. Shin, Ou Hon and Mou Ten fight together as three young units.
  3. Mou Gou defeats Ren Pa's army and Qin takes Sanyou.

Sanyou Aftermath Ep 72–77

  1. Ka Ryo Ten joins the Hi Shin Unit as its strategist.
  2. Shin's unit is tested in smaller skirmishes.

Coalition Invasion Ep 78–101

  1. Kankoku Pass holds against the coalition.
  2. Shin's unit fights the Chu general Ka Rin's forces.
  3. Ei Sei rallies the people at the city of Sai and holds it until help arrives.

Kyou Kai's Revenge Ep 102–103

  1. Kyou Kai returns to the Shiyuu assassin clan to confront Yu Ki, the woman who killed her sister.
  2. Shin follows Kyou Kai and helps her survive the clan's ritual battle.

Conspiracy in the Court Ep 104–108

  1. Seikyou is framed for rebellion in Tunliu.
  2. Shin fights to protect Seikyou and expose the plot.

Fire Dragons of Wei Ep 109–115

  1. Shin's unit faces the Wei Fire Dragons.
  2. Ou Hon and Mou Ten fight alongside Shin.

State of Ai Ep 116–129

  1. Ro Ai's forces march on Xianyang during the coronation.
  2. The Hi Shin Unit helps defend the capital.
  3. Ei Sei is formally crowned, and Ryo Fui steps back.

Battle of Kokuyou Hill Ep 130–142

  1. Shin's unit fights across the river in Kokuyou's forests.
  2. Kan Ki springs a trap that brings down the Zhao commander Kei Sha.
  3. Kan Ki has the captured Zhao soldiers killed, shocking Shin.

Invasion of Zhao Ep 143–155

  1. Ou Sen leads the three-army invasion of Zhao.
  2. Yo Tan Wa attacks Retsubi with her mountain tribes.
  3. Shin's unit forms an elite strike force against Zhao commanders.
