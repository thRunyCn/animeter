Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Undead and Unluck Ep 1–4

  1. Andy and Fuuko team up and are hunted by Union agents.
  2. They learn of the Union, its Roundtable seats and the rule-setting being called God.
  3. In Russia the pair defeats Gina, whose ability freezes things in place.

Spoil Ep 5–10

  1. Spoil turns a whole town's people into zombies.
  2. Andy's violent alter ego Victor surfaces and overpowers everyone.
  3. A failed quest triggers a penalty that adds the new UMA Galaxy to the world.

Under and the Auction Ep 11–15

  1. Andy and Fuuko protect the auctioned Negator boy Chikara from hunters.
  2. Andy clashes with Under's Rip, and Tatiana's backstory is revealed.
  3. Chikara decides to join the Union.

Billy's Betrayal Ep 16–18

  1. The Union's leader Billy betrays the Roundtable and abducts its members.
  2. Juiz reveals that God has destroyed and remade the world in repeating loops.

Autumn Ep 19–24

  1. Fuuko meets the manga's author, Anno Un, who has her own Negator ability.
  2. Fuuko's spirit travels into Andy's past and learns where his Undead ability came from.
  3. Andy unlocks his new Deadline technique.
  4. Autumn is captured, and Rip agrees to work with Andy and Fuuko.

Winter Arc Film

  1. The special picks up the Union's fight after the Autumn quest.
  2. Its broadcast doubled as the lead-in to the second season, announced the same day.
