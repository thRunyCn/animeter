Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Birth of Zero Ep 1–7

  1. Lelouch receives Geass from the witch C.C. in Shinjuku.
  2. He kills his half-brother Clovis and becomes Zero.
  3. Suzaku becomes the pilot of the Lancelot.

The Black Knights Ep 8–12

  1. Zero founds the Order of the Black Knights.
  2. The Battle of Narita causes a landslide that kills Shirley's father.

Geass vs. Geass Ep 13–17

  1. Lelouch uses Geass to erase Shirley's memories of him.
  2. The mind-reader Mao targets C.C. and Nunnally.
  3. Euphemia chooses Suzaku as her knight.

Kamine Island and Kyushu Ep 18–20

  1. Lelouch is forced to Geass Suzaku to live.
  2. Zero, Suzaku and Euphemia are stranded on Kamine Island and stumble on ancient Geass ruins.
  3. Zero and Suzaku team up to repel a Chinese Federation-backed invasion of Kyushu.

Black Rebellion Ep 21–25

  1. Lelouch's Geass accidentally orders Euphemia to kill the Japanese.
  2. Zero shoots Euphemia and launches the Black Rebellion in Tokyo.
  3. Suzaku unmasks Zero and the two face off at gunpoint.

Zero's Return Ep 26–29

  1. Lelouch's memories return and Zero reappears.
  2. Rolo, a Geass-wielding 'brother', is planted to watch him.
  3. Lelouch wins Rolo over and saves Black Knights from execution.

The Chinese Federation Ep 30–37

  1. The Knights of the Round arrive in Area 11.
  2. Zero disrupts the Tianzi's arranged marriage and sparks a Chinese revolution.
  3. The United States of Japan is effectively set up abroad.

The Geass Directorate Ep 38–40

  1. Shirley regains her memories and is killed by Rolo.
  2. Lelouch wipes out the Geass Directorate.
  3. Lelouch confronts his father in the C's World.

Tokyo II and Betrayal Ep 41–44

  1. Suzaku launches a FLEIJA bomb over Tokyo, devastating the city.
  2. Nunnally is believed dead.
  3. Schneizel turns the Black Knights against Zero.

Zero Requiem Ep 45–50

  1. Lelouch defeats his father and mother in the Sword of Akasha.
  2. Lelouch becomes Emperor of Britannia.
  3. In the Zero Requiem, Suzaku as Zero kills Lelouch to end the cycle of hatred.

Lelouch of the Re;surrection Film

  1. C.C. revives Lelouch in the desert kingdom of Zilkhstan.
  2. Lelouch helps rescue Nunnally and Suzaku.
  3. Lelouch leaves with C.C., calling himself L.L.
