Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

First Selection Ep 1–11

  1. Ego Jinpachi tells the players that losers are banned from Japan's national team forever.
  2. Isagi's Team Z wins its way out of elimination in a round-robin of five-on-five teams.
  3. Isagi learns to read the field and finish plays himself.

Second Selection Ep 12–22

  1. Isagi joins forces with Bachira to fight through the selection.
  2. Isagi, Nagi and Barou form an unlikely trio of egoists.
  3. Isagi's team falls to Rin Itoshi's top-ranked team, and Rin takes Bachira.

Third Selection Ep 23–24

  1. The survivors play mixed matches to fight for spots on the Blue Lock eleven.
  2. Ego announces the match against U-20 Japan, with Blue Lock's future on the line.

U-20 Tryouts Ep 25–29

  1. New talents like Karasu, Otoya and Yukimiya show what they can do.
  2. Isagi learns to adapt with his 'flow' state.
  3. Ego picks the starting eleven.

Blue Lock vs. U-20 Japan Ep 30–38

  1. Sae Itoshi takes on his brother Rin on the pitch.
  2. Substitutes like Bachira and Nagi change the game in the second half.
  3. Isagi scores the winning goal in the final minute.
