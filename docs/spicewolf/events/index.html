Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Pasloe and the Silver Coins Ep 1–6

  1. Holo leaves the village of Pasloe in Lawrence's wagon and offers to be his travel companion.
  2. Lawrence is drawn into a scheme to profit from a coming change in a silver coin's purity.
  3. The deal pits him against the powerful Medio Company, and Holo reveals her true wolf form to save him.

Ruvinheigen and the Gold Smuggling Ep 7–13

  1. Lawrence faces ruin when the armor he bought on credit suddenly loses its value.
  2. He and Holo enlist the young shepherdess Norah in a plan to smuggle gold into the city.
  3. The plan is betrayed, and Holo and Norah's skills get them out of a trap.
  4. Lawrence and Holo share a last meal with Norah before going their separate ways.

Kumersun and the Pyrite Speculation Ep 14–19

  1. The young merchant Amarty falls for Holo and asks for her hand.
  2. A speculative craze over pyrite turns into a contest between Lawrence and Amarty.
  3. Lawrence and Holo face a rift over their relationship before reconciling.

Tereo and the Snake God Ep 20–25

  1. Lawrence and Holo meet Elsa, the young woman who runs the village church, and the miller Evan.
  2. Tereo faces a crisis linked to the church and the pagan snake god.
  3. Holo learns hard truths about her homeland, Yoitsu, and the journey continues north.
