Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Living with the Sohmas Ep 1–11

  1. Tohru learns members of the Sohma family turn into zodiac animals when hugged by the opposite sex.
  2. She moves in with Yuki, Kyo and Shigure.
  3. Hatori reveals the pain the curse caused him and his former fiancee.

Second Year Ep 12–25

  1. Akito, the family head, is introduced.
  2. Kisa, Ritsu and Hiro open up to Tohru.
  3. Kyo's horrifying true form is revealed, and Tohru accepts him.

Student Council and Summer Ep 26–35

  1. Yuki works with the brash Kakeru and quiet Machi.
  2. Isuzu 'Rin' is introduced after breaking up with Haru.
  3. Akito disrupts the summer trip at the Sohma villa.

Akito's Grip Ep 36–50

  1. Kureno, the Rooster, reveals his curse is already broken.
  2. Rin goes missing after defying Akito.
  3. Tohru learns that Akito is a woman.

The Final Ep 51–63

  1. One by one, the zodiac members' curses break.
  2. Kyo's guilt over Tohru's mother's death is revealed.
  3. Akito is freed from her obsession, and Tohru and Kyo leave together for a new life.
