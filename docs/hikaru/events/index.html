Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

The Replacement Ep 1–4

  1. Yoshiki learns the returned Hikaru is an entity wearing his friend's body and memories, and chooses to keep the secret.
  2. An old woman's death and the arrival of the investigator Tanaka hint that something is wrong in the village.
  3. At the summer festival a barrier keeps Hikaru out of the shrine, and Yoshiki grieves the real Hikaru.

Asako and Determination Ep 5–7

  1. Yoshiki is dragged into a nightmarish space and nearly drowns Hikaru.
  2. Hikaru tries to kill the perceptive Asako and admits he killed Ms. Matsuura.
  3. Yoshiki stabs Hikaru, who gives him part of his body, and Yoshiki resolves to learn the truth.

Nonuki-sama Ep 8–12

  1. The group links the legend of 'Nonuki-sama' to a map of villages named after body parts.
  2. Tanaka decapitates Hikaru, and Yoshiki reattaches his head.
  3. Yoshiki's father reveals the Indo family's sin, and Hikaru confirms the real Hikaru is dead before the two reconcile on the beach.
