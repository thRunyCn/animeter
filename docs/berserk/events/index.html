Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

The Black Swordsman Ep 1

  1. Guts kills the corrupt Baron and takes his strange egg-shaped stone, a Behelit.
  2. The episode ends by flashing back to how Guts's past began.

Band of the Hawk Ep 2–6

  1. Griffith defeats Guts in a duel and claims him for the Band of the Hawk.
  2. Guts rises to become one of the band's commanders.
  3. Guts and Griffith barely survive the immortal warrior Zodd, who warns Guts about Griffith's fate.

Court Intrigue Ep 7–10

  1. Griffith becomes close to Princess Charlotte.
  2. Griffith survives an assassination attempt.
  3. Guts kills the scheming Julius and also a young boy who witnessed it.

Casca and the Battlefield Ep 11–14

  1. Casca explains why she follows Griffith.
  2. Guts holds off around a hundred enemy soldiers to cover Casca's escape.

Doldrey Ep 15–17

  1. Casca takes the fortress while Griffith and Guts beat the enemy commanders.
  2. The victory ends the war.
  3. The Queen plots to poison Griffith.

Departure and Downfall Ep 18–19

  1. Griffith burns the conspirators alive.
  2. Guts beats Griffith in a duel to leave the band.
  3. Griffith sleeps with Princess Charlotte and is imprisoned and tortured.

Rescue of Griffith Ep 20–22

  1. Guts rejoins the Hawks and grows close to Casca.
  2. The band finds Griffith crippled and unable to speak after a year of torture.

The Eclipse Ep 23–25

  1. The God Hand offer Griffith a place among them.
  2. Griffith sacrifices the Band of the Hawk and is reborn as Femto.
  3. Guts loses an eye and an arm, and the series ends at that moment of despair.
