Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Amusement Park Arc Ep 1–5

  1. Former subordinate Shin Asakura, a mind-reader, joins Sakamoto at the store.
  2. Sakamoto fends off assassins at the Sugar Park amusement park while on a family outing.
  3. Lu Shaotang, a Chinese mafia heiress, joins the store crew.

Lab Arc Ep 6–9

  1. Sniper Heisuke Mashimo targets Sakamoto and ends up joining the store.
  2. Lu is kidnapped by a lab that experiments on assassins.
  3. Sakamoto and Shin storm the lab to rescue her.

Death Row Prisoner Arc Ep 10–16

  1. Death row killers freed by Slur hunt Sakamoto and his friends.
  2. Order members including Nagumo, Shishiba and Osaragi clash with Slur's killers.
  3. Sakamoto pushes himself to his limit to protect his store crew.

JCC Transfer Exam Arc Ep 17–22

  1. Sakamoto, in disguise, and Shin take the JCC transfer exam.
  2. Each stage of the exam pits candidates against deadly traps and each other.
  3. Shin and his new classmates make it through the final stage.
