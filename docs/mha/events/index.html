Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Entrance Exam Ep 1–4

  1. Izuku rushes in to save his bully Bakugo from a villain, impressing All Might.
  2. All Might passes his Quirk, One For All, on to Izuku.
  3. Izuku breaks his body saving Ochaco in the U.A. entrance exam and is admitted.

Quirk Apprehension Test and Battle Trial Ep 5–8

  1. Aizawa threatens to expel the weakest student in a Quirk test.
  2. Izuku and Bakugo face off in a hero-versus-villain training exercise.

U.S.J. Ep 9–13

  1. Tomura Shigaraki leads the attack with the bio-engineered Nomu.
  2. All Might overpowers the Nomu with a barrage of punches despite his time limit.

U.A. Sports Festival Ep 14–25

  1. Izuku wins the obstacle race using a landmine.
  2. Izuku pushes Todoroki to use his fire side in their match.
  3. Bakugo wins the festival but is furious that Todoroki held back.

Vs. Hero Killer Ep 26–33

  1. Izuku trains with All Might's old teacher Gran Torino.
  2. Izuku, Todoroki and Iida defeat Stain in a Hosu alley.
  3. Stain's ideology inspires a wave of new villains.

Final Exams Ep 34–38

  1. Izuku and Bakugo must work together against All Might.
  2. Izuku runs into Shigaraki in a shopping mall.

Forest Training Camp Ep 39–45

  1. Izuku fights Muscular to protect the boy Kota.
  2. The League kidnaps Bakugo.

Hideout Raid Ep 46–50

  1. All For One appears and fights All Might at Kamino.
  2. All Might uses up his last power to win and retires as Symbol of Peace.
  3. U.A. moves its students into dorms.

Provisional Hero License Exam Ep 51–61

  1. Izuku develops a kick-based fighting style.
  2. Bakugo and Todoroki fail the license exam.
  3. Bakugo and Izuku fight at night, and Bakugo learns the truth about One For All.

Shie Hassaikai Ep 62–78

  1. Mirio Togata meets Izuku and the frightened Eri.
  2. Mirio loses his Quirk saving Eri, and Sir Nighteye is killed.
  3. Izuku uses Eri's rewind power to defeat Overhaul at 100%.

Remedial Course Ep 79–80

  1. Bakugo and Todoroki have to win over a group of rowdy children.
  2. Bakugo and Todoroki earn their provisional licenses.

U.A. School Festival Ep 81–86

  1. Izuku fights the villain Gentle Criminal to protect the festival.
  2. Eri smiles for the first time at the show.

Pro Hero Ep 87–90

  1. Endeavor and Hawks fight a powerful high-end Nomu.
  2. Endeavor's victory starts to change public opinion.

Joint Training Ep 91–100

  1. Izuku's Quirk erupts into the black-whip power of a past user.
  2. Izuku learns about the previous holders of One For All.

Endeavor Agency Ep 101–107

  1. The trio sharpens their Quirk control under Endeavor.
  2. Hawks feeds information from inside the League to the heroes.

Meta Liberation Army Ep 108–112

  1. Toga and Twice push through the fight against the Liberation Army.
  2. Shigaraki awakens his full Decay and takes command of the army.

Paranormal Liberation War Ep 113–131

  1. Hawks kills Twice.
  2. Shigaraki gets All For One's power and goes on a rampage.
  3. Dabi reveals he is Toya Todoroki, Endeavor's son.

Dark Hero Ep 132–138

  1. Izuku works himself ragged as a lone vigilante.
  2. Class 1-A confronts Izuku and brings him back to U.A.

Star and Stripe Ep 139–140

  1. Star and Stripe fights Shigaraki and damages his body before dying.
  2. Her sacrifice buys the heroes time to prepare for the final battle.

U.A. Traitor Ep 141–143

  1. Aoyama is revealed as All For One's informant.
  2. The students use Aoyama to lay a trap.

Final War and Epilogue Ep 144–170

  1. Bakugo is struck down and revived.
  2. Izuku fights Shigaraki, and Ochaco reaches Toga.
  3. Izuku defeats Shigaraki and gives up One For All, while Endeavor and Bakugo bring down All For One.
  4. The epilogue jumps ahead to Izuku as a teacher.
