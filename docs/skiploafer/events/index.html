Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

First Days in Tokyo Ep 1–4

  1. Mitsumi is guided to school by laid-back classmate Sousuke Shima and still manages her entrance speech after losing her script.
  2. She and Shima are made class representatives and she gathers her first circle of friends, including Makoto and Yuzuki.
  3. A misstep with the drama club hints that Shima has a past as a child actor.

Class Match and Rumors Ep 5–6

  1. Mitsumi trains with the prickly Mika and slowly wins her over.
  2. Rumors about Shima's absences cause a rift until he opens up about his home life.

Student Council and Summer Ep 7–9

  1. Mitsumi becomes council secretary and Shima agrees to a group zoo outing.
  2. Shima's childhood friend and fellow former actor Ririka confronts him about leaving acting behind.
  3. Mitsumi spends summer back home and returns with crackers for a downcast Shima.

School Festival Ep 10–12

  1. Overloaded with work, Mitsumi breaks down and Shima cheers her up with a song and dance.
  2. The class hunts for Shima's little brother Keiri during the festival.
  3. Shima's acting past comes out to his classmates and he tells Ririka he wants to move on.
