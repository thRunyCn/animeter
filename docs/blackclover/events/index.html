Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Magic Knights Entrance Arc Ep 1–10

  1. Yuno receives a four-leaf grimoire while Asta gets a battered five-leaf grimoire holding anti-magic swords.
  2. Asta is rejected by every squad at the Magic Knights exam except Yami's Black Bulls.
  3. Yuno joins the elite Golden Dawn.

Dungeon Exploration Arc Ep 11–19

  1. Asta and Noelle team up with Luck on the mission.
  2. Yuno and Klaus race the Diamond Kingdom's Mars through the dungeon.
  3. Asta and Yuno together overcome Mars as the dungeon collapses.

Royal Capital Invasion Arc Ep 20–27

  1. The Eye of the Midnight Sun strikes at the Royal Capital.
  2. Asta and his comrades fight off the attackers.
  3. Asta attends the Magic Knights' award ceremony and openly challenges the captains who look down on him.

Eye of the Midnight Sun: Encounter Arc Ep 28–37

  1. The Eye of the Midnight Sun's masked leader shows himself and takes an interest in Yuno.
  2. Asta's anti-magic proves a serious obstacle to the group's plans.
  3. Hints link the group to the long-dead elves.

Seabed Temple Arc Ep 38–50

  1. Noelle befriends Kahono, granddaughter of the temple's priest.
  2. Asta and friends beat the temple's priest at his game.
  3. Asta's arms are cursed after his fight with Vetto of the Third Eye.
  4. Noelle unlocks a new strength as the Black Bulls hold the line.

Witches' Forest Arc Ep 51–65

  1. The Witch Queen offers to heal Asta's arms for a price.
  2. The Eye of the Midnight Sun attacks the forest.
  3. Vanessa defies the Witch Queen to protect her friends.
  4. Asta's arms are healed and he gains a new demon-slaying sword.

Royal Knights Arc Ep 66–84

  1. The Black Bulls fight for respect during the squads' ranking.
  2. Asta, Yuno and others enter the Royal Knights selection test.
  3. Asta and Yuno both make the Royal Knights.

Eye of the Midnight Sun: Subjugation Arc Ep 85–94

  1. The Royal Knights storm the hideout and battle the Third Eye.
  2. Licht's group's true goal becomes clear.
  3. Captain William Vangeance is revealed as the leader of the Eye of the Midnight Sun.

Elf Reincarnation Arc Ep 95–112

  1. The elves possess mages across the kingdom and attack the Clover Kingdom.
  2. Patri, possessing Vangeance, defeats Wizard King Julius.
  3. Asta learns the elves were massacred in a tragedy staged by a devil.

Shadow Palace Arc Ep 113–127

  1. The Clover knights storm the Shadow Palace.
  2. Licht is revived and the devil Zagred is revealed as the true culprit behind the elf massacre.
  3. Asta, Yuno and the elves defeat Zagred together.

Heart Kingdom Training Arc Ep 128–157

  1. Asta is put on trial because of his devil powers.
  2. The Spade Kingdom's Dark Triad is introduced as a new enemy.
  3. Asta and Noelle train in the Heart Kingdom.

Heart Kingdom Joint Struggle Arc Ep 158–170

  1. The Clover Kingdom allies with the Heart Kingdom under Queen Loropechika.
  2. Noelle confronts Vanica, the Dark Triad member who killed her mother.
  3. Asta begins to face the devil sealed in his grimoire.

Spade Kingdom Raid Arc Ep 171–173

  1. The Magic Knights infiltrate Spade Castle.
  2. Asta and his comrades face the Dark Triad's forces.
