Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

The First Step Ep 1–12

  1. Ippo catches ten falling leaves to prove he is worth training.
  2. Ippo beats gym prodigy Ichiro Miyata in their rematch sparring bout, and Miyata leaves to turn pro elsewhere.
  3. Ippo earns his pro license and wins his debut against Yusuke Oda.

East Japan Rookie King Tournament Ep 13–28

  1. Ippo knocks out American serviceman Jason Ozma in the first round.
  2. Ippo outlasts Kobashi's clinching and Hayami's 'shotgun' flurries.
  3. Mashiba beats an injured Miyata in the semi-final, robbing Ippo of his promised rematch.
  4. Ippo defeats Mashiba to become East Japan rookie king but damages his right fist.

All Japan Rookie King vs Sendo Ep 29–34

  1. Sendo seeks Ippo out personally and declares him a rival.
  2. Ippo enters the bout with a hand that has not fully healed.
  3. Ippo wins a brutal slugfest to become All Japan rookie king.

Climbing the Rankings Ep 35–50

  1. Ippo spars with champion Eiji Date and later beats the unpredictable Okita.
  2. Miyata travels to Thailand to fight Jimmy Sisfa.
  3. Ippo overcomes the speedy Saeki despite a swollen-eye blind spot.
  4. Ippo outlasts Russian ex-amateur Volg Zangief, who befriends Ippo's family afterward.

Title Challenge vs Date Ep 51–57

  1. The episodes explore Date's past and his reasons for returning to the ring.
  2. Date's corkscrew 'heart break shot' dismantles Ippo.
  3. Ippo suffers his first professional loss.

Comeback Ep 58–62

  1. Ippo is heartbroken after the defeat and wonders if he belongs in boxing.
  2. Ippo returns to the ring with new resolve.

Kamogawa Gym Interlude Ep 63–68

  1. The spotlight shifts to Aoki, Kimura and Takamura outside Ippo's own bouts.
  2. Coach Kamogawa faces a personal crisis that rallies the gym.

Japanese Featherweight Title vs Sendo Ep 69–75

  1. Ippo and Sendo meet in a rematch for the Japanese featherweight belt.
  2. Ippo knocks Sendo out to become Japanese featherweight champion.

Champion Road Film

  1. Ippo defends his Japanese title against former amateur Kazuki Sanada.
  2. Sanada, a doctor by training, tries to counter Ippo with his swooping Hien technique.

Mashiba vs Kimura Film

  1. Kimura unveils a new technique against Mashiba but cannot take the belt.
  2. Mashiba keeps his junior lightweight title.

Miyata's OPBF Title and Date vs Ricardo Ep 77–84

  1. Miyata survives Ernie's counters and pressure to win the OPBF title.
  2. Ippo spars world champion Ricardo Martinez and is stopped after a Dempsey Roll.
  3. Date loses to Ricardo by TKO after breaking his fist.

Ippo vs Hammer Nao Ep 85–88

  1. Nao has reinvented himself as a rough journeyman fighter.
  2. Ippo wins with a liver blow, and Nao has to rethink his career.

Beach and Itagaki's Debut Ep 89–91

  1. Ippo fails to confess his feelings to Kumi at the beach.
  2. Itagaki is knocked out in a messy debut.

Takamura vs Bryan Hawk Ep 92–102

  1. Takamura endures a brutal 20 kg weight cut.
  2. Ippo knocks out his own opponent in the first round on the same card.
  3. Takamura survives Hawk's unorthodox attacks and knocks him out to become world junior middleweight champion.

Ippo vs Shimabukuro Ep 103–104

  1. Shimabukuro tries to counter the Dempsey Roll.
  2. Ippo retains his title.

Aoki vs Imae Ep 105–108

  1. Aoki uses his trademark tricks and a failed Frog Punch.
  2. Aoki and Imae fight to exhaustion and the match goes to a decision.

Ippo vs Sawamura Ep 109–115

  1. Volg returns to spar Ippo and demonstrates an anti-Dempsey counter.
  2. Sawamura counters the Dempsey Roll twice, nearly finishing Ippo.
  3. Ippo breaks through and knocks Sawamura out.

Takamura vs Eagle Ep 116–123

  1. Kimura and Aoki win undercard bouts, and Miyata routs his opponent.
  2. Takamura and Eagle open cuts on each other.
  3. A nearly blinded Takamura knocks Eagle out to win a second world title.

Kamogawa's Past Ep 124–127

  1. Nekota is revealed to be punch drunk.
  2. Kamogawa takes on the American who beat Nekota, rising again and again.
  3. Yuki leaves with Nekota to live in the mountains.
