Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Grace Field House Escape Ep 1–12

  1. Emma and Norman discover that the orphanage raises children as food for demons, and that Isabella works for the demons.
  2. The three start planning an escape while Isabella and the new caretaker Sister Krone watch them.
  3. Norman is shipped out, and Ray is revealed to have been Isabella's spy.
  4. Emma and Ray burn down the house and escape over the wall with the older children.

Sonju and Mujika Ep 13–14

  1. The children are rescued by Sonju and Mujika, demons who don't eat humans.
  2. The children follow clues left by the mysterious William Minerva.

The Shelter and Norman's Return Ep 15–19

  1. The group reaches a shelter left by Minerva.
  2. The season skips the manga's Goldy Pond arc entirely.
  3. Norman returns, alive, with a plan to wipe out the demons.

Anime-Original Ending Ep 20–23

  1. Emma seeks a peaceful way to free humans without genocide.
  2. The children return to the farms to rescue those left behind.
  3. The final episode ends with a still-image epilogue of the children's lives in the human world.
