Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Introductions Ep 1–6

  1. Maka and Soul lose all their collected souls after mistaking the cat Blair for a witch.
  2. The students' make-up lesson leads them to the eccentric meister Franken Stein.
  3. Stein beats all three teams and becomes an Academy teacher.
  4. Kid enrolls and loses his first fight when his symmetry is ruined.

Crona and the Black Blood Ep 7–13

  1. Soul is badly wounded shielding Maka from Crona and is infected with black blood.
  2. Black Star and Tsubaki face her soul-eating brother Masamune, and Tsubaki absorbs his soul.
  3. Kid and Black Star find the legendary Excalibur and quickly abandon it.
  4. Maka and Soul's wavelengths fall out of sync before they defeat the immortal werewolf Free.

Exams and Ghost Ship Ep 14–17

  1. Maka tops the class on a huge written exam.
  2. Kid's team investigates a ghost ship and runs into Crona.
  3. Ox becomes the latest victim of Excalibur's insufferable personality.

Revival of the Kishin Ep 18–24

  1. Maka embraces madness to reach Crona's heart and befriends them.
  2. Stein defeats Medusa, but Eruka injects black blood into the Kishin.
  3. Asura awakens, fights Lord Death and flees to spread madness across the world.

Arachnophobia Ep 25–33

  1. Crona enrolls at the DWMA and Marie becomes Stein's partner.
  2. Arachne reveals herself and vows revenge on Lord Death.
  3. Medusa returns in the body of a little girl.
  4. Maka, Black Star and Kid finally achieve a three-team soul resonance.

Battle for Brew Ep 34–36

  1. Soul uses his inner Little Demon's piano to link everyone's wavelengths.
  2. The seven students combine attacks against Mosquito.
  3. Arachnophobia gets away with Brew.

Crona's Escape and Medusa's End Ep 37–45

  1. Crona flees and confesses their betrayal to Maka and Soul.
  2. Medusa surrenders to the DWMA in a trade, and Stein disappears.
  3. The DWMA storms Arachne's castle while Maka goes after Crona.
  4. Crona is badly hurt saving Maka, and Maka and Soul defeat Medusa.

The Final Battle with Asura Ep 46–51

  1. Black Star masters the Enchanted Sword and beats Mifune.
  2. Asura kills Arachne, and Lord Death is wounded protecting Kid.
  3. Maka frees Soul from the black blood, and Kid awakens his true power.
  4. Maka's courage shatters Asura's fear, and she defeats him with a punch.
