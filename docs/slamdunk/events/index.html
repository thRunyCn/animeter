Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Hanamichi Joins Shohoku Ep 1–12

  1. Hanamichi falls for Haruko and challenges captain Akagi to a one-on-one.
  2. Hanamichi joins the team and clashes with rookie star Kaede Rukawa.
  3. Hanamichi grinds through fundamentals and learns the lay-up.

Practice Game vs Ryonan Ep 13–20

  1. Hanamichi makes his game debut but starts on the bench.
  2. Sendoh's skill pushes Shohoku to the limit, and Ryonan wins narrowly.

Miyagi and Mitsui Return Ep 21–27

  1. Mitsui's gang storms the gym to start a fight with the team.
  2. Mitsui breaks down in front of coach Anzai and rejoins the team.

Prelims: Miuradai and Gohoku Ep 28–35

  1. Hanamichi makes a chaotic official debut against Miuradai.
  2. Shohoku beats Miuradai and moves on through the bracket.

Prelims: Shoyo Ep 36–46

  1. Mitsui's three-pointers keep Shohoku in the game.
  2. Hanamichi dunks and Shohoku defeats Shoyo to reach the final league.

Final League: Kainan Ep 47–59

  1. Akagi injures his ankle but returns to the game.
  2. Hanamichi's late pass goes to the opponent and Shohoku loses by two.

Training and Kainan vs Ryonan Ep 60–66

  1. Hanamichi shaves his head in contrition and trains intensely.
  2. Maki and Sendoh duel as Kainan beats Ryonan.

Final League: Ryonan Ep 67–84

  1. Uozumi and Akagi battle under the basket.
  2. Shohoku beats Ryonan and qualifies for the Inter-High.

Road to the Nationals Ep 85–96

  1. Rukawa attends a national youth camp and aims for America.
  2. Hanamichi takes on 20,000 shots of jump-shot training with Anzai.

Anime-Original Finale Ep 97–101

  1. Shohoku plays a practice match against a combined team of rivals.
  2. The anime ends without adapting the Inter-High.

The First Slam Dunk Film

  1. Ryota's backstory with his late brother Sota is told alongside the game.
  2. Shohoku defeats Sannoh in the final seconds.
