Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Mami's Arc Ep 1–3

  1. Madoka and Sayaka meet Kyubey and transfer student Homura.
  2. Mami shows them how to hunt witches.
  3. Mami is killed by the witch Charlotte.

Sayaka's Arc Ep 4–9

  1. Sayaka contracts to heal Kyosuke's hand.
  2. It's revealed that Soul Gems hold the girls' souls.
  3. Sayaka becomes a witch, and Kyoko gives her life to stop her.

Homura's Past Ep 10

  1. Homura is a time traveller who has repeated the same month many times to save Madoka.

Walpurgisnacht Ep 11–12

  1. Homura fights Walpurgisnacht alone.
  2. Madoka wishes to erase witches before they're born, becoming a concept that rewrites the universe.

Rebellion Film

  1. Homura realises the world is a barrier inside her Soul Gem.
  2. Homura seizes part of Madoka's power and becomes a demon who rewrites the world.

Walpurgisnacht: Rising Film

  1. Continues on from Homura's rewriting of the world at the end of Rebellion.
  2. Released in Japanese theatres on 28 August 2026 after several delays.
