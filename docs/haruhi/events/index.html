Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

The Melancholy of Haruhi Suzumiya Ep 1–6

  1. Haruhi founds the SOS Brigade with Kyon, the silent reader Yuki Nagato, the timid Mikuru Asahina and the transfer student Itsuki Koizumi.
  2. Yuki, Mikuru and Itsuki each reveal to Kyon that they are an alien interface, a time traveler and an esper, all watching Haruhi.
  3. A rogue classmate, Ryoko Asakura, tries to kill Kyon, and Yuki stops her.
  4. Haruhi unknowingly traps herself and Kyon in a closed space that could remake the world, and Kyon brings them back.

Early Brigade Activities Ep 7–9

  1. Haruhi enters the brigade in a local baseball tournament, and her boredom threatens to cause a closed space.
  2. In Bamboo Leaf Rhapsody, Mikuru takes Kyon back three years to the Tanabata night when he helps a younger Haruhi.
  3. The brigade's first client case, a missing computer club president, leads to a strange space and a giant cricket.

Remote Island Syndrome Ep 10–11

  1. A storm cuts the group off and their host is found apparently murdered.
  2. Haruhi and Kyon solve the 'crime', which turns out to be a staged mystery set up by Itsuki's organization to entertain her.

Endless Eight Ep 12–19

  1. Without realizing it, Haruhi traps the world in a loop of the same two weeks of August.
  2. Yuki reveals the loop has repeated over 15,000 times, and she remembers all of them.
  3. Kyon breaks the loop by suggesting the group do their summer homework together.
  4. The story is told across eight nearly identical episodes.

The Sigh of Haruhi Suzumiya Ep 20–24

  1. Haruhi's demands for the movie start becoming reality, such as Mikuru shooting real beams from her eyes.
  2. Kyon finally loses his temper with Haruhi over how she treats Mikuru.
  3. Kyon convinces Haruhi to announce the film is fiction, keeping its effects from spilling into the real world.

The School Festival and After Ep 25–27

  1. The finished, very amateur film The Adventures of Mikuru Asahina Episode 00 is shown in full.
  2. Haruhi and Yuki fill in for a band at the festival concert.
  3. The SOS Brigade takes on the computer club in a space strategy game, which Yuki wins.

Someday in the Rain Ep 28

  1. Haruhi sends Kyon on a long errand to fetch a heater.
  2. Kyon returns to find the clubroom quiet, setting the scene before the events of the Disappearance film.

The Disappearance of Haruhi Suzumiya Film

  1. Haruhi has vanished, Ryoko Asakura is back, and Yuki is a shy, ordinary girl.
  2. Kyon finds Haruhi at another school and gathers the brigade's members.
  3. Kyon realizes Yuki changed the world and decides he wants his strange old life back.
  4. Kyon travels back in time to the moment the world changed, with help from his future self.
