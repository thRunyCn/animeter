Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Welcome to Class 3-E Ep 1–6

  1. Koro-sensei threatens to destroy Earth in a year unless Class 3-E assassinates him.
  2. The troublemaker Karma returns and wounds Koro-sensei.
  3. The seductive assassin Irina Jelavich joins as an English teacher.
  4. Koro-sensei helps Class E raise their exam scores.

School Trip Ep 7–8

  1. Delinquents kidnap Kayano and Kanzaki, and Nagisa's group rescues them.
  2. A hired sniper fails to kill Koro-sensei.

Transfer Students Ep 9–11

  1. Ritsu, an AI-driven gun platform, joins the class and learns to cooperate.
  2. Itona Horibe arrives with tentacles of his own, guided by the masked Shiro.
  3. Itona claims to be Koro-sensei's brother.

Ball Game, Takaoka and Exams Ep 12–16

  1. Class E challenges the main campus at the ball game tournament.
  2. Takaoka tries to take over Class E's training, and Nagisa defeats him.
  3. Class E's top scores in the end-of-term exams earn them a trip to an island.

Island Assassination Ep 17–22

  1. The class's elaborate attack forces Koro-sensei into a near-invulnerable form.
  2. Classmates are poisoned, and the healthy students infiltrate a hotel to get the antidote.
  3. Takaoka returns for revenge, and Nagisa beats him without killing.

Second Term Begins Ep 23–28

  1. Shiro and Itona return, and Itona is freed from his tentacles.
  2. Itona joins Class E for good.
  3. Class E learns freerunning, and a careless accident forces them to make amends.

The Reaper Ep 29–30

  1. The Reaper captures Irina and lures the students into a trap.
  2. Karasuma and the students fight back and rescue Irina.
  3. Irina's feelings for Karasuma come to the fore.

School Festival and Principal Asano Ep 31–35

  1. Class E runs a hit stall at the school festival.
  2. Karma and Gakushu Asano compete in the second-term exams.
  3. Principal Gakuho Asano challenges Koro-sensei directly.

Kayano's Secret Ep 36–37

  1. Kayano reveals she has tentacles and wants revenge on Koro-sensei.
  2. Nagisa stops her and saves her life with Koro-sensei's help.

Koro-sensei's Past Ep 38–40

  1. Koro-sensei was once a human assassin used in a tentacle experiment.
  2. His bond with teacher Aguri Yukimura made him a teacher.
  3. Class E splits into 'kill' and 'save' factions and settles it in a paint-ball battle.

Searching for a Cure Ep 41–44

  1. Class E sneaks into a space mission to get research data.
  2. The students learn Koro-sensei may not explode after all.
  3. Class E throws Koro-sensei a birthday celebration.

Graduation Ep 45–47

  1. The government traps the school mountain under a laser barrier.
  2. Shiro and a second Reaper attack Koro-sensei.
  3. The students kill Koro-sensei, and Nagisa delivers the final blow.
  4. Years later, the former students follow the paths their teacher helped them find.
