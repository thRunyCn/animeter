Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Descent to the Red Dragon Ep 1–10

  1. Senshi joins the party and teaches them to cook monsters properly.
  2. Laios replaces his broken sword with one taken from a living-armor guardian.
  3. The party meets the orc chief Zon, whose people were displaced by the dragon.
  4. Disguised in frog suits, the group makes it to the dragon's lair in the underground castle town.

The Red Dragon Ep 11–13

  1. The party slays the dragon, but only Falin's bones remain inside it.
  2. Marcille resurrects Falin with forbidden ancient magic, using the dragon's flesh.
  3. The Mad Sorcerer appears, turning Falin into a dragon chimera and taking her away.

Kabru and Shuro Ep 14–16

  1. A flashback shows Kabru's party being saved by Shuro's group.
  2. Marcille is petrified by a cockatrice and slowly cured.
  3. Shuro confronts Laios over the forbidden magic used to revive Falin.

Chimera Falin Ep 17–20

  1. Chimera Falin attacks Shuro's party, killing several members.
  2. A shapeshifter creates confusing copies of the party.
  3. Laios enters Marcille's dream to help her beat a Nightmare.
  4. The cursed cat-girl Izutsumi ends up joining Laios's party.

The Golden Country Ep 21–24

  1. The party learns the Mad Sorcerer has bound the Golden Kingdom's people with an undying curse.
  2. Senshi reveals his history tied to the Golden Castle and his fear of griffins.
  3. Mushrooms swap the party members' races for a time.
  4. Senshi proposes cutting the dragon parts away from Falin to free her.
