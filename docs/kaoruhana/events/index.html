Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Rintaro and Kaoruko Ep 1–3

  1. Rintaro learns the girl he helped at his family's bakery attends rival Kikyo.
  2. Hostility between the two schools threatens their budding friendship.
  3. Kaoruko's protective friend Subaru Hoshina confronts them.

Subaru Ep 4–6

  1. Subaru asks Rintaro to stop seeing Kaoruko.
  2. Rintaro's friend Saku confronts him after discovering a note from Kaoruko.
  3. Subaru and Kaoruko talk through their friendship and Subaru asks Rintaro to be friends.

Bridging Chidori and Kikyo Ep 7–10

  1. Rintaro's friends defend the Kikyo girls, and a sports festival follows.
  2. At the aquarium Rintaro realizes he loves Kaoruko.
  3. His friends visit the bakery and learn about his past, and he admits his feelings to them.

Summer Confession Ep 11–13

  1. Rintaro bakes Kaoruko a birthday cake with his father's help.
  2. At the beach Rintaro accidentally confesses.
  3. Kaoruko returns his feelings and they become a couple.
