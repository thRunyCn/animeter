Fares and Strangers Ep 1–4

  1. Yuki Mitsuya of Mystery Kiss goes missing.

  1. Mitsuya's body is found and the cover-up is exposed.

  1. The case is reconstructed from the perspective of those involved.
  2. The film continues past the TV ending.
