Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Tokyo Outbreak Ep 1–5

  1. Akira finally quits his exploitative job and writes a list of 100 things to do before he dies.
  2. He reunites with his best friend Kencho, who confesses he dreams of doing stand-up comedy.
  3. Akira teams up with the cautious survivor Shizuka.

Road Trip of the Dead Ep 6–9

  1. The group falls into the hands of Gonzou's gang at a truck stop.
  2. Akira finally stands up to Gonzou with Shizuka's help.
  3. German tourist Beatrix joins the group after a sushi feast.
  4. They help a man named Kumano build a treehouse before heading to Akira's hometown.

Hometown of the Dead Ep 10–12

  1. Higurashi's group breaks the tunnel barrier and lets zombies into the village.
  2. Higurashi takes Akira's father hostage.
  3. Akira outwits Higurashi, the bridge is saved, and the group prepares to move on.
