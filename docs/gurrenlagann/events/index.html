Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Breaking Through the Ceiling Ep 1–4

  1. Simon digs up a drill-shaped key and the tiny mecha Lagann.
  2. Yoko joins Simon and Kamina on the surface.
  3. Kamina steals a Gunmen and names it Gurren.

The Dai-Gurren Brigade Ep 5–8

  1. The brigade captures the Dai-Gurren fortress.
  2. Kamina is killed in battle, and Simon falls into despair.

March on Teppelin Ep 9–16

  1. Simon meets Nia, daughter of the Spiral King Lordgenome.
  2. The brigade defeats the Four Generals.
  3. Simon defeats Lordgenome in Teppelin, and episode 16 is a compilation episode.

Kamina City Ep 17–22

  1. Rossiu and Simon clash over how to govern.
  2. Nia turns out to be an agent of the Anti-Spiral.
  3. The Anti-Spiral sends the moon crashing toward Earth.

The Final Battle Ep 23–27

  1. The moon is revealed to be the giant ship Cathedral Terra, which becomes the Arc-Gurren.
  2. The crew pilots the galaxy-sized Tengen Toppa Gurren Lagann.
  3. Nia fades after the Anti-Spiral is defeated, and Simon wanders off into retirement.
