Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Prologue: Dr. Tenma Ep 1–4

  1. Tenma operates on the boy Johan Liebert and loses his career standing.
  2. The hospital director and the two doctors blocking Tenma die of poisoned candy.
  3. Nine years later, Tenma saves a criminal who leads him to the grown-up Johan.
  4. Johan kills a man in front of Tenma, revealing he is a murderer.

The Girl from Heidelberg Ep 5–10

  1. Nina Fortner, a Heidelberg student, starts recovering memories of her past as Anna.
  2. Johan has Nina's adoptive parents killed.
  3. Inspector Lunge becomes convinced Tenma is the true killer.

Kinderheim 511 and the Pursuit Ep 11–24

  1. Tenma learns about Kinderheim 511, an East German orphanage that experimented on children.
  2. Tenma rescues the abused boy Dieter, who travels with him.
  3. Nina tracks Johan to Frankfurt, where she and Tenma cross paths again.
  4. Tenma's bitter ex-fiancee Eva re-enters his life.

Munich: The Thursday Boy Ep 25–39

  1. Johan manipulates Karl Neumann, the hidden son of the blind magnate Schuwald.
  2. Tenma and Nina close in on Johan's scheme at the university.
  3. Johan sets fire to Schuwald's library during a gathering, and Tenma helps save the guests.

Prague Ep 40–46

  1. Tenma meets Wolfgang Grimmer, a cheerful former Kinderheim 511 child.
  2. A Prague detective chases a series of murders tied to a children's picture book.
  3. The picture books written by a mysterious author begin to surface.

The Rose Mansion Ep 47–57

  1. Nina remembers being taken to the Red Rose Mansion.
  2. The massacre at the mansion is revealed to have been caused by Bonaparta's experiments.
  3. Tenma questions what Johan's 'scenery' means.

Converging Paths Ep 58–67

  1. Eva is drawn back into the hunt for Tenma.
  2. Johan turns on the shadowy men who once backed him.
  3. Nina finally faces her full memory of the Red Rose Mansion.

Ruhenheim Ep 68–74

  1. Johan turns the townspeople against one another, sparking a massacre.
  2. Grimmer dies after a final stand as the 'Magnificent Steiner'.
  3. Johan asks Tenma to shoot him, but Wim's father fires first and Tenma saves Johan's life again.
  4. Tenma visits the comatose Johan, who later disappears from his hospital bed.
