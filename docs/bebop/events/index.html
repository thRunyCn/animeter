Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Assembling the Crew Ep 1–4

  1. Spike and Jet chase bounties across the solar system, usually ending up broke.
  2. A data dog, Ein, ends up with the crew after a botched bounty.
  3. The con artist Faye Valentine crosses paths with the crew and attaches herself to the Bebop.

Ballad of Fallen Angels Ep 5

  1. Spike walks into a trap set by his former partner Vicious and survives a fall through a cathedral window.
  2. Spike's memories of Julia are first hinted at.

On the Road (standalone sessions) Ep 6–11

  1. The genius hacker Radical Ed joins the crew after the hunt for an AI hacker.
  2. Jet's past in Ganymede resurfaces when he meets his ex-girlfriend Alisa.

Jupiter Jazz Ep 12–13

  1. Faye leaves with the crew's money, and Spike follows a lead on Julia to Callisto.
  2. Vicious's attempted coup against the Red Dragon elders is set in motion.
  3. Spike and Vicious clash, and Gren's story ends in tragedy.

Pasts Catching Up (mostly standalone) Ep 14–22

  1. Faye's past as a woman frozen in cryosleep for decades is revealed.
  2. Jet confronts the truth behind the betrayal that cost him his arm.
  3. Faye receives a videotape from her younger self, recorded long before.

Knockin' on Heaven's Door Film

  1. A mysterious man, Vincent, releases a deadly nanomachine pathogen on Mars.
  2. Spike, Faye, Jet and Ed race to stop a larger attack during Halloween.

The Crew Drifts Apart Ep 23–24

  1. Faye finally tracks down her childhood home, but finds it gone.
  2. Ed leaves the Bebop to follow her father, and Ein goes with her.

The Real Folk Blues Ep 25–26

  1. Vicious seizes control of the Red Dragon Syndicate and hunts Spike's old associates.
  2. Spike reunites with Julia, who is killed soon after.
  3. Spike storms the Syndicate headquarters, kills Vicious, and collapses after the fight.
