Karasuno's Revival and the Inter-High Ep 1–25

  1. Hinata and Kageyama develop their lightning-fast 'freak quick' attack.
  2. Karasuno plays practice matches against Aoba Johsai and its old rival Nekoma.
  3. Karasuno loses to Aoba Johsai in the Inter-High prefectural tournament.

Tokyo Training Camp and Spring High Qualifiers Ep 26–50

  1. Hinata and Kageyama clash over changing their quick attack.
  2. Karasuno gets revenge on Aoba Johsai in the prefectural semifinal.
  3. Karasuno reaches the final against powerhouse Shiratorizawa.

Karasuno vs. Shiratorizawa Ep 51–60

  1. Tsukishima blocks ace Ushijima and finally falls in love with volleyball.
  2. Karasuno beats Shiratorizawa to earn a trip to nationals.

To the Top: Training Camps and Nationals Begin Ep 61–73

  1. Hinata works as a ball boy at Shiratorizawa's camp to learn by watching.
  2. Karasuno opens the Spring High at Tokyo with a win over Tsubakihara.

To the Top: Inarizaki Ep 74–85

  1. Atsumu and Osamu Miya copy Karasuno's freak quick.
  2. Karasuno beats Inarizaki in a tight third set.

The Dumpster Battle Film

  2. Hinata pushes through the pressure, and Kenma admits volleyball was fun.
