Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

The Shattered Jewel Ep 1–12

  1. Kagome accidentally shatters the Shikon Jewel with an arrow, scattering shards across the land.
  2. Inuyasha's elder half-brother Sesshomaru fights him over their father's sword, Tessaiga.
  3. The fox-demon child Shippo joins the group after the Thunder Brothers kill his father.

Kikyo's Revival and Naraku Ep 13–23

  1. Inuyasha's human night under the new moon is revealed.
  2. The witch Urasue resurrects Kikyo, who still believes Inuyasha betrayed her.
  3. Miroku, cursed with a Wind Tunnel in his hand by Naraku, joins the party.
  4. Naraku is revealed to be born from the bandit Onigumo, who coveted Kikyo fifty years ago.

Sango and Naraku's Castle Ep 24–35

  1. Sango learns Naraku massacred her clan and controls her little brother Kohaku.
  2. Sango and her cat-demon Kirara join Inuyasha's group.
  3. Sesshomaru's Tenseiga and Inuyasha's Tessaiga are revealed to be twin swords that refuse to fight each other.

Koga and Kagura Ep 36–44

  1. Koga declares Kagome his woman, sparking a rivalry with Inuyasha.
  2. Naraku's incarnations Kagura and Kanna make their debut.
  3. The demon Goshinki snaps Tessaiga, and Inuyasha transforms into a full demon.
  4. The rogue swordsmith Kaijinbo forges an evil sword for Sesshomaru from Goshinki's fangs.

Tokijin and Ryukotsusei Ep 45–54

  1. Sesshomaru masters the demonic sword Tokijin.
  2. Kohaku's memories are erased, keeping him as Naraku's puppet.
  3. Inuyasha faces Ryukotsusei, the dragon who sealed his father, and learns the Backlash Wave.

Tsubaki and Naraku's Barrier Ep 55–74

  1. The dark priestess Tsubaki curses Kagome with a shikigami spell.
  2. Kagura considers rebelling against Naraku to win her freedom.
  3. Muso, a demon holding Onigumo's discarded human heart, resurfaces.
  4. Tessaiga gains the red barrier-breaking form by absorbing a bat demon's power.

Panther Demon Tribe Ep 75–79

  1. The Panther Demon Tribe's Four Generals target both Sesshomaru and Inuyasha.
  2. The brothers end up fighting a common enemy to stop the panther lord's revival.

Naraku's Whereabouts and Ayame Ep 80–87

  1. Kagura and Kanna kidnap Rin to bait Sesshomaru.
  2. Ayame of the northern wolf tribe chases Koga to collect his marriage promise.
  3. Kikyo continues her lonely journey hunting Naraku.

Filler Stretch and the Jewel Maker Ep 88–101

  1. Kagome's brother Sota confesses to a girl at school.
  2. The group investigates the legend of the Shikon Jewel's creation.
  3. Kikyo and Kagome are trapped together in a cave.

Band of Seven and Mount Hakurei Ep 102–122

  1. The Band of Seven are resurrected and attack the wolf tribe and Inuyasha's group.
  2. Miroku is poisoned by Mukotsu and Inuyasha fights the mechanical Ginkotsu.
  3. Inuyasha kills Jakotsu, and Bankotsu falls after absorbing his comrades' shards.

Naraku Reborn Ep 123–127

  1. Naraku is reborn and strikes Kikyo down with his miasma.
  2. Inuyasha grieves Kikyo, believing her gone.
  3. Kagome battles her own jealousy and resolves to stay by Inuyasha's side.

Hakudoshi and Entei Ep 128–142

  1. The group fights dried-up demons at Kagome's school festival.
  2. Miroku's master Mushin's past and the monk's own reckless confession are explored.
  3. Hakudoshi tames the fire-horse demon Entei.

The Father's Grave and Princess Abi Ep 143–148

  1. The group travels to the Inu no Taisho's resting place in the afterlife border.
  2. The jewel-keeping demon Hosenki and his diamond pearl hold a key to a new Tessaiga technique.
  3. Princess Abi's bird demons harvest human blood for Naraku.

The Saint and Moryomaru Ep 149–159

  1. Tessaiga learns the Adamant Barrage by absorbing Hosenki's diamond power.
  2. Sesshomaru and Inuyasha clash again at their father's grave.
  3. Kohaku chooses to stay with Kikyo and aid her hunt for Naraku.

End of the Original Series Ep 160–167

  1. Miroku's past philandering comes back to haunt him.
  2. Shippo is possessed by a parasite.
  3. Inuyasha and Kikyo cooperate, using a jewel shard as bait against Naraku.

Naraku's Heart Ep 168–174

  1. Kagura is freed of Naraku's grip and dies peacefully with Sesshomaru beside her.
  2. Inuyasha learns Dragon-Scale Tessaiga to cut demonic auras.
  3. Sesshomaru's own sword Bakusaiga awakens, and Moryomaru is destroyed and reabsorbed by Naraku.

Meido and Kanna's End Ep 175–181

  1. Sesshomaru enters the underworld to save Rin, learning the limits of Tenseiga.
  2. Tenseiga's Meido Zangetsuha is passed on to Inuyasha's Tessaiga.
  3. Kikyo is fatally wounded and dies in Inuyasha's arms.
  4. Kanna sacrifices herself in an attempt to kill Kagome.

Magatsuhi Ep 182–187

  1. The jewel's malevolent spirit Magatsuhi possesses Kohaku's body.
  2. Kagome's sealed spiritual power is freed.
  3. Sesshomaru battles Magatsuhi and drives it out of Kohaku.

The Final Battle Ep 188–193

  1. Kohaku's life-sustaining shard ties his survival to Kikyo's last light.
  2. Inuyasha and allies destroy Naraku from within.
  3. Kagome wishes the jewel away forever and is sent back to her own time.
  4. Three years later Kagome returns through the well to stay with Inuyasha.
