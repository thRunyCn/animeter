Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Night Flight Ep 1–6

  1. Nazuna drinks Ko's blood and he resolves to fall in love with her so he can become a vampire.
  2. Ko reconnects with his childhood friend Akira, who learns his secret and wants him back at school.
  3. Nazuna's side business as a sleep 'massage' provider brings an office worker into their night life.

The Vampire Circle Ep 7–10

  1. Ko is ambushed by a group of vampires who want to turn him, but he declares he wants only Nazuna.
  2. He learns a turned human must fall in love within a year of being bitten or face consequences.
  3. Ko helps Kikyo with her human admirer and exposes the voyeur behind photos at Midori's maid cafe.

Detective Anko Ep 11–13

  1. Detective Anko Uguisu appears and kills a starving vampire who attacked Ko's school.
  2. Shaken, Ko questions his goal and takes refuge with the vampire Hatsuka.
  3. Ko returns to Nazuna, who vows to become worthy of him.

Anko's Offensive Ep 14–16

  1. Ko and Nazuna commit to genuinely falling in love so they can stay together.
  2. Anko's attacks force the vampires to reveal their weaknesses.
  3. Ko starts digging into Nazuna's forgotten past, leading him toward a hospital's room 307.

Kabura's Past Ep 17–18

  1. Kabura is shown as a sickly young woman changed by a mysterious nurse.
  2. Kabura's story reveals the truth of how Nazuna was born.

Searching for Nazuna's Human Ties Ep 19–21

  1. An Akihabara outing brings Midori's backstory to the fore.
  2. A visit to a night school puts Nazuna face to face with a former acquaintance.
  3. Flashbacks reveal Anko is Nazuna's old friend Kyoko Mejiro and explain her hatred of vampires.

Halloween Night Ep 22–25

  1. Anko's open challenge sends Nazuna and the others into a tense chase across the city.
  2. Ko briefly awakens vampire abilities and survives the confrontation.
  3. Anko begins letting go of the burden she has carried, and Ko and Nazuna's bond deepens in the finale.
