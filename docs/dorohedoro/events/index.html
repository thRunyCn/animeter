Caiman in the Hole Ep 1–6

  1. Caiman bites sorcerers' heads so the man inside his mouth can judge whether they are the culprit.
  2. Caiman is decapitated during a zombie festival but survives.
  3. Caiman and Nikaido meet Dr. Kasukabe, who shows them a magic door.
  4. Caiman recalls that the man in his mouth is named Risu.

Blue Night Ep 7–12

  1. Noi, under a curse, transforms into a reptilian creature.
  2. En forces Nikaido into a contract after learning her secret.
  3. Caiman storms En's mansion to rescue Nikaido.
  4. Nikaido is stabbed, and she and Caiman vow to stay friends whatever comes.

The Cross-Eyes Ep 13–23

  1. The season opens on the Cross-Eyes and on Risu's search for the man who killed him.
  2. En serves a dream-inducing mushroom that exposes everyone's desires, and Ebisu sets out to find her parents.
  3. Caiman and Nikaido's friendship is pushed to a breaking point.
  4. A third season was announced after the finale.
