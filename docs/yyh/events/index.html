Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Spirit Detective Ep 1–13

  1. Yusuke earns his revival through a test set by Koenma.
  2. He recovers three stolen treasures, first meeting Kurama and Hiei.
  3. Yusuke trains under Genkai and defeats the technique thief Rando.

Saint Beasts Ep 14–21

  1. Kuwabara, Kurama and Hiei join Yusuke inside Maze Castle.
  2. Each of the Four Saint Beasts is defeated in turn.
  3. Yusuke defeats Suzaku, the leader, by pouring everything into his Spirit Gun.

Rescue of Yukina Ep 22–25

  1. Yusuke and Kuwabara confront Tarukane's hired guards, the Toguro brothers.
  2. Yusuke defeats the younger Toguro and Yukina is freed.
  3. Hiei is revealed to be watching over Yukina as her secret brother.

Dark Tournament Ep 26–66

  1. Team Urameshi fights its way through the bracket against demon teams.
  2. Genkai is killed by the younger Toguro.
  3. Yusuke defeats the younger Toguro in the final and the team wins.

Chapter Black Ep 67–94

  1. Kuwabara is kidnapped and the team learns of Shinobu Sensui's plan.
  2. Sensui shows the Chapter Black tape exposing Spirit World's crimes.
  3. Yusuke is killed by Sensui and revived as a demon descendant of Raizen.
  4. Raizen's power takes over Yusuke's body and defeats Sensui.

Three Kings Ep 95–112

  1. Yusuke serves the starving Raizen, while Hiei joins Mukuro and Kurama aids Yomi.
  2. Raizen dies and Yusuke proposes a tournament to decide the Demon World's ruler.
  3. The Demon World tournament is held and Yusuke returns home to Keiko.
