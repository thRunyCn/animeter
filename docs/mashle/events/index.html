Easton Enrollment Arc Ep 1–6

  1. Mash fakes his way through the Easton entrance exam using sheer strength.
  2. He joins Adler House and befriends Lemon, Finn and Lance.
  3. Mash clashes with arrogant students, including the iron mage Silva Armstrong.

Magia Lupus Arc Ep 7–12

  1. Mash takes on Magia Lupus leader Abel Walker and his puppet magic.
  2. Lance and Dot battle the group's other members.
  3. Mash defeats Abel, and his victories catch the Divine Visionaries' attention.

Divine Visionary Candidate Exam Arc Ep 13–24

  1. Divine Visionary Rayne Ames, Finn's older brother, tests Mash.
  2. Mash and his friends advance through the exam's dangerous stages.
  3. Innocent Zero's agents strike, and Headmaster Wahlberg Baigan steps in to fight them.
