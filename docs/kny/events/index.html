Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Final Selection Ep 1–5

  1. Giyu Tomioka spares Nezuko after seeing her protect Tanjiro.
  2. Tanjiro trains under Sakonji Urokodaki with help from the spirits of Sabito and Makomo.
  3. Tanjiro survives Final Selection and kills the Hand Demon that killed Urokodaki's pupils.

First Mission & Asakusa Ep 6–10

  1. Tanjiro comes face to face with Muzan Kibutsuji in Asakusa.
  2. He meets the demon doctor Tamayo and her assistant Yushiro, who agree to work on a cure.
  3. Tanjiro and Nezuko defeat the Temari and Arrow demons sent by Muzan.

Tsuzumi Mansion Ep 11–14

  1. Zenitsu Agatsuma reveals his skill only while asleep.
  2. Tanjiro defeats the former Lower Moon Kyogai.
  3. Inosuke Hashibira, a boar-masked fighter, joins the group.

Mount Natagumo Ep 15–21

  1. Tanjiro unlocks Hinokami Kagura against Lower Moon Five, Rui.
  2. Nezuko uses her Blood Demon Art to save Tanjiro.
  3. Hashira Giyu and Shinobu arrive, and Giyu kills Rui.

Rehabilitation Training Ep 22–26

  1. The Hashira meet and Sanemi stabs Nezuko to test her, but she refuses to bite.
  2. The trio trains with Kanao at the Butterfly Mansion and learns Total Concentration Constant.
  3. Muzan dissolves the Lower Moons, keeping only Enmu.

Mugen Train Ep 27–33

  1. Lower Moon One Enmu traps everyone in dream worlds.
  2. The group defeats Enmu, who had fused with the train.
  3. Upper Moon Three Akaza appears and Rengoku dies fighting him until dawn.

Entertainment District Ep 34–44

  1. The trio goes undercover as courtesans and boys of the district.
  2. Upper Moon Six turns out to be a pair of siblings, Daki and Gyutaro.
  3. Tanjiro, Zenitsu, Inosuke and Uzui behead both siblings at once.
  4. Uzui retires after losing an eye and a hand.

Swordsmith Village Ep 45–55

  1. Upper Moons Four and Five, Hantengu and Gyokko, attack the village.
  2. Muichiro Tokito regains his memories and awakens his Demon Slayer mark.
  3. Nezuko conquers the sun.
  4. Mitsuri Kanroji defends the villagers against Hantengu's strongest form.

Hashira Training Ep 56–63

  1. Tanjiro trains under multiple Hashira.
  2. Gyomei Himejima reveals his strength.
  3. Muzan finds the Ubuyashiki estate, and the Hashira fall into the Infinity Castle.

Infinity Castle Part 1: Akaza Returns Film

  1. Shinobu Kocho fights Upper Moon Two Doma.
  2. Zenitsu faces his former senior Kaigaku.
  3. Tanjiro and Giyu fight Akaza, and Akaza's tragic past is revealed.
