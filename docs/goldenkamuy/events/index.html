Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Sugimoto and Asirpa Ep 1–4

  1. Sugimoto learns the gold's location is encoded in tattoos on 24 escaped Abashiri prisoners.
  2. Asirpa saves Sugimoto from a bear and agrees to partner with him to find out what happened to her father.
  3. The escape artist Shiraishi becomes the pair's reluctant ally.
  4. Sugimoto is captured and tortured by Lieutenant Tsurumi of the 7th Division, then escapes.

Hunters and Killers Ep 5–10

  1. The legendary hunter-convict Nihei hunts the white wolf Retar and dies after the fight.
  2. Henmi, a killer who craves being killed, is defeated by Sugimoto.
  3. Kiroranke reveals that Asirpa's father is the masked prisoner Nopperabo held in Abashiri.

Murder Hotel and the Horse Race Ep 11–12

  1. The hotel's owner Ienaga is a murderous tattooed convict, and the hotel goes up in flames.
  2. Ogata switches sides and joins Hijikata's faction.

Edogai's Fakes and the 7th Division Ep 13–17

  1. Edogai makes counterfeit skins for Tsurumi and dies in an explosion.
  2. Shiraishi is captured by the 7th Division, and Hijikata's group plots a disguised rescue.
  3. Sugimoto's group escapes Asahikawa on a hijacked airship.

Road to Abashiri Ep 18–21

  1. Tanigaki learns the truth behind his sister's death and his vendetta against a fellow soldier.
  2. Inkarmat reveals what she knows about Asirpa's father.
  3. A band of blind bandits ambushes the group at a hot spring.

Abashiri Prison Ep 22–24

  1. Tsurumi's forces storm the prison in the middle of the infiltration.
  2. Nopperabo is revealed to be Wilk, Asirpa's father, and is shot dead by Ogata.
  3. Sugimoto is shot in the head and left behind as Kiroranke escapes with Asirpa.

Into Karafuto Ep 25–28

  1. Sugimoto's team fights the convict Gansoku in a Stenka brawl and copies his tattoo.
  2. Tsukishima recalls how Tsurumi manipulated him through the Igogusa incident.
  3. Sugimoto stages a circus act to get word to Asirpa.

Across the Border Ep 29–33

  1. Kiroranke's group crosses into Russia disguised with the Uilta and survives a sniper duel.
  2. Hijikata tracks down and kills the aging assassin Yoichiro in Nemuro.
  3. Flashbacks reveal how Kiroranke, Wilk and Sofia fled to Vladivostok and how Tsurumi was involved.

Ako Prison Break and the Ice Floe Ep 34–36

  1. Asirpa shoots Ogata in the eye as he tries to manipulate her.
  2. Kiroranke is fatally wounded and dies before answering Asirpa about her father.
  3. Sugimoto and Asirpa reunite and recommit to their partnership.

Return to Hokkaido Ep 37–49

  1. Sugimoto and Asirpa slip out of Karafuto and back to Hokkaido.
  2. Hijikata's and Tsurumi's factions recruit and betray their way to more tattooed skins, including a scheme built around a cinematograph.
  3. The pirate Boutarou and his skin become the focus of a three-way struggle at sea.
  4. Tsurumi pursues his plan to control Asirpa and the gold for his own ends.

Town of Reunions Ep 50–56

  1. A copycat Jack the Ripper stalks Sapporo and draws the tattooed convicts out.
  2. The fight at the Sapporo beer factory leaves the factions scrambling for the final skins.
  3. Asirpa's choices about the gold and her people come to the forefront.

Tokyo and Hakodate Ep 57–62

  1. A detour to Tokyo digs into the history and ambitions behind Tsurumi's plan.
  2. Hijikata's men fight the 7th Division at Goryokaku fort in Hakodate.
  3. The season ends with the final battle moving onto a runaway train, with the last chapters announced for Winter 2027.
