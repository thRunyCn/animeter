Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Land of Waves Ep 1–19

  1. Naruto learns he carries the Nine-Tailed Fox sealed inside him.
  2. Team 7 faces the rogue ninja Zabuza and his partner Haku.
  3. Sasuke awakens his Sharingan while protecting Naruto.
  4. Haku dies shielding Zabuza, and Zabuza falls soon after.

Chūnin Exams Ep 20–67

  1. Orochimaru ambushes Team 7 in the Forest of Death and brands Sasuke with a Curse Mark.
  2. Rock Lee fights Gaara and is badly crippled.
  3. Naruto beats Neji in the finals and challenges his fatalism.

Konoha Crush Ep 68–80

  1. The Third Hokage dies sealing away Orochimaru's arms.
  2. Naruto defeats the fully transformed Gaara and reaches him emotionally.
  3. Jiraiya takes Naruto under his wing.

Search for Tsunade Ep 81–101

  1. Itachi and Kisame of the Akatsuki come for Naruto, and Itachi breaks Sasuke in a one-sided fight.
  2. Naruto masters the Rasengan.
  3. Tsunade defeats Orochimaru's bid to heal his arms and becomes the Fifth Hokage.

Land of Tea Escort Mission Ep 102–106

  1. Naruto, Sasuke and Sakura protect their client Idate during the Wasabi–Wagarashi race.
  2. The team clashes with hired ninja from the rival family.

Sasuke Recovery Mission Ep 107–135

  1. The Sound Four escort Sasuke out of the village.
  2. Each genin pursuer is pinned down in a separate battle, with the Sand Siblings arriving to help.
  3. Naruto and Sasuke fight at the Valley of the End, and Sasuke leaves Naruto behind.

Anime-original missions Ep 136–220

  1. Missions include the Bikōchū search, the Kaima capture and the Yakumo Kurama rescue.
  2. Naruto finally leaves the village to train with Jiraiya in the closing episode.

Kazekage Rescue Mission Ep 221–252

  1. Deidara captures Gaara and the Akatsuki extract his One-Tail.
  2. Sakura and Granny Chiyo defeat Sasori.
  3. Chiyo gives her life to revive Gaara.

Tenchi Bridge Reconnaissance Mission Ep 253–273

  1. Sasori's spy turns out to be Kabuto.
  2. Naruto's four-tailed form badly injures Sakura.
  3. Naruto briefly reunites with Sasuke at Orochimaru's hideout.

Twelve Guardian Ninja Ep 274–291

  1. Former Guardian Ninja Kazuma raids the Fire Temple.
  2. Sora, a boy with Nine-Tails chakra, joins Naruto's group.

Akatsuki Suppression Mission Ep 292–308

  1. Asuma is killed by Hidan.
  2. Shikamaru avenges his teacher by burying Hidan alive.
  3. Naruto debuts the Rasenshuriken against Kakuzu.

Three-Tails' Appearance Ep 309–332

  1. Leaf teams and Kabuto's forces compete for the Three-Tails.
  2. Tobi and Deidara end up capturing it.

Itachi Pursuit Mission Ep 333–346

  1. Sasuke kills Orochimaru and forms Team Hebi.
  2. Deidara dies in his self-destruct against Sasuke.
  3. A two-episode side story shows Kakashi's past with Obito and Rin.

Tale of Jiraiya the Gallant Ep 347–353

  1. Jiraiya discovers Pain is his former student Nagato's creation.
  2. Jiraiya dies, leaving a coded message for Naruto.

Fated Battle Between Brothers Ep 354–363

  1. Itachi dies after the duel with Sasuke.
  2. Tobi reveals the truth behind the Uchiha massacre.
  3. Sasuke turns his hatred toward the Leaf itself.

Six-Tails Unleashed Ep 364–371

  1. Naruto befriends Utakata and his apprentice Hotaru.
  2. Pain captures Utakata and the Six-Tails.

Pain's Assault Ep 372–395

  1. Kakashi falls during the invasion and Hinata confesses to Naruto.
  2. Pain flattens the village.
  3. Naruto defeats Pain and persuades Nagato, who revives the dead before dying.

Past Arc: The Locus of Konoha Ep 396–416

  1. Episodes retell Naruto's academy days and early friendships.
  2. A 'Big Adventure' story follows Naruto chasing Jiraiya's legacy.

Five Kage Summit Ep 417–434

  1. Danzō is named acting Hokage and dies fighting Sasuke.
  2. Tobi declares the Fourth Great Ninja War.
  3. Sasuke takes Itachi's eyes for the Eternal Mangekyō.

Fourth Shinobi World War: Countdown Ep 435–442

  1. Sakura's attempt to confront Sasuke ends badly and Naruto takes back the burden.
  2. Naruto meets Killer Bee, the Eight-Tails host.

Paradise Life on a Boat Ep 443–462

  1. The episodes are a run of standalone stories about Naruto and his allies.
  2. Naruto stays unaware that the war is starting without him.

Nine-Tails Taming Ep 463–476

  1. Naruto faces his own darkness at the Falls of Truth.
  2. He meets his mother Kushina's spirit and wins the tug-of-war with Kurama.
  3. Kabuto uncovers the island and begins reviving dead ninja.

Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation Ep 477–509

  1. The reanimated Seven Swordsmen and fallen Akatsuki return to fight.
  2. Kankurō seals Sasori and Deidara is captured.
  3. The Fourth Kazekage and his son Gaara meet again in battle.

Power Ep 510–515

  1. Naruto, Kakashi and Yamato face artificially revived copies of powerful dead ninja.
  2. The story is anime-only and sits in a break from the war storyline.

Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation (continued) Ep 516–541

  1. Ōnoki and the Kage face the reanimated Mū and the past Kage.
  2. Naruto charges onto the battlefield to protect his allies.

Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax Ep 542–568

  1. Nagato is reanimated against Naruto and Itachi stops him.
  2. Sasuke and Itachi defeat Kabuto and Itachi ends the Reanimation.
  3. Madara Uchiha joins the battlefield.

Kakashi: Shadow of the ANBU Black Ops Ep 569–581

  1. Kakashi works under Minato and later Danzō's shadow.
  2. He watches over a young Itachi as a fellow ANBU member.

Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax (continued) Ep 582–595

  1. The masked man is revealed to be Obito Uchiha.
  2. Sasuke revives the previous Hokage and joins the war.

Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki Ep 596–651

  1. Neji dies protecting Naruto and Hinata.
  2. Naruto pulls the tailed beasts out of Obito.
  3. Madara is revived and Sasuke and Naruto receive the Sage of Six Paths' power.

Jiraiya Shinobi Handbook: The Tale of Naruto the Hero Ep 652–670

  1. Familiar story beats are retold, including Naruto's time training and childhood friendships.
  2. The framing device is Jiraiya's novel about a ninja hero, the source of Naruto's name.

Itachi Shinden: Light and Darkness Ep 671–677

  1. Itachi befriends Shisui, whose eye he later receives.
  2. He carries out the Uchiha massacre on orders and joins the Akatsuki.

Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes Ep 678–699

  1. Kaguya takes over Madara's body.
  2. Team 7 seals Kaguya with the help of Obito and Kakashi.
  3. Naruto and Sasuke fight their final battle and lose an arm each.

Nostalgic Days and epilogue novels Ep 700–720

  1. Episodes 700–703 are childhood filler.
  2. Sasuke Shinden and Shikamaru Hiden follow Sasuke's journey and Shikamaru's mission in a dark land.
  3. Konoha Hiden ends the series with Naruto and Hinata's wedding.
