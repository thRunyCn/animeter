Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

A Perfect Day for Bananafish Ep 1–4

  1. A dying man hands Ash a vial and an address.
  2. Eiji and Skip are kidnapped, and Skip is killed.
  3. Ash is framed for murder and jailed, and his brother Griffin is shot dead.

Cape Cod to Los Angeles Ep 5–8

  1. Max's ex-wife Jennifer is fatally shot in Cape Cod.
  2. The group uncovers that Banana Fish is a mind-control drug.
  3. Yut-Lung Lee forces Shorter to help kidnap Eiji.

Golzine's Mansion Ep 9–10

  1. Drugged with Banana Fish, Shorter is ordered to kill Eiji, and Ash kills him at his request.
  2. Freed by Yut-Lung, Ash rescues the group and kills Abraham Dawson.

War on the Corsicans Ep 11–13

  1. Ash drains about $90 million from Corsican front companies.
  2. He uncovers a government plan to use the drug overseas.
  3. Ash defeats Arthur in a duel but is gravely wounded and arrested.

The Garden of Eden Ep 14–16

  1. Ash's death is faked as the facility tests Banana Fish on humans.
  2. Eiji escapes to Sing.
  3. Ash breaks out and returns to New York to find Eiji.

Golzine's Heir Ep 17–21

  1. Golzine wipes out the Lee syndicate and hires the assassin Blanca.
  2. Ash returns to Golzine to save Eiji and is set to be adopted.
  3. Eiji, Sing and Cain rescue Ash, but Foxx's mercenaries force Ash to surrender.

The Catcher in the Rye Ep 22–24

  1. Eiji is shot on Yut-Lung's orders but survives.
  2. Foxx shoots Golzine, and both die in the final battle.
  3. Eiji returns to Japan, and Ash dies at the New York Public Library after being stabbed by Lao.
