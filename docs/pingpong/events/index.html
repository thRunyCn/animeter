Peco and Smile Ep 1–2

  1. Peco is easily beaten by Kong Wenge, a Chinese player who failed to make his national team.
  2. Coach Koizumi pushes Smile to play seriously.

First Inter-High Preliminaries Ep 3–4

  1. Smile holds back and loses to Kong.
  2. Peco is beaten by his old friend Akuma, and Dragon wins the tournament.

Falling and Rising Ep 5–7

  1. Peco quits table tennis while Akuma is expelled from Kaio after an assault.
  2. Akuma urges Peco to return, and Peco trains with veteran Obaba.
  3. Smile rises in reputation under Koizumi's coaching.

Second Inter-High Ep 8–11

  1. Peco defeats Kong, who stays in Japan.
  2. Peco plays through a knee injury and defeats Dragon.
  3. Peco and Smile face each other in the final, and years later their careers have diverged.
