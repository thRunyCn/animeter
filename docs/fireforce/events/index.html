Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Introduction Arc Ep 1–4

  1. Shinra, nicknamed the Devil's Footprints for his fire-powered feet, joins Company 8.
  2. He befriends fellow rookie Arthur Boyle and Sister Iris.
  3. Shinra competes in the Rookie Fire Soldier Games, which are attacked by a mysterious man.

VS. Special Fire Force Company 5 Ep 5–6

  1. Shinra defeats Hibana in a one-on-one fight.
  2. Hibana and Sister Iris are revealed to have grown up together.
  3. Hibana becomes an ally of Company 8.

VS. Special Fire Force Company 1 Ep 7–9

  1. Shinra meets Company 1's ninja Tamaki Kotatsu.
  2. Lieutenant Rekka Hoshimiya is revealed to be creating artificial Infernals.
  3. Rekka is shown to be working for the Evangelist and the White-Clad.

Preacher Pursuit Arc Ep 10–11

  1. Company 8 tracks the White-Clad's activities across Tokyo.
  2. Shinra learns more about the Adolla Burst and its link to his own flames.

Asakusa Arc Ep 12–14

  1. The White-Clad's agents attack Asakusa.
  2. Benimaru shows his overwhelming power in driving them off.

Vulcan's Workshop Arc Ep 15–18

  1. Vulcan's assistant Lisa is revealed to be a White-Clad spy.
  2. Vulcan joins Company 8 as its engineer.

Netherworld Arc Ep 19–24

  1. Company 8 battles the White-Clad's elite in the underground tunnels.
  2. Shinra fights his brother Shō, now a White-Clad commander.
  3. Shinra's power grows as he pushes past his limits against Shō.

VS. Special Fire Force Company 4 Ep 25–26

  1. Shinra works on controlling his powers more precisely.
  2. Company 8 deals with tensions involving Company 4.

Fifth Pillar Arc Ep 27–30

  1. The Fifth Pillar Inca Kasugatani is found.
  2. Inca chooses to side with the White-Clad.

Chinese Peninsula Arc Ep 31–34

  1. The team searches the wasteland for clues about the Great Cataclysm.
  2. They learn the world outside Tokyo is not as empty as they were told.

VS. Holy Sol Temple Arc Ep 35–37

  1. The church's ties to the White-Clad are investigated.
  2. Company 8 clashes with forces linked to the temple.

Haijima Industries Arc Ep 38–41

  1. Haijima's interest in the Pillars comes to light.
  2. Shinra fights to keep more Pillars out of enemy hands.

Iris Arc Ep 42

  1. Secrets about Iris and the Pillars surface.

Joint Investigation Arc Ep 43–46

  1. The White-Clad strike during the joint operation.
  2. The battle brings the Pillars' secrets closer to light.

Stigma Arc Ep 47–48

  1. Shinra grows stronger through his Adolla Link.
  2. Season 2 ends with the White-Clad moving toward a new Great Cataclysm.

Ōbi's Rescue Arc Ep 49–55

  1. Company 8 is framed and scattered.
  2. The team breaks into the prison holding Ōbi.
  3. Company 8 reunites with its captain.

Return to St. Raffles and Arthur's Adventure Ep 56–57

  1. Shinra returns to his old orphanage.
  2. Arthur heads off on a journey of chivalry.

Stone Pillars Arc Ep 58–61

  1. Company 8 races to protect the last Pillars.
  2. The Evangelist's forces gather what they need for a new Great Cataclysm.

Asakusa Showdown and Kusakabe History Ep 62–63

  1. Benimaru and Company 7 defend Asakusa.
  2. The truth about the Kusakabe family and Shinra's mother is revealed.

Final Pillar and Battle of Amaterasu Ep 64–66

  1. The last Pillar is claimed.
  2. Forces clash over Amaterasu, Tokyo's power source.

Great Cataclysm Ep 67–73

  1. The Great Cataclysm turns the world into a living nightmare.
  2. Shinra takes on a godlike role to stop the Evangelist.
  3. Shinra remakes the world, setting up a connection to Soul Eater.
