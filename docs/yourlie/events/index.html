Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Monotone/Colorful Ep 1–5

  1. Kaori stuns the judges with an unconventional performance at a violin competition.
  2. She drafts Kosei as her accompanist for the second round.
  3. Kosei falters and then recovers during their duet, and Kaori faints after the ovation.

Piano Competition Ep 6–10

  1. Rivals Takeshi Aiza and Emi Igawa, both shaped by Kosei's playing, perform before him.
  2. Kosei breaks down mid-performance as memories of his mother return.
  3. He recovers by playing for Kaori but does not advance.

Gala Concert Ep 11–13

  1. Hiroko offers to teach Kosei.
  2. Kosei nearly drowns in a pool and is rescued by Watari.
  3. Kaori fails to show at the gala, so Kosei plays alone and makes peace with his mother's memory.

Nagi and the School Festival Ep 14–18

  1. Takeshi's sister Nagi becomes Kosei's student.
  2. Kaori collapses and makes a shocking proposal to Kosei.
  3. Kosei and Nagi perform at the school festival, rekindling Kaori's love of music.

Spring Wind Ep 19–22

  1. Takeshi lets go of his rivalry after his own performance.
  2. Tsubaki confesses to Kosei.
  3. Kosei plays in the finals as Kaori's surgery fails, and her letter reveals her feelings.
