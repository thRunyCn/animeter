Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Operation Strix Ep 1–5

  1. Twilight becomes Loid Forger and adopts Anya, who can secretly read minds.
  2. Loid marries Yor, who is secretly the assassin Thorn Princess.
  3. The family passes Eden Academy's interview, and Anya is admitted.

Eden Academy Life Ep 6–12

  1. Anya punches Damian on her first day and gets a Tonitrus Bolt.
  2. Yor's brother Yuri, secretly a State Security officer, visits the Forgers.
  3. Anya earns her first Stella star by saving a boy from drowning.

Project Apple Ep 13–15

  1. Anya meets Bond, a dog who can see the future.
  2. Anya and Bond help foil the bombing.
  3. Bond joins the Forger family.

Family Missions and the Campbelldon Ep 16–25

  1. Loid's colleague Nightfall tries to replace Yor as his wife.
  2. Loid and Nightfall enter an underground tennis tournament for secret documents.
  3. Loid finally meets Donovan Desmond in person.

Everyday Missions Ep 26–29

  1. Bond's visions help the family avoid disasters.
  2. Damian joins a school field trip.

Cruise Ship Ep 30–34

  1. Yor guards a mother and baby targeted by assassins.
  2. Yor fights off waves of killers while hiding it from Loid.
  3. Anya's mind-reading helps Loid find and defuse a bomb on board.

Vacation's End and Berlint Life Ep 35–38

  1. Anya brags about her vacation at school.
  2. Members of the family's circle get caught up in comic misunderstandings.

Twilight's Memories Ep 39–40

  1. Twilight's childhood during the war is revealed.
  2. The story explains why he became a spy who wants a world where children don't cry.

Eden Busjacking Ep 41–46

  1. Yor tries to make friends among the other mothers.
  2. Anya and Damian are among the students taken hostage on a hijacked school bus.
  3. Loid and the Westalis spies race to stop the busjackers.

Season 3 Finale Ep 47–50

  1. Anya's school life brings fresh complications.
  2. A tense mission plays out in the sewers of Berlint.
