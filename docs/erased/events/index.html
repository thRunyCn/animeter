Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Revival Ep 1

  1. Satoru saves a boy from a truck using Revival.
  2. His mother is murdered and he is framed, then sent back to 1988 when he was eleven.

1988: Saving Kayo Ep 2–4

  1. Satoru notices Kayo's bruises and invites her to his birthday party.
  2. He uncovers the abuse Kayo suffers from her mother.
  3. The danger date passes, but Kayo still disappears.

Fugitive in 2006 Ep 5–6

  1. Coworker Airi shelters Satoru and is caught in an arson fire.
  2. Satoru rescues Airi and gets help from journalist Sawada.
  3. He is arrested after spotting the real killer.

Second Revival Ep 7–9

  1. Satoru hides Kayo in an old bus with her consent.
  2. Kayo stays with Satoru's family as they confront her mother.
  3. Kayo goes to live with her grandmother and Satoru turns to the other potential victims.

The Killer Ep 10

  1. Another classmate, Misato, goes missing.
  2. Teacher Gaku Yashiro reveals himself as the killer and drives Satoru into a frozen lake.

Future Ep 11–12

  1. Satoru wakes in 2003 after fifteen years in a coma.
  2. Yashiro's rooftop plot fails and he is arrested.
  3. A 2010 epilogue shows Satoru reunited with Airi.
