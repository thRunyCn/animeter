Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

The Service Club Ep 1–3

  1. Hachiman is made to join Yukino in the Service Club, which helps students who come with requests.
  2. The cheerful Yui Yuigahama comes asking for help baking cookies and ends up joining the club.
  3. The club takes on requests from the chuunibyou writer Zaimokuza and the tennis player Totsuka.

Classmates' Requests Ep 4–6

  1. The club deals with a chain-message problem in Hayama's friend group.
  2. They help Saki Kawasaki, who has been coming home late from a secret part-time job.
  3. It comes out that Hachiman was hurt in a traffic accident saving Yui's dog, which links him to both girls.

Summer Break Ep 7–9

  1. At the camp, the club tries to help Rumi, an isolated girl being ignored by her group.
  2. Hachiman's harsh plan breaks up the group bullying Rumi.
  3. Yukino's older sister Haruno, who seems to see right through everyone, appears.

The Cultural Festival Ep 10–12

  1. Sagami's poor leadership leaves Yukino carrying the committee alone until she collapses from overwork.
  2. When Sagami runs away during the festival's closing, Hachiman finds her and harshly insults her so others will comfort her.
  3. Hachiman becomes the school's most hated student, which he accepts.

The Sports Festival Ep 13

  1. The club helps plan the sports festival events with Sagami.
  2. Hachiman uses underhanded tactics to win the main event.

The School Trip Ep 14–15

  1. Ebina quietly asks Hachiman to stop the confession.
  2. Hachiman makes a fake confession to Ebina himself to stop Tobe, hurting Yukino and Yui.

The Student Council Election Ep 16–17

  1. Yukino decides to run for president herself, and Yui follows her.
  2. Hachiman instead persuades Iroha to run and win, keeping the club together but driving a wedge between its members.

The Christmas Event Ep 18–22

  1. The joint planning meetings go nowhere in endless buzzword-filled discussions.
  2. Hachiman breaks down and says he wants something 'genuine' with Yukino and Yui.
  3. The Service Club works together again and saves the Christmas event.

Hayama's Path Ep 23–26

  1. The club visits a theme park, where Iroha confesses to Hayama and is turned down.
  2. Hayama keeps his future plans secret amid rumors tied to Yukino.
  3. Yukino begins trying to stand on her own instead of depending on others.

The Prom Ep 27–33

  1. Yukino asks the club to let her settle a matter on her own.
  2. Yukino and Iroha's prom plan faces opposition from parents led by Yukino's mother.
  3. Hachiman pushes a rival 'dummy' prom plan to make the real one look reasonable.
  4. The prom is held successfully.

A Genuine Answer Ep 34–38

  1. The Service Club's competition ends, and Yukino and Hachiman try to part ways.
  2. Hachiman tells Yukino he wants to stay involved in her life, and she accepts.
  3. Yui brings a new request to the club, keeping their relationship going.
