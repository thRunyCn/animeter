Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Double Dungeon Ep 1–2

  1. The raid party finds a temple of statues that kill anyone who breaks its commandments.
  2. Jinwoo stays behind so the others can escape and is nearly killed.
  3. Jinwoo wakes in hospital with a mysterious System that only he can see.

Becoming the Player Ep 3–10

  1. Jinwoo levels up through daily quests and penalty zones.
  2. Other hunters betray him during raids, and he kills them in self-defense.
  3. Jinwoo hides his rapid growth from the Hunter Association.

Job Change Quest Ep 11–12

  1. Jinwoo faces the knight commander Igris.
  2. Jinwoo earns the hidden Necromancer class and gains the power to raise the dead as shadows.

Red Gate and Demon Castle Ep 13–20

  1. Jinwoo is trapped in a Red Gate with other hunters and fights his way out.
  2. Jinwoo climbs the Demon Castle in search of an elixir for his mother.
  3. Jinwoo is reassessed and revealed to be an S-rank hunter.

Jeju Island Ep 21–25

  1. The S-rank hunters of Korea and Japan plan the raid together.
  2. A newly born Ant King, Beru, overwhelms the raid team.
  3. Jinwoo arrives on the island to confront the Ant King.
