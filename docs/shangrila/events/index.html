Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Welcome to Shangri-La Frontier Ep 1–6

  1. Sunraku runs into the unique monster Lycagon the Nightslayer and is left carrying its curse after a hopeless fight.
  2. Helping the rabbit girl Emul earns him a way into the bunny kingdom of Rabituza.
  3. His old gaming rival Arthur Pencilgon tracks him down with a proposal.

Road to Wethermon Ep 7–14

  1. Sunraku, Pencilgon and fellow pro gamer Oikatzo prepare for the hunt.
  2. Exploring ancient ruins, Sunraku uncovers the tragic story behind Wethermon.
  3. The trio gather gear and items for a fight that has never been won.

Wethermon the Tombguard Ep 15–18

  1. Pencilgon and Oikatzo work to crack the Kirin armor while Sunraku duels Wethermon.
  2. Sunraku realizes how to trigger the final phase and parries Wethermon's ultimate attack.
  3. Wethermon falls, the first unique monster ever defeated in the game.

Aftermath and Bilac's Forge Ep 19–25

  1. The trio form a clan with Pencilgon as leader.
  2. Sunraku tries to get a prize repaired and sets about leveling up Emul's sister, the craftsman Bilac.
  3. His Lycagon curse lets him ignore weaker curses as he gathers rare materials.

Reunion and Material Hunting Ep 26–35

  1. Arthur scolds Sunraku for acting alone now that he has become a public figure.
  2. Sunraku defeats a rare gold scorpion to gather materials for Bilac.
  3. A lead surfaces on a chance encounter with one of the game's mythical creatures.

Embrace the Lamplight of Ambition Ep 36–40

  1. The clan SF-Zoo attacks Lycagon's shadow and is brutally wiped out.
  2. Sunraku, Rei Saiga and Akitsu Akane defeat Lycagon's shadow, unlocking a new Unique Scenario.
  3. Sunraku is branded with new curse marks and takes the tarot-based secondary job 'The Fool'.

The Ctarnidd Scenario Ep 41–49

  1. Sunraku's new party fights on a ghost ship, unlocking an upside-down sunken city.
  2. A time limit and environmental hazards make the quest more complex than it first seems.
  3. Sunraku outwits the boss Atlanticus Lepnorca by turning its strengths against it.

The Anonymous A-Team Ep 50

  1. The team plans cosplay disguises and stalling tactics around the match schedule.
  2. A crisis threatens Oikatzo's rematch with pro gamer Sylvia, setting up Season 3.
