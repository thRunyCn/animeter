Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

A Genius on the Ice Ep 1–2

  1. Tsukasa persuades Inori's mother to let him coach her.
  2. Inori passes her preliminary badge test and befriends prodigy Hikaru Kamisaki.
  3. Inori tells Tsukasa she wants to win Olympic gold.

Meikoh Cup Ep 3–6

  1. Inori overcomes her nerves and adds a broken-leg sit spin to her program.
  2. She takes first place, and Hikaru wins the Novice event.
  3. Tsukasa's past with the Kago family and his rival Jun comes to light.

West Japan Championship Ep 7–9

  1. Inori passes her level one badge test but loses her skates just before the championship.
  2. Tsukasa recovers the skates in time as rival skaters post high scores.
  3. Inori wins first place despite a sprained ankle.

Level Six Badge Test Ep 10–13

  1. Tsukasa earns the trust of the tough newcomer Rioh.
  2. At a training camp Inori lands her first double Axel and triple Salchow.
  3. Inori and Rioh both pass level six, and Inori renews her vow to become a gold medalist.

Chubu Block Championship Ep 14–17

  1. Olympian Riina Kojoh shares advice with the young skaters.
  2. Rival Yuna takes a risky jump to lead the field.
  3. Inori scores 81.20 to win Chubu Block gold.

Road to All-Japan Novice Ep 18–22

  1. Jun challenges Tsukasa to help Inori surpass Hikaru.
  2. Inori trains with a harness under Uobuchi and lands a quadruple Salchow.
  3. Tsukasa cracks a rib, unsettling Inori before the trip to Niigata for the competition.
