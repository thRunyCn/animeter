Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Tokyo beginnings Ep 1–5

  1. Kenshin is revealed as the legendary assassin Hitokiri Battosai and defends Kaoru's dojo.
  2. Kenshin frees the boy Yahiko from a yakuza gang and Kaoru takes him in.
  3. Brawler Sanosuke Sagara fights Kenshin and joins him after losing.

Kurogasa Ep 6–7

  1. Udo Jin-e kidnaps Kaoru to force Kenshin to fight as Battosai.
  2. Kenshin defeats Jin-e, who takes his own life.

Megumi and the Oniwabanshu Ep 8–13

  1. Megumi Takani is revealed to have been forced to make opium for Kanryu Takeda.
  2. Kenshin battles the Oniwabanshu and their leader Aoshi Shinomori.
  3. Aoshi's subordinates die protecting him, and Kanryu is defeated.

Yahiko's Battle Ep 14

  1. Yahiko defends Tsubame from former samurai thugs without Kenshin's help.
  2. Yahiko wins the fight and earns Kenshin's respect as a budding swordsman.

Raijuta Ep 15–17

  1. Ishin Raijuta takes Yutaro as a student.
  2. Kenshin defeats Raijuta and exposes him as a hollow fraud.

Nishiki-e Ep 18–19

  1. Tsunan Tsukioka plans a bombing to avenge Captain Sagara.
  2. Kenshin and Sanosuke talk Tsunan out of his revenge.

Act Zero Ep 20–21

  1. Kenshin protects the boy Dankichi and the foreign doctor Elder from criminals targeting them.
  2. Kenshin resumes his wandering and sets off toward Tokyo.

Saito and Okubo Ep 22–24

  1. Hajime Saito returns and nearly kills Sanosuke and Kenshin.
  2. Okubo Toshimichi asks Kenshin to stop Makoto Shishio in Kyoto.
  3. Sojiro Seta assassinates Okubo, and Kenshin leaves Tokyo for Kyoto.

Road to Kyoto Ep 25–30

  1. Kenshin meets the young Oniwabanshu ninja Misao Makimachi.
  2. Kenshin and Saito liberate a village terrorized by Shishio's men and meet Shishio and Seta Sojiro.
  3. Sojiro breaks Kenshin's reverse-blade sword, and Sanosuke meets the monk Anji in the forest.

Arrival in Kyoto Ep 31–34

  1. Kenshin stays with Okina and the Aoiya.
  2. Cho of the Juppongatana steals the last reverse-blade sword made by Arai Shakku.
  3. Kenshin recovers the new sakabato and defeats Cho.

Hiko's Training Ep 35–40

  1. Aoshi allies with Shishio and badly wounds Okina.
  2. Misao takes command of the Oniwabanshu in Kyoto.
  3. Kenshin learns the succession technique Amakakeru Ryu no Hirameki.

Great Kyoto Fire Ep 41–47

  1. Kenshin, Sanosuke and Saito sink Shishio's ironclad Rengoku.
  2. The Oniwabanshu, Kaoru, Yahiko and Hiko defend the Aoiya from the Juppongatana.
  3. Hoji sends challenges that set up a final confrontation at Shishio's hideout.
