Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Takopi Meets Shizuka Ep 1–2

  1. Takopi's Happy Gadgets fail to fix Shizuka's misery, and he uses a time-rewinding camera after she takes her own life.
  2. Takopi keeps rewinding time to protect her, but his naive help has dangerous blind spots.
  3. Escalating tension with bully Marina ends with Marina's death.

The Cover-Up Ep 3–4

  1. Takopi and Naoki conceal Marina's body.
  2. Shizuka manipulates the guilt-ridden Naoki to her own ends.
  3. The cover-up unravels as Naoki breaks down under the strain.

To You in 2022 Ep 5

  1. Takopi's returning memories reveal Marina's abusive home life.
  2. He learns of an earlier timeline in which he planned Shizuka's death at Marina's urging.

Takopi's Original Sin Ep 6

  1. Takopi sacrifices himself to reset time to 2016.
  2. Shizuka, Marina and Naoki get a chance at a better future.
