Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Prince Bojji Ep 1–3

  1. Bojji meets Kage, the last of the Shadow Clan, and the two become friends.
  2. King Bosse dies and the council crowns Bojji's younger half-brother Daida instead of Bojji.

Bojji's Journey and the Underworld Ep 4–10

  1. Bojji's sword teacher Domas, under orders, tries to kill him on the journey.
  2. Bojji ends up in the Underworld, where its king Desha's brother Despa agrees to train him.
  3. Kage's tragic past as a member of the persecuted Shadow Clan is revealed.

The Kingdom in Turmoil Ep 11–15

  1. The mirror spirit Miranjo helps resurrect Bosse inside Daida's body.
  2. Bosse's revival sparks war and division within the kingdom while Bojji races to return.

The Battle Against Ouken Ep 16–23

  1. Ouken, the cursed immortal brother of Despa and Desha, is revealed as the true mastermind behind Miranjo.
  2. Bojji fights using the swordsmanship he learned, and Bosse ultimately turns against Ouken.
  3. After the war, Daida remains king and Bojji leaves to build his own path toward becoming the greatest king.

The Treasure Chest of Courage Ep 24–33

  1. Queen Hilling gets an episode exploring her strength and past.
  2. Ouken's backstory shows how his father Satun cursed him with immortality and how it affected Despa and Desha.
