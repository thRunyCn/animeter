Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Rei and the Kawamoto Sisters Ep 1–5

  1. Akari, Hinata and Momo Kawamoto regularly feed and look after the lonely Rei.
  2. Rei's past as a foster child in the Kōda family and his tense relationship with Kyōko are revealed.
  3. Harunobu Nikaidō proudly declares himself Rei's lifelong rival.

Year-End Matches Ep 6–11

  1. Rei faces the veteran Matsunaga, who is fighting to stay in the pro ranks.
  2. Rei defeats Yasui in a Christmas-season match, aware it costs Yasui's family dearly.
  3. Rei falls ill over New Year and the Kawamoto sisters nurse him back to health.

Shimada and the Lion King Tournament Ep 12–16

  1. Rei joins Shimada's study group together with Nikaidō.
  2. Rei loses to Shimada in an official match and is shaken by how much he still lacks.
  3. Kyōko's visits reopen old wounds from Rei's foster home.

Shimada vs Sōya in Kyoto Ep 17–20

  1. Shimada fights through chronic stomach pain to play the title match.
  2. Sōya defeats Shimada and keeps the Lion King title.
  3. Rei resolves to become strong enough to reach the top himself.

New School Term Ep 21–22

  1. Rei finds a place at school through his teacher Hayashida and a small club.
  2. Rei recommits to fighting in shogi on his own terms.

Hina's Ordeal and the Rookie Tournament Ep 23–35

  1. Hina defends her friend Chiho, who is driven out of school by bullying, and the bullies turn on Hina.
  2. Rei vows to help Hina and throws himself into the Rookie Tournament.
  3. Rei wins the Rookie Tournament, while Nikaidō's chronic illness becomes clear.
  4. The school, with teacher Kokubu stepping in, confronts the bullies and Hina regains her footing.

White Storm: Rei vs Sōya Ep 36–38

  1. Rei plays strongly but one slip lets Sōya win quickly.
  2. A train stoppage leaves Rei and Sōya stranded together overnight, and Rei glimpses Sōya's hearing problem.

Burnt Fields: The Kishō Title Ep 39–40

  1. Yanagihara struggles with age and the weight of his peers' expectations.
  2. Yanagihara defeats Shimada and becomes Eternal Kishō.

Summer to Spring Ep 41–44

  1. Rei helps Hina prepare for her high school entrance exams.
  2. Hina passes and spring arrives with a fresh start for her and Rei.
  3. An extra chapter looks at the Kōda household from another angle.
