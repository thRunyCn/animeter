Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Thors and Iceland Ep 1–6

  1. Thors is revealed to be a deserter from the elite Jomsvikings.
  2. Thors is ambushed and killed by Askeladd's band after defeating them all without a sword.
  3. Thorfinn stows away with Askeladd, vowing to kill him in a duel.

Askeladd's Band and London Bridge Ep 7–12

  1. Thorfinn earns duels by completing dangerous missions but keeps losing.
  2. The giant Thorkell defends London Bridge for the English against the Danes.
  3. Askeladd seizes the young Prince Canute after Thorkell turns on the Danes.

Canute's Journey Ep 13–20

  1. Askeladd recruits help from Wales by playing on his own Welsh lineage.
  2. Thorfinn duels Thorkell, who is delighted to find a worthy opponent.
  3. Canute awakens to his ambition after Ragnar's death and breaks with his priest's teachings.

End of the Prologue Ep 21–24

  1. Askeladd's Welsh ancestry and his true goals are revealed.
  2. Askeladd kills King Sweyn to protect Wales and Canute's path to the throne.
  3. Canute kills Askeladd, and a devastated Thorfinn loses his reason for living.

Ketil's Farm (Slave) Ep 25–34

  1. Thorfinn and Einar strike a deal to earn their freedom by clearing a forest.
  2. Thorfinn is haunted by dreams of those he killed.
  3. Thorfinn vows never to kill again.

Gardar and Arnheid Ep 35–41

  1. Canute is haunted by the decision that made him king.
  2. Gardar comes to the farm to get Arnheid, and Thorfinn and Einar help her escape.
  3. Arnheid and Gardar are punished, and both die.

War at Ketil's Farm Ep 42–48

  1. Canute's army attacks the farm.
  2. Thorfinn takes a hundred punches without fighting back to earn an audience with the king.
  3. Thorfinn convinces Canute to spare the farm.
  4. Thorfinn returns home to Iceland and plans a peaceful land in Vinland.
