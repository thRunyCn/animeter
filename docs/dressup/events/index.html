Key events

Shizuku-tan Ep 1–5

  1. Gojo agrees to make Marin's Shizuku Kuroe cosplay.
  2. Gojo's grandfather collapses and Gojo finishes the outfit early.
  3. Marin debuts the cosplay at her first event, and Gojo compliments her sincerely.

Black Lobelia Ep 6–9

  1. Marin realizes she has fallen for Gojo.
  2. Juju is revealed as Sajuna Inui, and her sister Shinju joins the shoot.
  3. Shinju admits she wants to cosplay a male character.

Veronica and Liz Ep 10–11

  1. Gojo refuses to make the revealing Veronica outfit.
  2. A Liz the Succubus photoshoot takes place at a love hotel and leads to a tense moment.

Summer Festival Ep 12

  1. Marin and Gojo watch the fireworks together.
  2. Marin confesses her love while Gojo is asleep.

Halloween and Amane Ep 13–15

  1. The Arisha bunny-girl outfit debuts at a Halloween party.
  2. Amane Himeno, a man who crossplays, appears at an aquarium event.
  3. Gojo reflects on his feelings for Marin.

Culture Festival Pageant Ep 16–18

  1. Marin chooses to cosplay Rei for the pageant.
  2. Classmates help Gojo adjust the costume.
  3. Marin wins the pageant.

Akira and the Coffin Shoot Ep 19–24

  1. Akira Ogata is revealed to be a woman.
  2. Gojo stays at Marin's after trains are suspended.
  3. Juju's inferiority complex and Akira's backstory come to light.
  4. The group completes the Coffin shoot and Gojo and Marin walk home together.
