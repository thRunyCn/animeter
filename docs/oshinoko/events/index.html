Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Mother and Children Ep 1

  1. Gorou is murdered by a stalker and reborn as Aquamarine, while Sarina is reborn as Ruby.
  2. The twins grow up watching Ai become a top star.
  3. Ai is stabbed to death at her front door by a fan who knew her address.
  4. Aqua swears revenge on their unknown father, whom he believes tipped off the killer.

Sweet Today Ep 2–4

  1. Ruby and Aqua enroll at Youtou High's entertainment course.
  2. Aqua and Kana salvage a poorly made drama with a single strong scene.

Reality Dating Show Ep 5–8

  1. Akane Kurokawa is harassed online after an edited clip makes her look bad.
  2. Aqua stops Akane from attempting suicide and helps clear her name.
  3. Akane copies Ai's personality perfectly, alarming Aqua.

B Komachi Ep 9–11

  1. Kana reluctantly joins Ruby's group.
  2. B Komachi performs at the Japan Idol Festival.

Tokyo Blade Ep 12–22

  1. The play's script is rewritten after the original manga's author objects.
  2. Aqua uses his trauma as an actor to deliver a strong performance.
  3. Aqua learns more about the man he believes is his father.

Private (Miyazaki) Ep 23–24

  1. Ruby discovers Gorou's remains.
  2. Ruby takes up Aqua's quest for revenge.

Scandal Ep 25–28

  1. Ruby shows a darker, more driven side.
  2. Kana is caught in a scandal and Aqua makes a hard choice.

15 Years of Lies Ep 29–35

  1. Casting for the film begins.
  2. Ruby auditions for the role of Ai.
  3. Filming of Ai's story begins.
