Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Raditz Saga Ep 1–6

  1. Raditz reveals that Goku is a Saiyan sent to conquer Earth.
  2. Goku and Piccolo team up against Raditz, and Goku dies holding him in place.
  3. Piccolo takes the young Gohan away to train him for the coming Saiyans.

Vegeta Saga Ep 7–35

  1. Goku trains with King Kai on his tiny planet after crossing Snake Way.
  2. Nappa and Vegeta arrive; a Saibaman kills Yamcha and Nappa kills Tien, Chiaotzu and Piccolo.
  3. Goku returns and, with Gohan and Krillin, drives a badly injured Vegeta off Earth.

Namek Saga Ep 36–67

  1. Frieza's forces are already on Namek hunting the Dragon Balls.
  2. Vegeta turns on Frieza's soldiers and steals Dragon Balls for himself.
  3. Gohan and Krillin win the favour of Guru, who unlocks their hidden power.

Captain Ginyu Saga Ep 68–74

  1. Goku arrives and overpowers most of the Ginyu Force.
  2. Captain Ginyu swaps bodies with Goku, but the trick is undone and he ends up as a frog.
  3. Vegeta finishes off the remaining Ginyu Force members.

Frieza Saga Ep 75–107

  1. Frieza kills Vegeta and then Krillin in front of Goku.
  2. Goku becomes a Super Saiyan for the first time in his rage.
  3. Namek explodes; Goku escapes in a Ginyu Force pod and Frieza is left for dead.

Garlic Jr. Saga Ep 108–117

  1. Garlic Jr. infects the population with the Black Water Mist.
  2. Gohan and Piccolo lead the defence until Garlic Jr. is sealed back in the Dead Zone.

Trunks Saga Ep 118–125

  1. Future Trunks kills Frieza and King Cold as a Super Saiyan.
  2. Trunks secretly warns Goku about androids that will devastate the future.

Androids Saga Ep 126–139

  1. Goku collapses from his heart virus during the fight with Android 19.
  2. Dr. Gero awakens Androids 17 and 18, who easily defeat Vegeta and the others.
  3. A second time machine and a strange husk point to another creature from the future.

Imperfect Cell Saga Ep 140–152

  1. Piccolo fuses with Kami to stand against Cell.
  2. Cell hunts Androids 17 and 18 to absorb them and complete himself.
  3. Cell absorbs Android 17.

Perfect Cell Saga Ep 153–165

  1. Vegeta lets Cell absorb Android 18 out of pride, and Cell reaches his perfect form.
  2. Cell announces the Cell Games, a tournament for the fate of the Earth.

Cell Games Saga Ep 166–194

  1. Goku forfeits mid-fight and names Gohan as the one to beat Cell.
  2. Gohan becomes a Super Saiyan 2 after Android 16 is destroyed.
  3. Goku sacrifices himself to stop Cell's self-destruct, and Gohan kills Cell with a Kamehameha.

Other World Saga Ep 195–199

  1. Goku and Pikkon advance through the Other World Tournament.
  2. The final ends without a clear winner after both fighters touch the ceiling.

Great Saiyaman Saga Ep 200–209

  1. Gohan adopts the Great Saiyaman identity, and Videl uncovers who he is.
  2. Gohan teaches Videl to fly before the World Martial Arts Tournament.
  3. Goku is allowed to return to Earth for one day to enter the tournament.

World Tournament Saga Ep 210–219

  1. Goten and Trunks win the junior division and fight each other.
  2. Spopovich badly beats Videl, and the Supreme Kai reveals himself.
  3. Gohan's energy is stolen for a sinister plan.

Babidi Saga Ep 220–231

  1. Babidi takes control of Vegeta, who wanted to fight Goku again.
  2. Majin Vegeta fights Goku, then sacrifices himself against Buu.
  3. Majin Buu is released from his shell.

Majin Buu Saga Ep 232–253

  1. Goku reveals Super Saiyan 3 to buy time.
  2. Buu kills Babidi and sets out to destroy humanity.
  3. Goten and Trunks learn the Fusion Dance, and Gohan trains with the Z Sword.

Fusion Saga Ep 254–275

  1. Gotenks fights Super Buu inside the Hyperbolic Time Chamber.
  2. An empowered Gohan returns and outclasses Super Buu.
  3. Goku and Vegeta use the Potara earrings to become Vegito.

Kid Buu Saga Ep 276–291

  1. Kid Buu destroys the Earth and is pursued to the Supreme Kai's world.
  2. The Earth is restored with the Namekian Dragon Balls.
  3. Goku finishes Kid Buu with a Spirit Bomb using energy from all of Earth.
  4. Ten years later Goku meets Uub, Buu's reincarnation, and leaves to train him.
