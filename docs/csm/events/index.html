Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Introduction / Bat Devil Ep 1–4

  1. Pochita fuses with a murdered Denji, turning him into the Chainsaw Man.
  2. Denji is paired with Aki Hayakawa and the blood fiend Power.
  3. Denji saves Power and her cat Meowy from the Bat Devil.

Eternity Devil Ep 5–7

  1. Himeno gives Denji his first kiss, and it goes badly.
  2. Denji fights the Eternity Devil for three days straight and wins.
  3. Denji learns the Gun Devil killed Aki's family.

Katana Man Ep 8–12

  1. Himeno dies giving herself to the Ghost Devil to save Aki.
  2. Makima kills the yakuza-backed attackers from far away.
  3. Kishibe trains Denji and Power.
  4. Denji and Aki defeat the Katana Man together.

Reze Arc Film

  1. Reze is revealed to be the Bomb Hybrid, sent to steal Denji's heart.
  2. Denji beats Reze in a brutal fight that wrecks the city.
  3. Reze decides to run away with Denji but is killed by Makima before she can meet him.
