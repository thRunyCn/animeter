Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Rafal Ep 1–3

  1. The heretic Hubert introduces Rafal to heliocentric research.
  2. Hubert is executed, and Rafal comes to believe in the theory.
  3. Rafal is betrayed and chooses to die by poison rather than recant.

Oczy and Gras Ep 4–5

  1. Gras becomes obsessed with the movements of Mars.
  2. Gras dies in a bridge collapse and leaves the research to Oczy.

Badeni and Piast Ep 6–15

  1. Badeni takes up the work despite his own disdain for the world.
  2. Count Piast dies after confirming the Venus observation.
  3. Badeni makes the elliptical-orbit breakthrough.
  4. Inquisitor Nowak captures Oczy and Badeni, and Jolenta is tortured and freed.

Draka and the Heretic Liberation Front Ep 16–23

  1. Draka obtains the book of the theory and burns it after memorizing it.
  2. Jolenta is revealed as the leader of the Heretic Liberation Front and sacrifices herself.
  3. Draka and Schmidt try to print the theory, and Nowak dies in a church fire.

Albert Ep 24–25

  1. Albert speaks to a priest about his former tutor Rafal, who taught him about the sky.
  2. Albert reveals that his tutor killed his father.
  3. Albert decides to pursue astronomy despite his past.
