Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Future Gadget Lab and the D-Mails Ep 1–12

  1. Okabe sees Kurisu Makise apparently murdered, then finds her alive after sending a text that shifts the world.
  2. The lab's members send D-Mails that quietly rewrite their own pasts.
  3. Kurisu helps turn the phone microwave into a time leap machine that sends memories back in time.
  4. SERN agents raid the lab and Mayuri is killed, so Okabe makes his first time leap.

Mayuri's Fate Ep 13–16

  1. Every attempt to save Mayuri fails, as she dies again in a different way each time.
  2. Suzuha Amane reveals she is a time traveler from a future ruled by SERN, and that Daru is her father.
  3. Okabe learns that only returning to the Beta worldline, by cancelling the D-Mails, can change Mayuri's fate.

Undoing the D-Mails Ep 17–20

  1. Faris gives up the change that saved her father so that her message can be undone.
  2. Ruka lets go of the world where he was born a girl.
  3. Okabe confronts Moeka and undoes the D-Mail that let SERN find the lab.

Kurisu or Mayuri Ep 21–22

  1. Okabe realizes that cancelling his very first D-Mail will restore the world where Kurisu was killed.
  2. Kurisu herself urges him to save Mayuri, and Okabe undoes the message, returning to the Beta worldline.

Operation Skuld / Steins Gate Ep 23–24

  1. Suzuha arrives from the future and asks Okabe to save Kurisu to prevent a world war.
  2. Okabe's first rescue attempt fails and he accidentally stabs Kurisu himself.
  3. A video message from his future self guides Okabe to fake Kurisu's death without changing what his past self saw, reaching the Steins Gate worldline.

Egoistic Poriomania Ep 25

  1. Kurisu comes back to Japan and reunites with the lab members, who don't remember her.
  2. She and Okabe meet again, with hints that he still recalls traces of the other worldlines.

Load Region of Déjà Vu Film

  1. Okabe starts to fade out of existence, and the people around him forget him.
  2. Kurisu uses a time machine to travel into the past to restore him.
  3. Kurisu ensures that Okabe stays in the world, and the two reunite.
