Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Tiger and Dragon Ep 1–4

  1. A misdelivered love letter reveals that Taiga likes Ryuji's best friend Yusaku Kitamura.
  2. Ryuji admits he likes Taiga's best friend Minori Kushieda, and the two form an alliance.
  3. Ryuji learns why Minori works so many part-time jobs.

Ami and Summer Ep 5–10

  1. Ami's two-faced personality is exposed, and she drops her act around the group.
  2. Ryuji saves Taiga, who can't swim, at the pool.
  3. The group spends a summer trip at Ami's beach house, where Minori and Kitamura stage fake ghost scares.

The Cultural Festival Ep 11–13

  1. Taiga's father Rikuro reappears and promises to come to the festival.
  2. Rikuro fails to show up, leaving Taiga hurt.
  3. Taiga wins the beauty pageant, and Ryuji and Minori finish the relay together.

The Student Council Election Ep 14–16

  1. Kitamura runs away to Ryuji's home after quitting the student council.
  2. Taiga runs for student council president to help him.
  3. Kitamura confesses to council president Sumire Kano, who leaves to study abroad, and Taiga fights her.

Christmas Ep 17–19

  1. Minori avoids the Christmas party and pulls away from Ryuji.
  2. Taiga realizes on Christmas Eve that she loves Ryuji.
  3. Minori rejects Ryuji's confession.

The Ski Trip Ep 20–22

  1. Taiga falls during a blizzard and is rescued by Ryuji.
  2. A dazed Taiga confesses her feelings for Ryuji without realizing he can hear.
  3. Ryuji starts to realize what Taiga means to him.

Valentine's and Graduation Ep 23–25

  1. Ryuji's mother Yasuko collapses, and Taiga and Minori clash over hiding their feelings.
  2. Ryuji and Taiga confess to each other and decide to run away together.
  3. Taiga leaves to sort out her family, and the two reunite at graduation.
