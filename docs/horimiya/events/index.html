Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Secrets Revealed Ep 1–3

  1. Hori learns the quiet Miyamura has piercings and tattoos, and he learns she runs her household.
  2. Miyamura clears Hori of blame over a lost report.
  3. Miyamura makes friends in class and clashes with Ishikawa over Hori.

Confession Ep 4–6

  1. Miyamura confesses while nursing a sick Hori.
  2. Hori and Miyamura begin dating.
  3. Miyamura cuts his hair and the couple shares a first kiss.

Growing Closer Ep 7–9

  1. Miyamura confronts his younger, lonelier self in a dream.
  2. Miyamura faces his former middle-school bully.

Winter and Graduation Ep 10–13

  1. Ishikawa comforts Yuki as jealousy strains the group.
  2. At Christmas, Hori says she wants to stay with Miyamura after graduation.
  3. The group graduates and plans a trip together.

The Missing Pieces Ep 14–26

  1. Episodes revisit the class trip, sports day and a sleepover.
  2. Side characters like Iura and Yanagi get spotlight episodes.
  3. The season ends again at graduation.
