Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Romance Dawn Ep 1–3

  1. Luffy frees the young Koby from Alvida's crew.
  2. Luffy rescues the bound swordsman Roronoa Zoro from a corrupt Marine captain, and Zoro becomes his first crewmate.
  3. Flashbacks show how Shanks gave Luffy his straw hat and lost an arm saving him.

Orange Town Ep 4–8

  1. Luffy defeats Buggy the Clown, whose Devil Fruit lets him split his body apart.
  2. Nami agrees to travel with Luffy for her own reasons.

Syrup Village Ep 9–18

  1. The butler Klahadore is revealed to be the pirate Captain Kuro planning to murder Kaya.
  2. Luffy defeats Kuro and Usopp joins the crew.
  3. Kaya gifts the crew their ship, the Going Merry.

Baratie Ep 19–30

  1. Zoro challenges Dracule Mihawk and is badly defeated, vowing never to lose again.
  2. Luffy defeats Don Krieg and Sanji joins the crew.
  3. Nami steals the Going Merry and sails off.

Arlong Park Ep 31–45

  1. Nami's past is revealed: Arlong killed her adoptive mother Bell-mère and forced her to draw maps for him.
  2. Nami asks Luffy for help, and he defeats Arlong and wrecks his base.
  3. Luffy receives his first bounty of 30 million berries.

Loguetown Ep 46–53

  1. Zoro acquires the sword Sandai Kitetsu and meets the Marine Tashigi.
  2. Buggy nearly executes Luffy, but a lightning strike saves him.
  3. The Marine Smoker pursues Luffy until the mysterious Dragon intervenes.

Warship Island (filler) Ep 54–61

  1. The crew helps Apis and Grandpa Boss protect a legendary sea dragon.
  2. They clash with a Marine officer seeking the dragon's power.

Reverse Mountain & Whisky Peak Ep 62–67

  1. The crew meets the giant whale Laboon, who is still waiting for his old crew.
  2. Whisky Peak turns out to be a bounty-hunter base of Baroque Works.
  3. The crew agrees to escort Princess Vivi back to Alabasta.

Little Garden Ep 68–77

  1. The crew meets the giants Dorry and Brogy.
  2. Usopp and Luffy beat Mr. 3 and his Baroque Works partners.
  3. Nami falls gravely ill as they leave.

Drum Island Ep 78–91

  1. Luffy carries Nami and Sanji up a mountain to the doctor Kureha.
  2. Chopper's backstory with Dr. Hiriluk is revealed.
  3. Luffy defeats Wapol and Chopper joins the crew.

Arabasta Ep 92–130

  1. Ace appears and briefly travels with the crew.
  2. Crocodile is revealed as Mr. 0 and defeats Luffy twice.
  3. Luffy defeats Crocodile on his third attempt and the rebellion ends.
  4. Vivi stays behind, giving the crew a farewell with the X marks on their arms.

Post-Arabasta, Goat Island & Ruluka Island (filler) Ep 131–143

  1. Each crew member tells a story of their past in the Post-Arabasta episodes.
  2. The crew visits Goat Island and Ruluka Island in standalone anime adventures.

Jaya Ep 144–152

  1. Luffy refuses to fight Bellamy, who mocks dreamers.
  2. Luffy first encounters Blackbeard without realizing it.
  3. Cricket and the Saruyama alliance help the crew ride the Knock Up Stream skyward.

Skypiea Ep 153–195

  1. The crew fights through Enel's priests in the Ordeals.
  2. Enel's lightning power seems unstoppable, but Luffy's rubber body is immune.
  3. Luffy rings the Golden Bell after defeating Enel, proving Noland's story true.

G-8 (filler) Ep 196–206

  1. The crew must escape the base without their ship.
  2. Base commander Jonathan tries to outwit them.

Long Ring Long Land Ep 207–226

  1. Chopper is briefly lost to the Foxy Pirates and then won back.
  2. Luffy beats Foxy in the final round.
  3. Admiral Aokiji appears and freezes Robin and Luffy.

Water 7 Ep 227–263

  1. The Going Merry is declared beyond repair.
  2. Usopp quits after a fight with Luffy.
  3. Robin leaves with CP9, who take her away.
  4. Franky is introduced and drawn into the conflict.

Enies Lobby Ep 264–312

  1. Robin shouts that she wants to live, and Luffy declares war on the World Government.
  2. Luffy debuts Gear Second and Third to defeat Rob Lucci.
  3. The Going Merry carries the crew away one last time before being burned.

Post-Enies Lobby Ep 313–336

  1. Garp is revealed to be Luffy's grandfather and Dragon his father.
  2. Franky builds the Thousand Sunny and joins the crew.
  3. Usopp apologizes and returns.

Thriller Bark Ep 337–381

  1. Gecko Moria steals the crew's shadows.
  2. Brook joins after his search for Laboon's promise is revealed.
  3. Zoro takes on all of Luffy's pain from Kuma to save him.

Sabaody Archipelago Ep 382–405

  1. Luffy punches a Celestial Dragon.
  2. Rayleigh is revealed as Roger's first mate.
  3. Kuma sends each crew member flying to a different island.

Amazon Lily Ep 406–421

  1. Luffy meets Boa Hancock, who falls for him.
  2. Luffy learns Ace is to be executed and sets out to save him.

Impel Down Ep 422–456

  1. Luffy is nearly killed by Magellan's poison and saved by Ivankov.
  2. Luffy forms an alliance of escaped prisoners.
  3. Blackbeard breaks in and frees dangerous inmates.

Marineford Ep 457–489

  1. Luffy frees Ace, but Ace dies protecting him from Akainu.
  2. Whitebeard dies, and Blackbeard steals his power.
  3. Shanks arrives and ends the war.

Post-War Ep 490–516

  1. Jinbe helps Luffy realize he still has his crew.
  2. Luffy sends a coded message to his crew to wait two years.
  3. Luffy trains under Rayleigh.

Fish-Man Island Ep 517–578

  1. The crew shows off their two years of training.
  2. Hody Jones attempts a coup.
  3. Luffy defeats Hody and declares Big Mom his enemy.

Punk Hazard Ep 579–628

  1. Luffy allies with Trafalgar Law to take down Kaido.
  2. Caesar Clown is captured.

Dressrosa Ep 629–746

  1. Sabo returns alive and wins Ace's Flame-Flame Fruit.
  2. Luffy debuts Gear Fourth and defeats Doflamingo.
  3. The Straw Hat Grand Fleet is formed.

Zou Ep 747–782

  1. The Mink Tribe is introduced.
  2. Sanji has been taken to marry into Big Mom's family.
  3. The Road Poneglyphs are explained.

Whole Cake Island Ep 783–877

  1. Sanji's family, the Vinsmokes, is revealed.
  2. Luffy defeats Katakuri with Snakeman.
  3. The crew escapes Big Mom.
  4. Luffy becomes known as the Fifth Emperor.

Levely Ep 878–889

  1. Vivi disappears during the Levely.
  2. Imu appears as a hidden ruler.

Wano Country Ep 890–1085

  1. The Scabbards' backstory with Oden is revealed.
  2. Luffy awakens his Devil Fruit, revealed to be the Human-Human Fruit, Model: Nika.
  3. Luffy defeats Kaido.

Egghead Ep 1086–1155

  1. Vegapunk and his satellites reveal hidden history.
  2. The Five Elders arrive in person.
  3. Kuma's past is revealed.

Elbaph Ep 1156–1179

  1. The crew arrives in Elbaph with the Giant Warrior Pirates.
  2. The Holy Knights of God move to bring Elbaph under the World Government.
