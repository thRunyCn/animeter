Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Jujutsu Kaisen 0 Film

  1. Yuta learns to control Rika's overwhelming curse while training with Maki, Toge and Panda.
  2. The rogue sorcerer Suguru Geto declares the Night Parade of a Hundred Demons to take Rika for himself.
  3. Yuta lifts Rika's curse, and Gojo kills his old best friend Geto.

Introduction / Fearsome Womb Ep 1–5

  1. Yuji becomes Sukuna's vessel and is sentenced to death, with execution postponed until he eats all the fingers.
  2. Yuji joins Megumi Fushiguro and Nobara Kugisaki as first-years at Jujutsu High.
  3. During the cursed-womb mission, Sukuna rips out Yuji's heart and Yuji dies, only to be revived.

Vs. Mahito Ep 6–13

  1. Gojo shows his domain expansion, Unlimited Void, against Jogo.
  2. Yuji befriends Junpei Yoshino, whom Mahito manipulates and then kills.
  3. Yuji and Nanami fight Mahito, whose soul-shaping touch backfires on Yuji because Sukuna shares his body.

Kyoto Goodwill Event Ep 14–21

  1. Yuji wins over Aoi Todo, and they land a series of Black Flashes together.
  2. Curses invade the event, and a special-grade cursed spirit is beaten back.
  3. The cursed user Mahito's group steals Sukuna's fingers and other cursed objects.

Death Painting Ep 22–24

  1. Yuji and Nobara defeat Eso and Kechizu.
  2. Yuji kills for the first time and struggles with it.
  3. Hints emerge of a spy inside Jujutsu High.

Gojo's Past (Hidden Inventory / Premature Death) Ep 25–29

  1. Toji Fushiguro kills Riko and nearly kills Gojo.
  2. Gojo awakens Reverse Cursed Technique and kills Toji.
  3. Geto loses faith in protecting non-sorcerers and leaves the jujutsu world.

Shibuya Incident Ep 30–47

  1. Gojo is sealed in the Prison Realm by 'Geto', revealed to be Kenjaku.
  2. Sukuna takes over Yuji and kills thousands.
  3. Nanami and Nobara are struck down by Mahito.
  4. Kenjaku absorbs Mahito and starts the Culling Game.

Itadori Extermination Ep 48–49

  1. Yuta Okkotsu returns as Yuji's appointed executioner.
  2. Yuta fakes Yuji's death and teams up with him.

Perfect Preparation Ep 50–53

  1. The rules of the Culling Game are explained.
  2. Maki wipes out the Zen'in clan.
  3. Megumi is made head of the Zen'in clan.

Culling Game (Tokyo No. 1 & Sendai Colonies) Ep 54–59

  1. Yuji fights the reincarnated sorcerer Higuruma in the Tokyo No. 1 colony.
  2. Yuta enters the Sendai colony and battles its strongest players.
