These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Two Nanas Ep 1–5

  1. Nana Komatsu and Nana Osaki sit together on a snowbound train.
  2. Nana K.'s relationship with Shoji is shown.
  3. Nana O.'s past with Ren and the band Black Stones is revealed.

Room 707 Ep 6–16

  1. The two move into Room 707 together.
  2. Yasu and Nobu reunite with Nana O., and the young bassist Shin joins the band.
  3. Shoji cheats on Nana K. with his coworker Sachiko.
  4. Blast plays its first live show.

Trapnest Ep 17–30

  1. Nana O. reunites with Ren at a Trapnest concert.
  2. Nana K. starts a relationship with Trapnest's leader Takumi.
  3. Nana K. becomes torn between Takumi and Nobu.

Hachi's Choice Ep 31–36

  1. Nana K. discovers she is pregnant.
  2. She chooses to stay with Takumi, hurting Nobu.
  3. The strawberry glasses the Nanas share are broken.

Blast's Debut Ep 37–45

  1. Blast debuts under a record label.
  2. A tabloid scandal puts Nana O. and Ren in the spotlight.
  3. Ren proposes and Nana O. accepts.

Fireworks Ep 46–47

  1. Nana K. runs into Shoji again.
  2. A flash-forward shows Nana K. and her daughter while Nana O. has vanished.
  3. The two Nanas' fireworks night together is remembered.
