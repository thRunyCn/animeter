Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Liore and the Sewing-Life Alchemist Ep 1–4

  1. Edward becomes the youngest State Alchemist to gain access to research.
  2. The brothers expose Father Cornello's fake miracles in Liore.
  3. Shou Tucker is revealed to have turned his daughter Nina into a chimera.

Scar and Laboratory 5 Ep 5–9

  1. Scar destroys Edward's automail arm and nearly kills the brothers.
  2. Marcoh's notes reveal that Philosopher's Stones are made from human lives.
  3. The brothers fight guards bound to armor inside Laboratory 5.

Hughes, Rush Valley and Dublith Ep 10–14

  1. Maes Hughes is murdered by Envy after uncovering the military's secret.
  2. Izumi reveals she too attempted human transmutation.
  3. The brothers clash with the homunculus Greed, and King Bradley's forces wipe out his gang.

Visitors from Xing and Lust's End Ep 15–19

  1. Ling Yao and May Chang reach Amestris.
  2. Lieutenant Ross is framed for Hughes's death and Mustang fakes her execution.
  3. Mustang burns Lust to death beneath Central.

Hunting Scar and Inside Gluttony Ep 20–27

  1. The brothers learn the body they made was not their mother.
  2. Winry learns Scar killed her parents.
  3. Edward, Ling and Envy are swallowed by Gluttony and escape through a Gate.
  4. Episode 27 is a recap-style interlude centered on Hohenheim.

Father and the Ishval War Ep 28–32

  1. Ling is turned into the new host for Greed.
  2. Bradley is revealed as the homunculus Wrath.
  3. Flashbacks show the Ishval war and the alchemists who fought it.

Fort Briggs Ep 33–41

  1. Selim Bradley is revealed to be Pride, the first homunculus.
  2. The tunnels under Amestris prove to be a nationwide transmutation circle.
  3. Kimblee leads a hunt for Scar and the escaped Elric party.

Preparing the Counterattack Ep 42–48

  1. Edward knocks out the lights to defeat Pride's shadows.
  2. Greed regains his own memories and rebels.
  3. Pride devours Gluttony to recover his powers.

The Promised Day Ep 49–58

  1. Mustang takes revenge on Envy for Hughes.
  2. Izumi, Armstrong and Sig defeat Sloth.
  3. Bradley returns to Central and fights through the capital.

Final Battle Ep 59–64

  1. Mustang is forced into human transmutation and loses his sight.
  2. Alphonse trades his soul to restore Edward's arm.
  3. Edward gives up alchemy to bring Alphonse's body back, and Hohenheim dies at his wife's grave.
