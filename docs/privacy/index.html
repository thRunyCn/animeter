Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-18.

What we store

If you sign in, we store your email address and the ratings, list entries and drop-point reports you make, so you can see and change them. Sign-in and storage are handled by Supabase. Your ratings and reports are counted, without your email, in the public totals on each series page.

On your device

Your browser keeps your sign-in session and a few display choices (such as list or cover view) in local storage. We use no tracking cookies and no advertising.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To have your account and data deleted, open an issue on GitHub or write to the address you signed in with and ask; we delete it within 30 days.

Others

Covers are served from this site. Fonts are served from this site too. Links to AniList, MyAnimeList and other sources take you to sites with their own policies.
