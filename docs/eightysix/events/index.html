Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Spearhead Squadron Ep 1–11

  1. Lena gradually learns the real names and lives of the 86 pilots she commands.
  2. Shin can hear the voices of the dead inside the Legion, including his brother's.
  3. Spearhead is sent on a suicidal 'special reconnaissance' mission meant to kill them off.
  4. Shin defeats the Legion unit carrying his brother's mind, and the survivors keep marching east.

The Federacy Ep 12–16

  1. The survivors are adopted by the Federacy's president and introduced to the young girl Frederica.
  2. They choose to return to the battlefield and join the Nordlicht Squadron.
  3. A massive Legion offensive strikes the continent, and Lena rallies the remaining 86 in the Republic.

Morpho / Run Through the Battlefront Ep 17–23

  1. The Morpho railgun shells Federacy bases while the Republic's capital falls.
  2. The mission turns into a trap, and Shin faces the Legion carrying Frederica's knight, Kiriya.
  3. Shin destroys Morpho and survives.
  4. Shin finally meets Lena in person, and she joins the 86 in a new unit.
