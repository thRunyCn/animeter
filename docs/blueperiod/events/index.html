These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Awakening to Painting Ep 1–2

  1. Yatora paints Shibuya at dawn and decides to pursue art.
  2. Yatora sets his sights on Tokyo University of the Arts, Japan's hardest art school to enter.

Art Prep School Ep 3–6

  1. Yatora meets the gifted Yotasuke Takahashi and the talented Maki Kuwana.
  2. Yatora's lack of experience leads to a mental breakdown over his work.

The First Exam Ep 7–8

  1. Yatora sits the first exam and passes to the second round.

Ryuji's Escape Ep 9–10

  1. Yatora joins Ryuji on a trip to the sea.
  2. The two paint nude portraits of each other, and Yatora finds his own blue.

The Second Exam Ep 11–12

  1. Yatora battles self-doubt through the second exam's painting task.
  2. Yatora is accepted into Tokyo University of the Arts.
