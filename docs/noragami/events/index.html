Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

The Stray God Ep 1–5

  1. Hiyori becomes a half-phantom whose spirit leaves her body at random.
  2. Yato takes in the dead boy's spirit Yukine as his regalia weapon.
  3. Hiyori learns that Yato was once a god of calamity.

Yukine's Blight Ep 6–9

  1. The war god Bishamon attacks Yato over a long-held grudge.
  2. Yukine's stealing and bitterness blight Yato until he collapses.
  3. Yukine is put through a purifying ablution and finally apologizes to Yato.

Rabo Ep 10–12

  1. Nora steals Hiyori's memories of Yato and Yukine.
  2. The revived god-slayer Rabo tests Yato's strength.
  3. Yato defeats Rabo, and Hiyori recovers her memories and chooses to stay with him.

Bishamon Ep 13–18

  1. A schemer among Bishamon's regalia, Kugaha, kills Suzuha and turns her family against her.
  2. Kazuma's tie to Yato and Bishamon's past is revealed.
  3. Yukine evolves into a Blessed Vessel to protect Yato.
  4. Yato and Bishamon finally make peace.

Ebisu and the Underworld Ep 19–25

  1. Hiyori builds Yato a tiny shrine of his own.
  2. Yato's father reappears and Yato disappears.
  3. Yato travels into the Underworld to rescue Ebisu from Izanami.
  4. Ebisu is reborn as a child, and Yato renounces his days as a god of calamity.
