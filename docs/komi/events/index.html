Komi's First Friends Ep 1–4

  1. Tadano and Komi talk through a blackboard and become her first friend.
  2. Najimi Osana, who claims to be everyone's childhood friend, joins in.
  3. Komi is crowned the class 'goddess', and Yamai and Agari join her circle.

Summer Break Ep 5–9

  1. Komi visits Tadano's home and they share an umbrella.
  2. A pool trip ends with Tadano holding Komi's hand.
  3. Komi's grandmother notices her crush during Obon.

Sports Day and Culture Festival Ep 10–12

  1. Komi runs the relay at sports day and feels jealous of Onemine.
  2. The class runs a maid café at the culture festival.
  3. The season ends with a dance and a karaoke after-party.

Winter Holidays Ep 13–18

  1. The stern Katai joins the group after being mistaken for a delinquent.
  2. Komi gets a joint Christmas and birthday celebration.
  3. Komi visits Tadano when he catches a fever.

Kyoto Field Trip Ep 19–21

  1. Komi confides her worries about the trip to Najimi in a letter.
  2. On day two, Komi realizes she has a crush on Tadano and falls asleep on his shoulder.

Valentine's and White Day Ep 22–24

  1. Komi gives Tadano chocolate after encouragement.
  2. Komi and Tadano have their first fight.
  3. Tadano gives Komi hand cream for White Day as their first year ends.
