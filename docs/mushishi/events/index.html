Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Mushi-Shi (Series 1) Ep 1–26

  1. The Green Seat (ep 1): Ginko meets Shinra, a boy whose drawings come to life, and the mushi that keeps him company.
  2. One-Eyed Fish (ep 12): Ginko's origin is revealed, including how he lost an eye and his memories to a mushi of darkness.
  3. A Sea of Writings (ep 20): Ginko visits Tanyuu, whose family seals mushi away in writing across generations.
  4. The Sound of Footsteps on the Grass (ep 26): a look back at Ginko's youth traveling the mountains.

The Shadow That Devours the Sun Film

  1. Ginko works with a girl who cannot go out in sunlight as an eclipse approaches.
  2. Ginko stops the shadow mushi from returning during the eclipse.

Next Passage (Part 1) Ep 27–36

  1. Banquet at the Forest's Edge (ep 27): a sake brewer's son stumbles into a strange mushi ceremony and meets Ginko.
  2. The Warbling Sea Shell (ep 28): a girl isolated on a sea crag hears a shell's warning of disaster.
  3. Depths of Winter (ep 36): Ginko is trapped on a mountain locked in an endless winter.

Path of Thorns Film

  1. Ginko investigates a dangerous mushi and meets Kumado, a girl tied to the family's mushi-sealing work.
  2. Ginko confronts a soul-devouring mushi living inside her.

Next Passage (Part 2) Ep 37–46

  1. Lingering Crimson (ep 39): a girl vanishes at dusk and a memory-less girl appears in her place.
  2. Lightning's End (ep 44): a boy who draws lightning strains his mother's feelings for him.
  3. Tree of Eternity (ep 46): a man carries memories of lives before his own birth.

Bell Droplets Film

  1. The girl is chosen as the mountain's lord, a role normally held by a mushi or animal.
  2. Ginko risks himself to offer her a chance to become human again, and the series closes with her fate settled.
