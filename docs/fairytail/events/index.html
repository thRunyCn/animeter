Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Macao Arc Ep 1–2

  1. Natsu rescues Lucy from a fake 'Salamander' who was trafficking girls, then brings her to Fairy Tail.
  2. Lucy joins the guild and receives her guild mark.
  3. Natsu climbs Mt. Hakobe alone and brings back Macao, Romeo's missing father.

Daybreak Arc Ep 3–4

  1. Lucy's maid disguise fails and the team storms Duke Everlue's mansion instead.
  2. The book Daybreak turns out to hide a message from its author to his son, so the team refuses the reward.

Lullaby Arc Ep 5–10

  1. Erza, Natsu, Gray and Lucy form what fans call Team Natsu.
  2. Erigor of Eisenwald plans to play the death flute Lullaby at a meeting of guild masters.
  3. Natsu defeats Erigor, and Lullaby reveals itself as a demon of Zeref that the guild masters help destroy.
  4. Erza is briefly arrested by the Magic Council as a formality for the property damage.

Galuna Island Arc Ep 11–20

  1. Gray meets his fellow apprentice Lyon, who wants to revive the demon Deliora frozen by their teacher Ur.
  2. The island's 'cursed' villagers turn out to be demons whose memories were warped by the Moon Drip ritual.
  3. Gray defeats Lyon and Deliora crumbles, already killed long ago by Ur's Iced Shell.
  4. Erza arrives to drag the trio home but helps them finish the job first.

Phantom Lord Arc Ep 21–29

  1. Gajeel beats Levy's team and pins them to a tree, provoking Fairy Tail into war.
  2. Phantom Lord was hired by Lucy's wealthy father to bring her home.
  3. Natsu defeats Gajeel, and Makarov uses Fairy Law to defeat Master Jose.
  4. Lucy confronts her father and cuts ties with him.

Loke Arc Ep 30–32

  1. Loke is revealed to be the Celestial Spirit Leo, banished to the human world after the death of his former owner Karen.
  2. Lucy defies the Celestial Spirit King to save him, and Loke becomes her spirit.

Tower of Heaven Arc Ep 33–40

  1. Erza's past as a child slave building the Tower of Heaven is revealed.
  2. Jellal, possessed by his obsession with Zeref, plans to use the tower as an R-System to revive him.
  3. The Magic Council fires Etherion at the tower; Natsu eats the Etherion-infused lacrima and defeats Jellal.
  4. Simon dies shielding Erza, and Erza is saved by Jellal as the tower collapses.

Battle of Fairy Tail Arc Ep 41–48

  1. Laxus turns the girls to stone and makes the members fight each other to win them back.
  2. The Thunder God Tribe is defeated one by one.
  3. Natsu and Gajeel together defeat Laxus, and Mystogan's identity is briefly glimpsed.
  4. Makarov expels Laxus, who salutes the guild during the Fantasia parade.

Oración Seis Arc Ep 49–68

  1. Wendy Marvell and Carla of Cait Shelter are introduced as allies.
  2. Jellal is revived but has lost his memories and turns against Oración Seis.
  3. Natsu defeats Zero to stop the superweapon Nirvana.
  4. Cait Shelter is revealed to be an illusion, and Wendy and Carla join Fairy Tail.

Daphne Arc Ep 69–75

  1. Daphne lures Natsu with the promise of information about Igneel so she can drain his dragon power.
  2. Fairy Tail fights her Dragonoid weapon, and Natsu breaks free to destroy it.

Edolas Arc Ep 76–95

  1. Happy and Carla learn that Exceeds were sent to Earth Land on a mission to hunt Dragon Slayers.
  2. The team meets alternate versions of their guildmates in Edolas.
  3. Mystogan is revealed as Edolas's Prince Jellal and helps restore balance.
  4. Lisanna, thought dead, is found alive in Edolas and returns to Earth Land.

Tenrou Island Arc Ep 96–122

  1. Zeref appears on the island, and Grimoire Heart's master Hades seeks to awaken him.
  2. Gildarts reveals he is Cana's father.
  3. Makarov and the guild defeat Hades, with Laxus returning to help.
  4. The black dragon Acnologia attacks, and the members on the island vanish, frozen in time for seven years by Fairy Sphere.

X791 Arc Ep 123–124

  1. Romeo and the remaining members have kept the struggling guild alive.
  2. Lucy learns her father has died during her absence.

Key of the Starry Sky Arc Ep 125–150

  1. The Oración Seis remnants (Reborn Oración Seis) and the Legion fight over the clock's pieces.
  2. Lucy is captured and used as part of the clock's activation.
  3. Fairy Tail stops the clock and saves Lucy.

Grand Magic Games Arc Ep 151–203

  1. Fairy Tail enters two teams and battles Sabertooth, led by Dragon Slayers Sting and Rogue.
  2. Natsu and Gajeel defeat Sting and Rogue, and Fairy Tail wins the Games.
  3. Future Rogue opens the Eclipse Gate, releasing seven dragons on Crocus.
  4. Future Lucy dies protecting the present, and the Dragon Slayers fight the dragons until the gate is closed.

Eclipse Celestial Spirits Arc Ep 204–226

  1. Lucy's spirits break their contracts and attack Fairy Tail as 'Eclipse Spirits' with reversed personalities.
  2. Lucy and the guild work to restore the spirits and return them to the Celestial Spirit World.

Sun Village Arc Ep 227–233

  1. The village's eternal flame turns out to be the dragon Atlas Flame.
  2. Natsu frees the dragon from the ice, and it tells him a cryptic message about Igneel.

Tartaros Arc Ep 234–265

  1. Tartaros kills most of the Magic Council and targets its former members.
  2. Lucy sacrifices Aquarius' key to summon the Celestial Spirit King.
  3. Igneel emerges from inside Natsu, fights Acnologia, and is killed by it.
  4. Makarov disbands Fairy Tail after the battle.

Fairy Tail Zero Arc Ep 266–275

  1. Mavis meets Yuri, Precht and Warrod on Tenrou Island.
  2. Mavis meets Zeref and learns from him.
  3. The four found Fairy Tail together.

Avatar Arc Ep 276–284

  1. Natsu and Lucy find their friends living new lives across Fiore.
  2. Gray, Juvia and Erza work to stop the dark cult Avatar from carrying out a mass sacrifice.
  3. Fairy Tail is officially reformed and Makarov's whereabouts in Alvarez come to light.

Alvarez Empire Arc Ep 285–328

  1. Zeref is revealed as Emperor Spriggan and Natsu as his younger brother E.N.D.
  2. Makarov sacrifices himself using Fairy Law against the Alvarez army.
  3. Mavis and Zeref's curse is lifted when Natsu and Mavis confront Zeref together.
  4. The Dragon Slayers defeat Acnologia in a final battle inside time, and the guild celebrates.
