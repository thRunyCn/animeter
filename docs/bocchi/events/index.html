Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

Joining Kessoku Band Ep 1–4

  1. Nijika recruits Hitori, nicknamed Bocchi, as Kessoku Band's guitarist.
  2. Bocchi starts working at the live house Starry despite her terror of customers.
  3. Bocchi brings in Ikuyo Kita, the guitarist who previously ran away from the band.
  4. Bocchi writes the band's lyrics with Ryō's advice to be herself.

The First Live Ep 5–8

  1. The band passes Seika's audition, and Bocchi has to sell her share of tickets.
  2. Drunken bassist Kikuri Hiroi helps Bocchi through an impromptu street performance.
  3. A typhoon thins the crowd, but Bocchi's spontaneous solo saves the first live.
  4. Nijika reveals she knows Bocchi is the online guitarist 'guitarhero'.

Summer and the School Festival Ep 9–12

  1. The band drags Bocchi on a trip to Enoshima.
  2. Bocchi submits the band's entry for the cultural festival.
  3. A string breaks mid-song, and Bocchi improvises with a bottleneck while Kita covers for her.
  4. Bocchi ends the show with a stage dive and later buys a new guitar.
