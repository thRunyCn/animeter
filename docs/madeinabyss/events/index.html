Key events

These are spoilers. They stay blurred until you reveal them, one at a time or all at once.

The City of the Great Pit Ep 1–3

  1. Riko finds Reg, a robot boy, while scavenging in the Abyss.
  2. A message from her mother urges Riko to come to the bottom.
  3. Riko and Reg sneak away to begin their descent.

Edge of the Abyss and Seeker Camp Ep 4–8

  1. The pair meet Ozen the Immovable at Seeker Camp.
  2. Ozen reveals Riko was born in the Abyss and her mother's grim situation.
  3. Ozen tests and trains the children before letting them go deeper.

The Great Fault and Nanachi Ep 9–13

  1. Riko is badly poisoned and suffers the curse of the Abyss.
  2. Nanachi, a 'Hollow', saves Riko.
  3. Nanachi's past with the White Whistle Bondrewd and her friend Mitty is revealed.
  4. Reg ends Mitty's suffering, and Nanachi joins the journey.

Dawn of the Deep Soul Film

  1. Bondrewd welcomes the trio while hiding his cruel experiments.
  2. His daughter Prushka befriends Riko.
  3. Reg and Nanachi battle Bondrewd, and Prushka becomes a White Whistle.

Iruburu Village Ep 14–19

  1. A parallel story follows the Ganja expedition that came to the sixth layer long ago.
  2. The trio enter the Hollow village of Iruburu.
  3. Reg meets Faputa, who has a mysterious bond with him.

Vueko's Tale Ep 20–21

  1. Vueko tells of the cursed land and the girl Irumyuui.
  2. The village is revealed to be built from Irumyuui's body and the Ganja's wish.

Faputa's Return Ep 22–25

  1. Faputa attacks the village, and Reg is caught between her and the villagers.
  2. Riko and Nanachi try to protect their friends in the village.
  3. The village falls, and the trio continue their descent with Faputa's promise to find Reg again.
