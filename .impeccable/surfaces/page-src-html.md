Scope: the whole site (home, rankings, series pages, hubs, my list). Visitor mode: Persuade on home, Read on series pages and hubs.
Audience and job: a watcher who searched one show and wants the stop, skip or continue answer fast, often on a phone.
Chosen direction: Colour chapters (user picked it on a card; tasteskill seed 683942572). Memorable moment: each series page is drenched in that anime's own cover colour.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Every anime already has a colour, the `tone` taken from its cover. The site is a run of full-bleed colour chapters and each series page is drenched in its own show's colour. Refuses the category default: a neutral page with a grid of rounded cover cards.
OWN-WORLD: Flat fields butted edge to edge, no gaps, no lines, no shadows: slate #a6b3bc, sage #b7c8be, blush #d6c4b8, olive #2c3620, ground #e7e8e4, ink #1c1c1c, a brick #b8402a only for "stop / filler / start here". One face, Schibsted Grotesk 800 for display at 8 to 12vw, tight tracking, 400/600 text. Square corners, solid olive buttons.
STORY: The visitor sees that Animeter is about where people stop watching, picks a show, lands on a page in that show's colour, and reads where to stop, skip or pick up the manga.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Two fields meeting at half height: ground above, the No. 1 show's colour below. "Where people / stop watching" at about 9.5vw is centred on the seam and changes ink where it crosses it. Below the seam: one sentence and a row of the top six covers. Tapping a cover repaints the lower field in that show's colour and rewrites the sentence with its manga hand-off. Wordmark top left; Rankings, A to Z, Genres, and the My list button top right.
FORM: Colour chapters, position 3 on my ordered list (user-pinned over the roll's assignment of 5). seed:dc9319a3c34d0866
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
