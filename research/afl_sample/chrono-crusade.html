Thus Spoke Rohan Kishibe Filler List

Updated on July 31, 2024

Thus Spoke Rohan Kishibe was an anime series that ran from 2017 to 2019. In total 4 episodes of Thus Spoke Rohan Kishibe were aired. Thus Spoke Rohan Kishibe has no reported filler.

This non-consecutive series follows the JoJo's Bizarre Adventure character Rohan Kishibe: an eccentric manga artist from the fourth major story arc of the series. He travels around the world to draw inspiration from people's lives with the aid of his Stand ability, Heaven's Door. The Stand's main power is to read and write in a person, allowing him to learn everything about them and alter their memories and behaviors.
