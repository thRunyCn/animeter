Yowamushi Pedal Filler List

Updated on October 26, 2022

Yowamushi Pedal was an anime series that ran from 2013 to 2014. In total 14 episodes of Yowamushi Pedal were aired. Yowamushi Pedal has no reported filler.

Onoda Sakamichi is a high school first year that just wanted to re-establish the anime club, but ended up joining the one thing he never expected, a sports team! Finding his talent and passion for bicycles, Onoda joins the Sohoku High cycling club. He aims to climb high and get his team first across the finish line.
